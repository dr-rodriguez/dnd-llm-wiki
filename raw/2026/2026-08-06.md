
# Session 2026-08-06

We all feel a wave, something uncomfortable, like a shudder, but it doesn't harm us.
Amber and Maxim have an adverse reaction to it, though.
Aolis is panicking at seeing all this.

Amber opens the door, steps outside, and falls on her face. Gulping for air.
Aolis rushes to get us to help.

Zinjaro is out of spell slots, but there are some Goodberries (bananas) that he is trying to feed Amber and Maxim.
Amber is trying to crawl inside, Ogra helps her. She touches her head, but now both of them are unconscious.

In Maxim’s mind: when the wave hit, it was pure darkness. 
He stands up, but is incorporeal with no body. 
He casts Light and it attaches to a pedestal he didn’t see before. It is diminished, only reaching 5 feet of bright light and an additional 5 of dim. It feels surreal.
He can cast some spells, but only close to the pedestal. Too far and everything disappears.
An indeterminate time passes.
A clear glass sphere appears on the pedestal. He wills himself to touch it.
It rises from the pedestal, glows milky white. His vision shifts. 
An older human, late 50s, medium length scraggly beard is leaning on the pedestal.
“You know, this wasn’t the plan”
He says he’s been called a god before.
He asks if Maxim has ever thought of going back in time. Physically, with another person maybe. 
Maxim says he is not that powerful.
He offers to send Maxim and one other back in time, in exchange for a favor.
He wants us to go deep into the facility and recover the AI core of my wife (it’s Odin AI!). He also wants us to bring him the leader of that facility. Their state of life is not important.
Maxim really doesn’t have a past worth going to- all he remembers is the training facility and the cryopod.
The sphere turns to amber gold. Maxim feels Amber’s presence.
Odin leaves and the emptiness returns.

In real-time, a bunny character (Ras’ old character- Juneberry) runs in and attaches a tiara-like object on Maxim’s head. She attaches it to a machine and hits a button. Maxim regains consciousness, but he feels like he was torn apart. He is in a lot of pain, but alive. He is holding his head on the floor.
Juneberry goes to help Amber.

Sofia AI arrives.
Amber is on her third shock, but hasn’t woken up.
Sofia explains that individuals like Maxim and Amber were made to receive telepathic instructions. 
The “Second Space”- the mental space they can go to for this. The First Space is the physical world.
Odin has figured out a way to weaponize Second Space and is attempting to rip out their minds.
Sofia confirms Odin is her ex-husband.
Cognitive Retriever (the tiara thing), tries to do the same thing but in reverse to help bring them back.

In a final shock, Amber regains consciousness and screams. Coughs up some blood and falls unconscious again.
Zinjaro tries Channel Divinity and is able to help Maxim a little bit. June takes Amber elsewhere.

Sofia asks Maxim if Odin offered to do something with time.
Maxim confirms it.
Sofia thinks Odin wants to use a Demiplane to either whip his allies back in time or the creators forward in time.
Sofia warns that he shouldn’t believe Odin.
She says the “bring a friend” is a sacrifice required to help build the demiplane.
Maxim argues he can’t believe her either.
Sofia says it’s up to him to decide, but she points out she isn’t attacking him.

Maxim insight checks her, but he just senses that she seems tired.
We’re ushered out to take our rest.
We finally get our long rest.

The next day we walk by Amber- she is hooked up to medical equipment.
Maxim and Aolis stay in their rooms.
They chat about what happened. Maxim says he is happy here, with Aolis. He has nothing in the past to go to. He doesn’t care about the power struggles.
Neither of them care about having power. Aolis ran away; Maxim impersonated the middle child, not the heir. He is annoyed he has been sucked into these games.

Maxim’s mother (Maxwella) shows up and rushes into the room.
She says Sofia sent a message. It had runes of teleportation so she was able to get here quickly.
She doesn’t appear to know anything of Maxim’s real nature- she’s just worried about her child.

Queen Tesselia is observing Amber through the window.
She says we should wait, scans of the mind show that some of it is missing.
She claps her hand and a robot trolley brings us breakfast food.
We go to Maxim and Aolis’s room for breakfast.
Maxim asks about Amber, they wonder if she has accepted a deal, or maybe she didn’t.

Maxim and his mother talk about home. A new faction- the Severance.
They want to break the chains and control the floating mountain where the college is.
