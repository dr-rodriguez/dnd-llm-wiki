
# Session 2026-10-08

Clarification: The Mountain is a large primordial creature. 
Lumber Camp has been having some problems, it’s on the way. 

Supplies in the city are low, no longer a Tier 1 capital city. We can still get basic supplies, but potions are limited and no magical items.
We chat about getting a cart and having Brambleberry pull it. Decided against it- we’ll just walk.

It’s mid spring. We head towards Wheat Burrow on the way to Lumber Camp.

While crossing a forest pass, we encounter some gnolls that ambush us.
It’s not a challenge- we take them out without breaking a sweat.
The gnolls seem well fed, no disease, unlikely to have been aggressive. Their eyes which had been red dulled to amber. We don’t know if this is normal.

We go back to travelling. Bramble notices a pale white pigmy goat following us. It’s wearing a floppy white hat. Bramble attempts to speak with it, it bleats back; Aolis goes to pet it. It moves away- doesn’t want to be pet. Villhelm tries to pet, but scares him instead. It still follows us.

We make camp, Zinjaro uses Goodberries to provide magical bananas for any who want one, including the goat. Maxim prepares a tent for us. Bramble and Aolis offer Goodberries, Bramble throws her into the campfire, though. Aolis manages to pet it. The hat looks like a bit of a wizard hat.

Zinjaro casts True Seeing. It’s just a goat…. But not. Zinjaro sees the true form of it.
It’s an Alp, a fey creature. It’s not innately dangerous, but inflicts nightmares on travelers. Sometimes it travels with other creatures that take advantage of the exhausted adventurers to eat them.

Zinjaro says we should kill it.
Aolis initially doesn’t want to kill it, but Zinjaro explains it’s an Alp. Aolis casts Chill Touch, killing it.
Aolis wanders away to bury it.

We go to sleep, setting watches.

Ogra and Aolis take the first watch. Ogra sees a fully saddled horse enter the camp.
It has a saddlebag with 30 gold, rations, and assorted supplies, he takes it. The bag is bloody. A second saddlebag is slashed open, seemingly from a poorly maintained weapon. The horse came from the northeast.

Aolis and Bramble take the second watch. There is a rustling near the fire- a small rabbit. It has an injured leg. It eagerly eats up a bit of rations and some of Aolis’s Sentinels Flask.
It starts chewing a hole into Bramble’s pants (which are technically Maxim’s old pants). The rabbit then bites and starts draining blood. She passes out with 2 levels of exhaustion. 
Aolis notices, but too late. He Chill Touches hurting it, but it starts lunging around. Maxim wakes up noticing a second spell attack from Aolis taking out the rabbit. 
Aolis tries to end Bramble’s wound. There is something preventing the wound from closing, but Aolis wipes it and helps bind it. She finishes a long rest, recovering one level of exhaustion.

Above table: it was a treacle, though not recognized by the players.
