
# Session 2026-11-19

Aolis investigates the remains of the drow mage.
He finds a letter and a delicate silver box. The box is warped from heat, so it’s not easily openable. Bramble can open it, but it takes her a few hours. Aolis tells her to unlock but not open it yet.

The letter is in code, Maxim can’t read it but Aolis can. Outrider parties use this as coded documents. Outrider parties: “gathering” (taking people- slaver) or “agents of the court” (specific tasks). It will take time to break the cypher.

Zinjaro casts Temple of the Gods and an Aztec-style temple to Solar emerges from the night. There are bananas and wine and glowing sunlight despite it being night. It’s a protected area we can long rest in.

![[SolAr-Temple-Spell.jpg]]
> Temple of the Gods, dedicated to Sol'Ar

Inside the silver box is a small sphere, dark. Surrounding it are two rings, the inner one is gold and the other brass. There are runes in the ring, but Aolis can’t tell what it’s for. It looks like a gyroscope.

The letter is from an agent of the court. Talks about ongoing operations in the Northern Andore region. The appearance of the dragon and “infestation” of the Mountain has provided cover for their operation. “We know she is in the region, we have not laid eyes on her” (possibly about Aolis). Some names at the bottom- 2 in another language, a 3rd- Essa. Aolis doesn’t recognize this. This is written in different scripts, possibly written by different people- 3 different writers, 3 different languages.

We head back towards the Lumber Camp to report back on the cult situation. Along the way, Maxim spots a goose in our way. It honks at us. 
Villhelm attempts to grab it, but fails. It tries to bite him, but Villhelm steps away fast enough.
Aolis tries to pet but accidentally touches the beak and it bites him.
We ignore it and keep going.

We arrive at the camp and talk to the hadozee about what we encountered.
We have lunch at the tavern. 
The goose has followed us! 
Aolis feeds it some vegetables while Villhelm grabs it. We talk about cooking it, it flies out and walks away.

We head back north towards the mountains.
No Temple of the Gods tonight.
As the sun starts to set, Maxim notices 5 wolves following us. They appear to fan out around the party. (It’s not a tarrasque; the DM gave us a funny choice of choosing between a 1 or a 100 on a d100 roll after Maxim rolled 1). Maxim kills the wolves with magic, we don’t run it since they have such low HP.

The next day, we arrive at an encampment by the mountains. Looks like a mining camp, but has been raided. There is a mine shaft at the other side of the town, it goes deeper into the mountain.

Aolis gets separated and spots the goose… again. He follows it into a room that gets locked.
Two slimes appear and the goose disappears through the floor boards, honking as it goes away. It’s a trap?!
