
# Session 2026-11-05

We arrive at the cult encampment.

There is a campfire with several people around it. Mismatched clothing. 
It’s very dark, only a few of us have dark vision.

Maxim, then a few others, gets closer to find out more.
There are some big yurts around. 
Bramble hears several conversations- gnolls, hobgoblins, goblins. All small talk.
“I wonder who will get picked next for ascension” “Lizardmen near river”

Zinjaro gets closer and casts Thaumaturgy to try and distract them with some ominous whispers, but it doesn't work too well. A hobgoblin spots him, but just him.
One of the gnolls calls for backup and more enemies show up.
It’s combat time.

Faerie Fire helps illuminate a few enemies. Zinjaro gets hit by Heat Metal, but Villhelm breaks their concentration with some punches. Maxim’s Fireball takes out a large group of gnolls and sets a yurt on fire. Aolis uses Inflict Wounds on another gnoll while Bramble jumps in with some psychic dagger action.

One of the hobgoblins says something in goblin but none of us understand.
An ashari firetamer enemy starts chanting something and transforms… into a fire elemental!
Ogra kills two hobgoblins with their frost iron sword.

![[fire elemental.png|469]]
> A fire elemental

We start to hear ominous chanting from a distance. Bramble takes out the elemental, but new enemies show up- some duergar and a goliath called Rivermaw Stormborn.

Maxim shoots a Fireball towards the chanting, illuminating the area a bit. 
We all notice a drow mage there.
Aolis rushes towards them- he recognizes them! It’s the retainer to his sister (Aolis is the oldest, this would be the next oldest).
He summons his Hound of Ill Omen.

At a nod from the drow, Rivermaw rushes in and grapples Aolis.
Zinjaro cats Shield of Faith on Aolis, it’s like warm sunlight bathes down on him. In addition, he uses The Solar Gift to cast Daylight to illuminate the area.

Villhelm and Bramble rush in to attack Rivermaw. Maxim casts Hold Person. The Hound brings him prone. Ogra’s vines bring Aolis to safety. Villhelm finishes off Rivermaw.

Several of us see the completion of a ritual where several goblins are killed. 
Several creatures are summoned: an aeorian nullifier and two adult kruthik.
Ogra, Bramble, Villhelm all get the Horrified condition (Zinjaro resists).
(Next session we’ll learn what Horrified means in-game)
