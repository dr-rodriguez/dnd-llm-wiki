
# Session 2026-10-22

Maxim takes the horse so we now have a draft horse. It’s a clydesdale.
Bramble still has 2 levels of exhaustion after resting (she had started with 3).
We continue our journey towards Lumber Camp.

We find an overturned, abandoned cart. One of the wheels is missing and heavily damaged.
There is a nearby intact barrel. It appears to be filled with liquid.
Ogra opens it- it is full of a deep, crimson wine.
We argue about what to do about it. Several consider selling it, but Zinjaro wants to drink it.
We start dividing it among our water skins, but Ogra carries the wine barrel so we can bring it with us.
Maxim offers Aolis to share the horse; Bramble is tired but doesn’t get a ride.

We are at the edge of a temperate, coniferous forest.
We arrive at Lumber Camp around noon.
Several of us go inside one of the buildings with a tankard and fork symbol- some kind of common house. 
There is another hadozee in the house. They are normal sized (Zinjaro is small). They are looking over a map. 
They mention they are having goblinoid issues. They don’t have enough people to take care of them. 
They’ve also noticed reptilian creatures, also a male drow. 
We agree to help.

Zinjaro mentions the barrel and the need to protect it. Apparently, the third cart was missing- now they know where it went. We lose the barrel; Zinjaro is sad.
They don’t recognize the horse that we have.

They offer Bramble some coffee to help with her exhaustion. It’s very bitter.
Maxim also takes some. He reels- this is not what he is used to.

Zinjaro buys some mushroom powder (25gp) for his spells. He casts Greater Restoration to clear Bramble’s remaining exhaustion.

We head into the woods.
Maxim hears some people on the move. We chase after them.
There are 21 orcs carrying a pair of halflings.

Ogra approaches, but they keep running. Maxim is inclined to hide, but Zinjaro wants to save the halflings. Zinjaro casts a Wall of Fire to block their movement- we are in combat.
We start taking them down. They are pretty weak.

One of the halflings breaks free, his feet are shackled but makes his way to Ogra.
We keep casting fire spells so to take them out in groups, lots of trees are now on fire too.
We save both the halflings.

We argue about the fire- we don’t know how to put it out.
The two halflings ask Zinjaro and Aolis to stand in particular places and start chanting- circle magic.
After about 5 minutes, a rainstorm appears and starts putting out the fires.

They don't know why they were captured, they were traveling through the woods with friends and the orcs ambushed them. They were saying something about a ring. 
They point us to where they’ve seen goblins.
