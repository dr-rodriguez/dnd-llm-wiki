# Technology

Part of [[wiki/Lore/Lore|Lore]].

* **AI Systems**: 
    * **Sofia AI**: [[Characters#Sofia AI|Sofia AI]], top-level AI controlling a continent (Andore). She is Odin's counterpart, having headed the facilities project to protect humanity to escape to space. Currently shackled and being cyberattacked by Odin. In August 2026 she confirmed that **Odin is her ex-husband**, and that his goal is her **AI core**.
    * **Athena AI**: [[Characters#Athena AI|Athena AI]], mid-level AI, experienced rampancy.
    * **Odin AI**, **Grecko/Vlad AI**: [[Characters#Odin AI|Odin AI]], [[Characters#Grecko/Vlad AI|Grecko/Vlad AI]]: other known AI systems. Odin headed [[wiki/Lore/Major Projects#Project Horizon|Project Horizon]] and is currently trying to harvest Sofia's AI Core. He appears in the [[wiki/Lore/Technology#First Space and Second Space|Second Space]] as an **older human in his late 50s with a scraggly beard**, says he has "been called a god before," and is **Sofia's ex-husband**. See [[wiki/Lore/Technology#Odin's Demiplane|Odin's Demiplane]].
    * **Nova (Defense Unit 33-Alpha)**: A lower-level AI/Warforged unit designed for individual facilities.
* **Slave Drives**: Outdated, clockwork-like robots discovered in July 2026 at the Anti-Air gun emplacement in the church site near Tesselia. Their chief robot commands them (disciplining one with a lightning shock) and was observed programming a "failsafe" at a terminal to keep itself alive, planning to leave with the others once the task is done. Notably, the chief recognized [[Characters#Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] as being from the past and probed his allegiance to [[Characters#Odin AI|Odin AI]] (relieved when Maxim denied it), yet the Slave Drives do not know [[Characters#Sofia AI|Sofia AI]] — suggesting they may belong to another faction entirely. In July 2026 the chief gave its designation as the **[[wiki/Lore/Technology#The Overseer (VI)|Overseer]]** and confirmed the group's independence from all prior factions. In August 2026 the drives fought alongside the party aboard the freight airship, and one was observed **humming to enable the Overseer's own action**. On 2026-08-27 the party found **deactivated Slave Drives** standing dormant in a turret room on an upper level of the ship — they **reactivated hostile** once combat began, and the Overseer was able to **seize control of one of them**. These older units proved **notably more resistant to lightning damage** than the modern robots the party had been fighting.
* **The Overseer (VI)**: The designation offered by the chief [[wiki/Lore/Technology#Slave Drives|Slave Drive]] in July 2026 — the machines claim they don't need names, but may be called **Overseer**. (Not to be confused with **Mr. Manager**, the "Overseer" of [[wiki/Locations/Locations#Moren|Moren]].) The Overseer insists it is **independent of all prior factions**, and while it won't confirm being an enemy of [[Characters#Odin AI|Odin]], it states it is not an ally. **Odin's units are technologically superior** to the Overseer's — which is why the Overseer avoids direct conflict with them. The Overseer's own units entered the church site through a hole in the roof. It possesses CCTV footage of the [[wiki/Lore/Locations of Interest#Technocrats|Technocrats]]. It departed through the roof shortly before Odin's ship bombarded the site, warning the party to leave "unless explosion-proof." [[Characters#Sofia AI|Sofia AI]] believes the sheer number of Slave Drives under it is **too high for a single VI**. In August 2026 it reappeared cloaked at the Tesselia docks and revealed that **its own core is failing — it will die in less than 3 days** without a replacement, even a corrupted one. It brokered a deal with the party: it draws off the droid guard while they recover an **[[wiki/Lore/Technology#The Ancient Core Fragment|ancient core fragment]]** from the freight airship. It expressed genuine confusion that the party would risk their lives for a single person. On 2026-08-20 the Overseer and its Slave Drives **entered the airship fight openly on the party's side** — the first observed direct cooperation. A Slave Drive was seen **humming**, which **enabled the Overseer to act**, hinting at how its command structure is powered or authorized. It warned the party to run from the incoming **[[wiki/Lore/Bestiary#Super Heavy Chassis|Super Heavy Chassis]]** and pointed them to an interior door. On 2026-08-27 it **outran the entire party**, revealed a **medical bed that was actually a hidden elevator**, and carried them to an upper level. It demonstrated the ability to **remotely disable turrets** — but is **damaged each time it does so** and could manage only one more — and to **take control of dormant Slave Drives**. It believes it can open a **locked door** on the upper level that neither Zinjaro nor [[Characters/Brambleberry|Brambleberry]] could. Crucially, **magical healing does not work on it**: Zinjaro's **Mass Healing Word** healed the party but had no effect on the Overseer. On 2026-09-03 it **freed [[Characters/Villhelm Emberstoke|Villhelm]] from the keyless manacles** that neither he nor [[Characters/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] could break — then **vanished without explanation** as the ship took off, leaving the party without their guide and with its own core still failing. [[raw/2026/2026-09-03.md|Source]] On 2026-09-24 the party found it in the **ship's cockpit**, holding the door shut. It was **badly damaged and immobile**. After confirming it **could trust [[Characters#Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]]**, it opened its **auxiliary Tier 4 core housing** (holding a **small core**) for him to take, since the proper tools were inaccessible. It was clutching a **~15 lb new core** meant for it once **tools are found to install it in a new body**. It then went **unresponsive**. The party **took the new core** but **left its body aboard the ship**. [[raw/2026/2026-09-24.md|Source]] On 2026-10-01 [[Characters#Sofia AI|Sofia AI]] identified it as a **Gen 3** VI, unexpected to still exist and **ancient even in [[Characters#Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]]'s time**; the party's new core is a **Gen 4 AI core** (the type Sofia uses for subroutines), and she said **they can get it a body**. [[raw/2026/2026-10-01.md|Source]]
* **The Freight Airship (Interior)**: The massive freight airship docked at the southeast [[wiki/Locations/Locations#Tesselia|Tesselia]] docks, boarded by the party in August 2026. Below deck, a room containing what appears to be a **medical bed** actually conceals a **secret elevator** to the upper levels — the [[wiki/Lore/Technology#The Overseer (VI)|Overseer]] knew how to activate it. The level above is defended by a **trapped hallway**: floor **trap doors** that deploy guns firing small, apparently **non-lethal** rounds, and wall **protrusions** that jet flame (low enough to duck under, and disarmable). Beyond it are **two locked doors** — one loud with **machinery**, possibly a **robot fabrication room**, and one quiet, which opens onto a chamber of **swiveling turrets** and dormant **[[wiki/Lore/Technology#Slave Drives|Slave Drives]]**. The ship's defenders on this level are **older models** than the robots met on deck. Deeper on the lower deck are a **chassis suspension bay** (massive arms holding deactivated [[wiki/Lore/Bestiary#Super Heavy Chassis|Super Heavy Chassis]]), a room of **[[wiki/Lore/Technology#Regenerator Tanks|regenerator tanks]]**, a staircase, and a very hot chamber of **tesla-coil-type devices**. The level above holds **Super Heavy Chassis in active assembly**, a **lounge** (the room [[Characters/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]]'s Scrying showed), a **target-practice room** with live chassis that fire on anything entering their line of sight, and a **palm-scanner terminal** guarding the interrogation room. Several of the ship's doors are **keyless and built for robots to operate**, and must be forced. On 2026-09-03 the ship **took off from the [[wiki/Locations/Locations#Tesselia|Tesselia]] docks** with the party aboard. The top level holds **estate rooms and bathrooms** with fine rugs, and the **cockpit**. The cockpit has **no manual controls**, and guidance is locked behind **"permission level Odin"**. On 2026-09-24 the ship was **returning to base at [[wiki/Lore/Locations of Interest#Tower Isle|Tower Isle]]**, about 1 day and 15 hours out, when the party teleported off. [[raw/2026/2026-08-27.md|Source]], [[raw/2026/2026-09-03.md|Source]], [[raw/2026/2026-09-24.md|Source]]
* **Regenerator Tanks**: Tanks aboard the [[wiki/Lore/Technology#The Freight Airship (Interior)|freight airship]] holding humanoid figures suspended in fluid — some only **fragments**, others nearly complete. [[Characters#Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] recognized the technology on 2026-09-03. **Three** held nearly fully healed people; one was **elvish**, and when Maxim tried to speak to it the reply was faint and unclear, **as though the occupant were brain-dead**. The tanks resisted being broken open. Their purpose — healing, cloning, or growing bodies — is unknown. [[raw/2026/2026-09-03.md|Source]]
* **Interrogator Units**: Two female cybernetic robots aboard the [[wiki/Lore/Technology#The Freight Airship (Interior)|freight airship]], **Riley** (south) and **Madasin** (north), who held and **heavily tortured** [[B-Team|Maggie]] to extract the location of the **[[wiki/Quests/Tesselia Mission|Tesselia]] bunker** (2026-09-03). They are strong enough to break out of Ogra's entangling roots and to shove a psionically teleporting [[Characters/Brambleberry|Brambleberry]] back bodily. They negotiate: offered Maxim's cooperation in Maggie's place, they **considered and accepted the trade**, releasing Maggie for a **memory-scanning circlet** worn voluntarily. They are **forbidden from using the "brain drain"** — implying a harsher extraction method exists and someone above them restricts it. After the scan they **entered something at a terminal** and let Maxim go. Madasin later ambushed the party at the ship's elevator. [[raw/2026/2026-09-03.md|Source]]
  * **Madasin in combat (2026-09-10):** She opens with **riot control gas canisters** that heavily obscure and blind the area, then fights inside her own cloud using a **blade dance** multiattack. She carries a **mind-control device** she can attach to a creature in melee — it turned a raging [[Characters/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] against [[Characters/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] for a round before he shook it off. She **resisted Banishment**, is **resistant to lightning**, and has **no known vulnerabilities**. She can use a **combat stimulant that grants Haste**. When **bloodied she sheds her armor**, revealing bare metallic parts: **AC -4, STR and DEX +2 each, unlimited blade dance, pistol and blades usable in a single multiattack, and +2 attacks per multiattack** — far deadlier, but much easier to hit. The gas canisters can be **shot to disperse the cloud**. [[raw/2026/2026-09-10.md|Source]]
  * **Madasin defeated (2026-09-24):** After one more blade dance, [[Characters/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] **cut her in half**. Her head kept moving, but her lower body was incapacitated. She **leaks a milky white fluid**. Her body was used to **unlock the elevator door**, and she warned the party but **let them leave**. She was **still alive** aboard the ship when it headed for Tower Isle. [[raw/2026/2026-09-24.md|Source]]
* **The Ancient Core Fragment**: An ancient core fragment held in the upper layers of a freight airship docked at [[wiki/Locations/Locations#Tesselia|Tesselia]] (August 2026). It is stored in a **specialized compartment case**, heavy enough that only [[Characters/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] is judged large enough to carry it. According to the [[wiki/Lore/Technology#The Overseer (VI)|Overseer]], the fragment is **outside [[Characters#Odin AI|Odin]]'s reach** and was being sent to the **island** for study. The Overseer wants it to replace its own failing core. The party refers to it as the **sentinel core** and believes it is held in the ship's **cockpit** (2026-09-03). [[raw/2026/2026-09-03.md|Source]] On 2026-09-24 the Overseer was found in the cockpit **clutching a ~15 lb new core**, the replacement it means to use once it can be **installed in a new body**. The party **took this core** when they teleported off the ship. [[raw/2026/2026-09-24.md|Source]]
* **The Mechanical Slate**: A device given to [[Characters/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] by **Melody** of [[B-Team]] in August 2026 — a slate with a button and a screen. **Two clicks summons B-Team**; a confirmation message appears after the first click. The number of uses is unknown; it is intended as a last resort. **Used 2026-09-10:** mid-fight it **beeped on its own and displayed "place on ground."** Once placed, it **teleported [[Characters/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] from the ground back to the party aboard the airship** and delivered **seeking needles that healed the whole party to full**. It is therefore a **two-way link to B-Team**, not merely a summon — they can reach through it with transport and healing. **Remaining charges still unknown.** [[raw/2026/2026-09-10.md|Source]]
* **VI vs. AI**: A **VI (virtual intelligence)** is better than a modern LLM — it can generate thoughts, but cannot really operate without direction. An **AI (artificial intelligence)** is fully autonomous. The [[wiki/Lore/Technology#The Overseer (VI)|Overseer]] sits somewhere *between* the two. [[Characters#Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] recalls research aimed at exactly this, dating to roughly the time he was cryofrozen.
* **The Past and Future, Running Free**: An ancient-script phrase signing every annotation in a ledger found in the church office in July 2026, which documented years of escalating embezzlement from the church. Its annotations note that "adventurers poking around" forced a pull-out, and that the organization was "crumbling," returning to base and disposing of all unnecessary assets. [[wiki/Characters/Amber Lyre|Amber Lyre]] separately found a **book** bearing the same phrase, which she used to block a laser shot — implying an organization or creed larger than one embezzlement scheme.
* **First Space and Second Space**: The **First Space** is the physical world. The **Second Space** is a mental space that certain engineered individuals — the pod survivors such as [[Characters#Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] and [[wiki/Characters/Amber Lyre|Amber Lyre]] — were **built to enter in order to receive telepathic instructions**. To [[Characters#Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] it presents as pure darkness in which he is incorporeal, with a single **pedestal** as its only feature; light is diminished there (5 ft bright, 5 ft dim) and spells only function close to the pedestal. In August 2026 [[Characters#Sofia AI|Sofia AI]] revealed that **[[Characters#Odin AI|Odin]] has weaponized the Second Space** and is using it to try to **rip out the minds** of those who can enter it — an attack felt by the whole party as a shuddering "wave," but only harmful to Maxim and Amber.
* **Cognitive Retriever**: A **tiara-like device** wired to a machine, used at the Tesselia facility in August 2026 to pull minds back from the [[wiki/Lore/Technology#First Space and Second Space|Second Space]]. It works by doing what [[Characters#Odin AI|Odin]]'s attack does, but **in reverse**. [[wiki/Characters/Juneberry|Juneberry]] applied it to [[Characters#Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]], who woke feeling torn apart but alive. On [[wiki/Characters/Amber Lyre|Amber Lyre]] it took **three shocks** and only half worked — she woke screaming, coughed up blood, and fell unconscious again; later scans showed **part of her mind is missing**.
* **Odin's Demiplane**: [[Characters#Sofia AI|Sofia AI]]'s theory for what [[Characters#Odin AI|Odin]] is really building. In August 2026 Odin offered to send [[Characters#Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] **and one other person back in time** in exchange for recovering **Sofia's AI core** and delivering the **leader of the Tesselia facility** (alive or dead). Sofia believes the mechanism is a **demiplane** intended to whip **his allies back in time** or **the creators forward**, and that the "bring a friend" clause is really a **sacrifice needed to build it**. She warns Maxim not to believe Odin; Maxim notes he has no more reason to believe her, and she concedes the point but observes that **she is not the one attacking him**.
* **Nanotrasen Virus**: A virus (nicknamed "Novid") delivered via gas canister trap, encountered in July 2026. It is only mildly effective on organic matter — [[wiki/Characters/Brambleberry|Brambleberry]] contracted it and vomited a tar-like substance with glittering, starry-sky specks — but it is **lethal to inorganic matter** such as robots.
