# Bestiary

Part of [[Lore/Lore|Lore]].

* **Flumph**: Docile, jellyfish-like aberrations from the Outer Planes. They are typically room-temperature jelly with a mucus coating and have multiple eye stalks. They are known to protect jewels and communicate through bioluminescence or touch.
* **Sticky Slime**: A sewer-dwelling creature that dissolves organic matter (like pencils) on contact.
* **Goon Balloon**: Cactus-eyed, chaotic evil creatures from other planes.
* **Grell**: Aberrations resembling floating brains with bird-like beaks and numerous tentacles.
* **Neh-thalggu (Brain Collector)**: Crab-like aberrations that collect brains to increase their magical power. They possess a lethal "Extract Brain" ability. One appeared when a symbol flared during the tower fight in May 2026. [[Sessions/2026/2026-05-14|Source]]

  ![[neh-thalggu.png|300]]
* **Eye Monger**: A one-eyed stone aberration.
* **Cranium Rats**: Rats with exposed, glowing brains that gain telepathic abilities and increased intelligence when in a swarm.
* **Death Kiss**: A beholder-kin with blood-draining tentacles instead of eye stalks.
* **Gibbering Mouther**: Amorphous masses of **eyes and mouths**. Two appeared in a bright flash when the party opened the yellow book in May 2026. The ground around them turns **putty-like**, and anyone nearby must make a **Strength save or get stuck** ([[Characters/PC/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] did). Their amorphous forms make them **immune to being knocked prone**, and their **gibbering** causes bad effects. Zinjaro's **Banishment failed**: something, likely the book, anchored them to the plane. [[Sessions/2026/2026-05-07|Source]]

  ![[GibberingMouths.png|300]]
* **Scion in Yellow**: The yellow-robed figure behind the disappearances near the Guild in April 2026, carrying an unnaturally yellow book whose pull ("Yellow madness") compelled [[Characters/PC/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]], [[Characters/PC/Villhelm Emberstoke|Villhelm]] and [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]] to want it. On 2026-05-21 the party met it in a tower room as a **floating figure in a yellow cloak** speaking with **three discordant voices** (two male, one female), heard differently by each listener: Aolis heard his mother and sisters screaming, [[Characters/PC/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] a crackling fire, [[Characters/PC/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] generic voices. It delivered a prophecy, *"In morte imperii deus noster de caelo descendet"* (in the death of the empire, our god will descend from space), then **transformed into a tentacled creature**. The party fought it on 2026-05-28: it tried to dominate Ogra, proved immune to cold and poison, and at half health pulled everyone onto the Transcendent plane (Wild Magic, difficult terrain) where it crashed planets onto them. Maxim killed it with a Psychic Lance. When he opened its yellow book, the party was blinded and returned, and a message remained: *"He has seen you. You have been noted."* [[Sessions/2026/2026-04-30|Source]], [[Sessions/2026/2026-05-07|Source]], [[Sessions/2026/2026-05-21|Source]], [[Sessions/2026/2026-05-28|Source]]

  ![[yellow-cloak.png|300]]
* **Super Heavy Chassis**: A **Colossal**-sized robotic war machine of the invading army. In August 2026, a tank robot aboard the freight airship at the [[Locations/Tesselia|Tesselia]] docks died interfacing with a console but succeeded in **summoning one**; the [[Characters/NPC/Overseer (VI)|Overseer]] urged the party to flee rather than face it. The party escaped it on 2026-08-27 via a hidden elevator, [[Characters/PC/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] buying time with a **Light Snare** that pinned it for a turn. Later that same session, **a second Super Heavy Chassis** was spotted on the upper level, in the room the surviving robots retreated into. On 2026-09-03 the party found the ship is **building them**: a bay of **massive suspension arms holding deactivated chassis**, an **active assembly** room a level up, and a **target-practice room** where live chassis fire on anything crossing their line of sight ([[Characters/PC/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] was burned by flames doing exactly that). [[Sessions/2026/2026-08-20|Source]], [[Sessions/2026/2026-08-27|Source]], [[Sessions/2026/2026-09-03|Source]]
* **Blue Slaad**: Large, toad-like aberrations from the Outer Planes. They are formidable combatants and can be summoned by powerful entities like the [[Lore/Bestiary#Scion in Yellow|Scion in Yellow]].
* **Gnolls (Red-Eyed)**: A band of gnolls ambushed the party in a forest pass on the way to Wheat Burrow (October 2026) and were easily beaten. They were **well fed and free of disease**, so **unlikely to have been aggressive**, and their **red eyes dulled to amber** after death. Whether that is normal is unknown. [[Sessions/2026/2026-10-08|Source]]
* **Alp**: A **fey creature** met in October 2026 disguised as a **pale white pygmy goat in a floppy, wizard-like hat** that followed the party, bleated when spoken to, and shied from petting. [[Characters/PC/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]]'s **True Seeing** revealed it. Alps are not innately dangerous but **inflict nightmares on travelers**, and sometimes travel with **other creatures that prey on exhausted adventurers**. [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]] killed it with Chill Touch. [[Sessions/2026/2026-10-08|Source]]

  ![[Alp.png|300]]
* **Treacle**: A blood-drinking creature that looks like a **small rabbit with an injured leg**. One crept to the party's campfire in October 2026, ate rations and some Sentinel's Flask, then chewed through [[Characters/PC/Brambleberry|Bramble]]'s pants and **drained her blood** until she passed out with **2 levels of exhaustion**; its wound **resisted closing**. When hurt it **lunges around**. [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]] killed it. The players did not recognize it; the name comes from the GM. [[Sessions/2026/2026-10-08|Source]]
