# Bestiary

Part of [[wiki/Lore/Lore|Lore]].

* **Flumph**: Docile, jellyfish-like aberrations from the Outer Planes. They are typically room-temperature jelly with a mucus coating and have multiple eye stalks. They are known to protect jewels and communicate through bioluminescence or touch.
* **Sticky Slime**: A sewer-dwelling creature that dissolves organic matter (like pencils) on contact.
* **Goon Balloon**: Cactus-eyed, chaotic evil creatures from other planes.
* **Grell**: Aberrations resembling floating brains with bird-like beaks and numerous tentacles.
* **Neh-thalggu (Brain Collector)**: Crab-like aberrations that collect brains to increase their magical power. They possess a lethal "Extract Brain" ability.
* **Eye Monger**: A one-eyed stone aberration.
* **Cranium Rats**: Rats with exposed, glowing brains that gain telepathic abilities and increased intelligence when in a swarm.
* **Death Kiss**: A beholder-kin with blood-draining tentacles instead of eye stalks.
* **Yellow Cloak (Figure)**: A mysterious entity that speaks with three discordant voices. It delivered a prophecy regarding the death of the empire and a descending god before transforming into a tentacled creature.
* **Scion in Yellow**: The yellow-robed figure behind the disappearances near the Guild in April 2026, carrying an unnaturally yellow book whose pull ("Yellow madness") compelled [[wiki/Characters/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]], [[wiki/Characters/Villhelm Emberstoke|Villhelm]] and [[wiki/Characters/Aolis|Aolis]] to want it. The party fought it on 2026-05-28: it tried to dominate [[wiki/Characters/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]], proved immune to cold and poison, and at half health pulled everyone onto the Transcendent plane (Wild Magic, difficult terrain) where it crashed planets onto them. [[wiki/Characters/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] killed it with a Psychic Lance. When he opened its yellow book, the party was blinded and returned, and a message remained: *"He has seen you. You have been noted."* See also the [[wiki/Lore/Bestiary#Yellow Cloak (Figure)|Yellow Cloak (Figure)]]. [[wiki/Sessions/2026/2026-04-30|Source]], [[wiki/Sessions/2026/2026-05-07|Source]], [[wiki/Sessions/2026/2026-05-28|Source]]
* **Super Heavy Chassis**: A **Colossal**-sized robotic war machine of the invading army. In August 2026, a tank robot aboard the freight airship at the [[wiki/Locations/Locations#Tesselia|Tesselia]] docks died interfacing with a console but succeeded in **summoning one**; the [[wiki/Lore/Technology#The Overseer (VI)|Overseer]] urged the party to flee rather than face it. The party escaped it on 2026-08-27 via a hidden elevator, [[Characters/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] buying time with a **Light Snare** that pinned it for a turn. Later that same session, **a second Super Heavy Chassis** was spotted on the upper level, in the room the surviving robots retreated into. On 2026-09-03 the party found the ship is **building them**: a bay of **massive suspension arms holding deactivated chassis**, an **active assembly** room a level up, and a **target-practice room** where live chassis fire on anything crossing their line of sight ([[Characters/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] was burned by flames doing exactly that). [[wiki/Sessions/2026/2026-08-20|Source]], [[wiki/Sessions/2026/2026-08-27|Source]], [[wiki/Sessions/2026/2026-09-03|Source]]
* **Blue Slaad**: Large, toad-like aberrations from the Outer Planes. They are formidable combatants and can be summoned by powerful entities like the [[wiki/Lore/Bestiary#Scion in Yellow|Scion in Yellow]].
