# Locations of Interest

Part of [[Lore/Lore|Lore]].

* **[[Locations/Tower Isle|Tower Isle]]**: The location of [[Lore/Major Projects#Project Horizon|Project Horizon]], currently inhabited by "[[Lore/Factions and Organizations#Technocrats|Technocrats]]" (an insectoid race). Protected by 300mm guns. In September 2026 the GM clarified out of character that **this world's drow**, [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]]'s people, are a **blend of the original drow and the Drukhari**, and that they **live beneath Tower Isle**, along with **hostile, racist insectoids** and **more robots**. It is the **home base of the freight airship**, which Odin's guidance returned there. [[Sessions/2026/2026-09-24|Source]]
* **The Mountain**: A **large primordial creature** (GM clarification, 2026-10-08) [[Sessions/2026/2026-10-08|Source]]. Before that it was known only as a mysterious location or phenomenon mentioned by [[Characters/NPC/Queen Tesselia|Queen Tesselia]] and others. It may be related to the tectonic activity, though [[Characters/NPC/Sofia AI|Sofia AI]] lacks sufficient data. Some believe it is a destination or a source of power. On 2026-10-01 [[Characters/PC/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] recalled it from **folklore**: it is **worshiped by many subterranean creatures** (kobolds, goblins, goblin-adjacent folk) and has existed **since before the material plane**. **Another group of adventurers awoke it accidentally**; it quieted but **seems to be rising again** as new cults form. [[Characters/NPC/Angela Nevermore|Nevermore]] raised it and the Queen wants the party to **scout it**. See [[Quests/Quests#Tasks|Scout The Mountain]]. [[Sessions/2026/2026-10-01|Source]] On 2026-10-08 the party set out **on foot** toward it, by way of **Wheat Burrow** and the troubled **Lumber Camp** ([[Locations/Locations|Locations]]). [[Sessions/2026/2026-10-08|Source]]
* **Libra Sands**: A region where artificers may be trying to build airships.
