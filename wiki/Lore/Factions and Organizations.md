# Factions and Organizations

Part of [[wiki/Lore/Lore|Lore]].

* **The Severance**: A new faction back home in [[wiki/Locations/Locations#Moren|Moren]], reported by [[Characters#Maxwella|Maxwella]] in August 2026. They want to **"break the chains"** and take control of the **floating mountain where the college is** — the [[wiki/Locations/Locations#Mountain Home Academy of Magic|Mountain Home Academy of Magic]].
* **The Past and Future, Running Free**: See the entry under [[wiki/Lore/Technology|Technology]] — an organization or creed signing an embezzlement ledger recovered near Tesselia, also found on a book carried by [[wiki/Characters/Amber Lyre|Amber Lyre]].
* **The Overseer (VI)**: See [[wiki/Lore/Technology#The Overseer (VI)|The Overseer]] — a body of [[wiki/Lore/Technology#Slave Drives|Slave Drives]] claiming independence from all prior factions.
* **Technocrats**: The "Technocrats of the Ministrate", an insectoid race on [[wiki/Lore/Locations of Interest#Tower Isle|Tower Isle]], the island in the central sea. They control its government, which is split into oligarchic dictators by region. They act as a trading kingdom and are known as hoarders; 300mm guns with a 20+ mile range keep non-Technocrats away. The party's insectoid friends (Tick, Nock and Screeck) are of this folk, and in July 2026 the [[wiki/Lore/Technology#The Overseer (VI)|Overseer]] held CCTV footage of them.
