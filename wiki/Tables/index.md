# Tables

Obsidian Bases that list wiki notes by their `type` frontmatter property. They update on their own as notes are added, so keep each note's properties correct rather than editing the bases.

| Table | Lists | Views |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Tables/Characters.base\|Characters]] | Notes with `type: character` (PCs and NPCs), with name, species, class, PC/NPC, player and sheet link | All Characters, Player Characters, NPCs |
| [[Tables/Sessions.base\|Sessions]] | Notes with `type: session-note`, by date | All Sessions, By Year |
| [[Tables/Quests.base\|Quests]] | Notes with `type: quest` | Quests |
| [[Tables/Lore.base\|Lore]] | Notes with `type: lore` (the Lore hub and topic notes) | Lore Topics |
