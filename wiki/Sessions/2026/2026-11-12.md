# Session: 2026-11-12

Picking up from [[Sessions/2026/2026-11-05|2026-11-05]], the **Horrified** effect scrambles the party as they fight the **aeorian nullifier** and **adult kruthik** summoned at the cult camp. Channelers in the back finish a chant and bring in an **aeorian absorber**, but [[Characters/PC/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] kills both aeorians. With the fight over, the party finds **altars to three different deities** and a **pit of bodies**. The cult is dealt with, but there is **still no specific information about the dragon**.

## Horrified and the Kruthik
- The **Horrified** effect causes several random things: [[Characters/PC/Villhelm Emberstoke|Villhelm]] is **stunned**, [[Characters/PC/Brambleberry|Bramble]] **slashes randomly and hits Villhelm**, and **Ogra** and Aolis's **Hound of Ill Omen** **run away frightened**.
- Bramble **kills one kruthik** with **psychic daggers**.
- [[Characters/PC/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] throws a **Fireball** into the crowd and **kills the drow mage**, the same mage identified last session as the retainer of Aolis's sister. The party did not comment on his identity this session.

## The Nullifier
- [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]] casts **Circle of Death**, but it does **no damage** to the **aeorian nullifier**, which is **immune to necrotic**.
- Nullifiers are **the bane of all magic users**: they can **resist and deny magical effects**. [[Characters/PC/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]]'s **Flame Pillar** is **Counterspelled**.
- The nullifier **rushes the party to attack up close**, though most of its attacks miss. Villhelm gets **frightened** and **runs away**.
- A **group of lizardfolk** shows up.
- Maxim and Aolis **focus their spells on the channelers** at the back, who are chanting. Ogra rushes in to attack the nullifier.
- Zinjaro steps back and uses **Divine Intervention** to cast **Aura of Purity**, protecting his allies from further **Horrid Gnashing** saves (the Horrified effect, which triggers if they are **within 20 feet of the nullifier**).

## The Absorber
- The channelers **finish their chant** and an **aeorian absorber** appears. (See [[Lore/Bestiary#Aeorian Creatures|Aeorian Creatures]].)
- The absorber **pounces on Bramble**, knocking her **prone**. The nullifier **strikes her several times**, but Ogra takes the chance for a **sentinel attack that crits**.
- Maxim casts **Cone of Cold** on the nullifier, the absorber and **two lizardfolk**, but the absorber's **Tail Ray absorbs half the damage and redirects it to Maxim**.
- After a few more hits, **Ogra kills the nullifier** (good, as it could have used an **anti-magic ability**).
- Zinjaro "casts" **staff**: he hits the absorber with a **Shillelagh**-empowered staff.
- **Ogra finishes off the absorber**, shoving its **eyestalk into its mouth**.

## The Altars and the Pit
- After combat, the party investigates some **unconscious lizardfolk**. There is a **pit with bodies**.
- Zinjaro investigates the **altars**: they are of **different religions**: one for a **nondescript blood god**, one of **balance**, with **scales of justice** iconography, and one **draconic**. They would **give traits to the summoned creatures**. They may be **lesser deities**; Zinjaro recognizes none of them. (See [[Lore/Deities and Religions#Cult Encampment Altars|Cult Encampment Altars]].)
- Villhelm reveals his **father was a messed-up dragonborn**, whom he left to **study the monk ways**.
- Villhelm goes to pull a body from the pile but is **overcome** and has to back away.
- The unconscious lizardfolk **continue to bleed**. Maxim gives one a sip of the **Sentinel flask** to heal him, but it **immediately starts attacking**, so the party **puts them down**.
