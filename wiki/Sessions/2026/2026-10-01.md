# Session: 2026-10-01

After the long rest that followed [[Sessions/2026/2026-09-24|2026-09-24]], the party meets three guests at the [[Locations/Locations#Adventurers Guild Hall|guild hall]]: **[[Characters/NPC/Alisha Tower|Alisha Tower]]** (now Queen of Drakenweld), **[[Characters/NPC/Angela Nevermore|Angela Nevermore]]**, and **[[Characters/NPC/Maxwella|Maxwella]]**. The siege of Tesselia is over, [[Characters/NPC/Sofia AI|Sofia AI]] debriefs the party through a metallic spider, and a scouting mission to **[[Lore/Locations of Interest#The Mountain|The Mountain]]** is assigned.

## Guests Arrive
- Everyone except [[Characters/PC/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] and [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]] heads downstairs.
- **Alisha** explains she was away **caring for her mother**, who was **trying to hand the kingdom to a demon**. A **new court of nobles** now sits in [[Locations/Drakenweld|Drakenweld]]. She got a lot of use out of her spear, **Sky Dancer**. (See [[Quests/Drakenweld Quest|Drakenweld Quest]].)
- Maxwella goes upstairs to see Maxim and Aolis, who are **sharing a room**. Maxim is **only in underwear**; she offers to help him dress but **refuses to do it for him**, and waits outside while they get decent. She suggests Aolis **whip Maxim** so he gets up earlier.
- **Angela Nevermore** and [[Characters/NPC/Maggie North|Maggie North]] step aside to talk while the rest go to the bar with Alisha.

## The Siege Is Over
- Alisha came to help **break the siege**, but it is **already over**: the **ships have all left**.
- Aolis asks Nevermore and Maggie about it. The reason is unclear. **Sofia AI thinks the ships may have been recalled**, and the notes say **[[Characters/NPC/Odin AI|Odin]] itself recalled them**.
- Aolis asks about [[Characters/NPC/Amber Lyre|Amber]]: she is **stable**.
- Nevermore and Aolis join the table. Maxim is handed a **floating plate of food**, which he assumes is magic. It is carried by a **little pixie**, and the party then notices **a bunch of pixies ferrying plates** around.

## Debrief with Sofia AI
- The party goes upstairs to a room to talk. **Nevermore sets a device to prevent eavesdropping**. Maxim recognizes it as an **anti-espionage device**, but nothing more.
- A **metallic spider** crawls out and **projects Sofia AI**. She asks for a rundown of what happened on the ship.
- She asks for more on the **[[Characters/NPC/Overseer (VI)|Overseer VI]]** and realizes it is a **Gen 3**. They **did not expect any to still be around**; it was **ancient even in Maxim's time**.
- The **new core** the party took is a **Gen 4 AI core**, the kind Sofia would use for **subroutines**. Sofia says **they can get the Overseer a body**. (See [[Quests/Quests#Tasks|Restore the Overseer]].)

## The Mountain
- Nevermore brings up **[[Lore/Locations of Interest#The Mountain|The Mountain]]**. [[Characters/PC/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] recalls it from **folklore**: it is **worshiped by many subterranean creatures** (kobolds, goblins, goblin-adjacent folk) and has existed **since before the material plane**.
- **Another group of adventurers awoke it accidentally**. It quieted down but **seems to be rising again** as new worshipers appear.
- **The Queen wants the party to scout it.** Maxim wonders whether this is as pressing as the Odin situation, but there is no information to judge by.

### Three Goals
1. Find out whether the **cults are actually affecting The Mountain** or **empowering** it.
2. **Thin out the cults.**
3. Deal with an **adult dragon** that has been talked about; they want it **gone**.

- The dragon has **stolen things and attracted attention**. The party is **welcome to any hoard**.
- Reward: **500 platinum**, described in the notes as **half a queen's random**.
- [[Characters/PC/Villhelm Emberstoke|Villhelm]] is convinced the party **can talk to the dragon**. Maxim is not sure.

## Open Threads
- Why the siege fleet withdrew, and whether **Odin recalled it**.
- **The Mountain** scouting mission: cult influence, thinning the cults, and the adult dragon.
- Whether the dragon can be reasoned with, and what hoard it holds.
- A **Gen 4 core** (the Overseer's replacement) and Sofia's offer of a **body**.
- **Amber** is stable, but her missing piece of mind is unresolved.
