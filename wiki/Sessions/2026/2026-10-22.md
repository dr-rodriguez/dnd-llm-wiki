# Session: 2026-10-22

Picking up from [[Sessions/2026/2026-10-08|2026-10-08]], the party keeps heading for **Lumber Camp** with a **Clydesdale draft horse** (the riderless horse, now [[Characters/PC/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]]'s) and a found **barrel of crimson wine**. At the camp a second **hadozee** asks for help with **goblinoids**. In the woods the party meets **21 orcs** carrying two **halflings**, **rescues them** in a fire-filled fight, and the halflings use **circle magic** to summon a rainstorm and douse the blaze.

## On the Road
- [[Characters/PC/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] takes the horse, so the party now has a **draft horse**: a **Clydesdale**.
- [[Characters/PC/Brambleberry|Bramble]] still has **2 levels of exhaustion** after resting (she had started with 3).
- Maxim offers [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]] a ride on the horse; Bramble is tired but **does not get a ride**.

### The Abandoned Cart
- The party finds an **overturned, abandoned cart** with one wheel **missing and heavily damaged**.
- A nearby **intact barrel** is full of **deep, crimson wine** ([[Characters/PC/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] opens it).
- The party argues: several want to **sell** it, [[Characters/PC/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] wants to **drink** it. They begin dividing it among their **water skins**, but Ogra **carries the barrel** along.

## Lumber Camp
- The party reaches the edge of a **temperate, coniferous forest** and arrives at **[[Locations/Locations|Lumber Camp]]** around **noon**.
- Several go into a building with a **tankard and fork symbol**, a kind of **common house**.
- Inside is another **hadozee**, of **normal size** (Zinjaro is small), studying a map.
- They report **goblinoid issues** and **too few people** to handle them. They have also noticed **reptilian creatures** and a **male drow**. **The party agrees to help.** (See [[Quests/Quests#Tasks|Scout The Mountain]].)
- Zinjaro mentions the barrel and the need to protect it. The camp's **third cart had gone missing**; now they know where it went. **The barrel is handed over** and Zinjaro is sad.
- The camp **does not recognize the horse** the party has.
- They offer Bramble **coffee** for her exhaustion; it is **very bitter**. Maxim tries some too and **reels**, as it is not what he is used to.
- Zinjaro buys **mushroom powder (25 gp)** for his spells and casts **Greater Restoration**, clearing Bramble's **remaining exhaustion**.

## Orcs in the Woods
- Heading into the woods, Maxim **hears people on the move** and the party gives chase.
- They find **21 orcs** carrying **a pair of halflings**.
- Ogra approaches but the orcs **keep running**. Maxim is inclined to hide, but Zinjaro wants to save the halflings and casts **Wall of Fire** to block their path. **Combat begins.**
- The orcs are **pretty weak**. One halfling, **feet shackled**, breaks free and reaches Ogra.
- The party keeps casting **fire spells** to hit the orcs in groups, which sets **many trees on fire**. **Both halflings are saved.**

## Putting Out the Fire
- The party argues about the fire; nobody knows how to put it out.
- The two halflings have Zinjaro and Aolis **stand in particular places** and start chanting: **circle magic**.
- After about **5 minutes**, a **rainstorm** appears and puts out the fires.
- The halflings **do not know why they were captured**. They were traveling through the woods with friends when the orcs ambushed them; they said **something about a ring**.
- They **point the party to where they have seen goblins**.

## Open Threads
- The **goblinoid problem at Lumber Camp**, plus the **reptilian creatures** and **male drow** the camp has seen. The halflings pointed out where the goblins were.
- Why the orcs **captured the halflings**, and what the **ring** they mentioned is.
- Where the **Clydesdale** came from: Lumber Camp does not recognize it (see the riderless horse in [[Sessions/2026/2026-10-08|2026-10-08]]).
- The **wine cart**: the camp's third cart is recovered, but who abandoned it is unknown.
- The **fires** started in the woods were put out by the halflings' rainstorm.
