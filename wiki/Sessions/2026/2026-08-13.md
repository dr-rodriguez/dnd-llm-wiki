# Session: 2026-08-13

Breakfast with [[wiki/Characters#Maxwella|Maxwella]] is interrupted: [[B-Team]] arrives badly wounded and one member short. The party takes their rescue mission for them and infiltrates an airship at the docks.

## B-Team's Loss
- The party hears a commotion mid-breakfast. **B-Team is here and badly hurt** — and **[[B-Team|Maggie]] is missing**. [[wiki/Characters#Sofia AI|Sofia AI]] shows up and tells them to stop running.
- They had been facing a **large droid**. **Maggie fell over while everyone got sick momentarily**, and she was captured then. The party judges this **likely the same phenomenon** that struck [[Characters/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] and [[Characters/Amber Lyre|Amber]] — a [[wiki/Lore#First Space and Second Space|Second Space]] attack.
- The various queens emerge from a side area. [[Characters/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] spends time healing B-Team; [[Characters/Aolis|Aolis]] throws down bananas (**Goodberries**) from upstairs for Zinjaro to distribute.
- B-Team is eager to go straight back out after Maggie, but **Taylor is out of spell slots** and everyone is heavily injured even after Zinjaro's help. **The party offers to go in their stead.**

### The Mechanical Slate
- **Melody** hands Zinjaro a **mechanical slate** — a device with a button and a screen. **Click the button twice and B-Team will show up**; a confirmation message appears after the first click.
- They don't know how many times it can be used. It is meant as a **last resort** to bring them over.

## Scrying the Airship
- [[Characters/Brambleberry|Brambleberry]] gets a notification of **coordinates in the docks**. The docks are **fully in the hands of the invading army**.
- Maxim sends a message; the rest of the party doesn't know what it said.
- While [[Characters/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] and others buy potions, Zinjaro casts **Scrying** as a ritual to find Maggie:
  - The southeast part of the city — a **massive airship**, offloading **hordes of metallic soldiers**.
  - On an upper layer, **Maggie is tied to a chair**. Featureless room, a small table along the left wall from her perspective. **Two very sleek androids with fully articulating faces**.
  - The connection shakes; one android holds up a device and clicks it. **The connection is severed.** The enemy now knows they were being watched and may be on alert.
- Ogra buys all **3 greater healing potions** available.

## The Overseer's Bargain
- The party moves stealthily to the docks and finds a **utilitarian freight vessel, like a big cube**.
- Brambleberry spots **weird ripples**. Maxim throws dirt at the area and feels something **hard and mechanical clamp around his neck** — cloaked [[wiki/Lore#Slave Drives|Slave Drives]]. The **[[wiki/Lore#The Overseer (VI)|Overseer-VI]]** appears and calls its drive off. It has **5 Slave Drives** with it.
- The Overseer is **confused as to why the party would risk their lives for one person**.
- **The deal:** the Overseer will **draw the large droid group away** while the party infiltrates. In exchange, the party retrieves an **ancient core fragment** from the ship's upper layers.
  - **The Overseer's own core is failing — it will die in less than 3 days.** It needs a core, even a corrupted one.
  - The fragment is **outside [[wiki/Characters#Odin AI|Odin]]'s reach** and is being shipped to the **island** for study.
  - It sits in a **specialized compartment case**, very heavy. **Ogra** is deemed large enough to carry it.
- The party agrees.

## Infiltration
- A **Slave Drive rushes in and detonates**, taking out a cluster of droids. The remaining enemy force pursues the Overseer and its drives. The party takes cover and the way clears.
- Some guards remain, but far fewer. The ship's opening is about **10 feet up**, reachable by a walkway; the party hides underneath it to plan.
- With no reliable way to climb a sheer wall and no guarantee anything is up top, they **rush in**.
- **Combat begins.** Lots of robots remain, and they are landing lucky big hits.

*Session ends mid-combat.*
