# Session: 2026-11-19

Picking up from [[Sessions/2026/2026-11-12|2026-11-12]], [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]] searches the remains of the **drow mage** and finds a **coded letter** and a **heat-warped silver box**. [[Characters/PC/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] raises a **Temple of the Gods** to [[Characters/NPC/Sol'Ar|Sol'Ar]] for a safe rest, and the party reads that the letter comes from an **agent of the court** working in Northern Andore. After reporting to the [[Locations/Locations|Lumber Camp]] and being tailed by a **persistent goose**, the party reaches a **raided mining camp** by the mountains, where the goose lures Aolis into a **locked room** with two slimes.

## The Drow Mage's Effects
- Aolis investigates the remains of the drow mage and finds **a letter** and **a delicate silver box**.
- The box is **warped from heat** and not easily opened. [[Characters/PC/Brambleberry|Bramble]] can open it but it **takes her a few hours**; Aolis tells her to **unlock it but not open it yet**.
- The letter is **in code**. [[Characters/PC/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] can't read it but Aolis can. Outrider parties use this style of coded document, and **breaking the cipher will take time**. (See [[Lore/Factions and Organizations#Outrider Parties|Outrider Parties]].)

## Temple of the Gods
- Zinjaro casts **Temple of the Gods** and an **Aztec-style temple to Sol'Ar** emerges from the night, with **bananas, wine and glowing sunlight despite the darkness**.
- It is a **protected area where the party can long rest**.

![[SolAr-Temple-Spell.jpg]]
> Temple of the Gods, dedicated to Sol'Ar

## The Box and the Letter
- Inside the silver box is a **small dark sphere** surrounded by **two rings**, the inner **gold** and the outer **brass**, with **runes** on the rings. Aolis can't tell what it is for; it **looks like a gyroscope**. (See [[Lore/Technology#The Silver Box Gyroscope|The Silver Box Gyroscope]].)
- The letter is **from an agent of the court** and discusses **ongoing operations in the Northern Andore region**. The **dragon's appearance and the "infestation" of the Mountain** have provided **cover** for the operation.
- It says **"We know she is in the region, we have not laid eyes on her"**, possibly about Aolis.
- Several **names** are at the bottom: **two in another language** and a third, **Essa**, which Aolis doesn't recognize.
- The letter is written in **different scripts, possibly by three writers in three languages**.

## The Goose
- Heading back toward the Lumber Camp to report on the cult, Maxim spots a **goose** in the way; it **honks** at them.
- [[Characters/PC/Villhelm Emberstoke|Villhelm]] tries to grab it and **fails**; it tries to bite him but he **steps away in time**. Aolis tries to pet it, **touches the beak** and **gets bitten**. The party ignores it and moves on.
- At the camp they **talk to the hadozee** about what they encountered and **have lunch at the tavern**.
- **The goose has followed them.** Aolis feeds it vegetables while Villhelm grabs it, and they discuss **cooking it**, but it **flies out and walks away**.

## Wolves on the Road
- Heading north again, there is **no Temple of the Gods tonight**.
- At sunset Maxim notices **5 wolves** following them, **fanning out around the party**.
- *Out of character:* it wasn't a tarrasque. After Maxim rolled a 1, the DM gave the party a **funny choice of a 1 or a 100 on a d100 roll**.
- **Maxim kills the wolves with magic**; the party doesn't run the fight since they have such low HP.

## The Raided Mining Camp
- The next day the party reaches an **encampment by the mountains** that looks like a **mining camp that has been raided**. A **mine shaft** on the far side of the town **goes deeper into the mountain**.
- Aolis **gets separated and spots the goose again**, follows it into a room, and the room **gets locked**.
- **Two slimes appear** and the goose **disappears through the floorboards, honking** as it goes. Aolis suspects **a trap**.

## Open Threads
- The **coded letter** from an agent of the court: cipher still being broken; mentions **operations in Northern Andore**, a woman "in the region" (maybe Aolis), and the names **Essa** plus two others.
- The **silver box gyroscope**: a dark sphere inside gold and brass runed rings, purpose unknown. Bramble unlocked the box.
- The **goose**: does it lead Aolis into a trap, and what is it?
- **Aolis is locked in a room with two slimes** at the raided mining camp; the rest of the party's whereabouts are unclear.
- The **mine shaft** leading deeper into the mountain.
