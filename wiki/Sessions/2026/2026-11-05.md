# Session: 2026-11-05

Picking up from [[Sessions/2026/2026-10-22|2026-10-22]], the party arrives at a **cult encampment** in the dark and is quickly spotted, sparking a fight against **gnolls, hobgoblins, goblins and duergar**, a transformed **fire elemental**, and a goliath named **Rivermaw Stormborn**. [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]] recognizes a **drow mage** as the **retainer of his next-oldest sister**. The session ends with a **ritual** that summons an **aeorian nullifier** and two **adult kruthik**, leaving three party members **Horrified**. [[Characters/PC/Villhelm Emberstoke|Villhelm]] kills Rivermaw along the way.

## The Cult Encampment
- The party reaches a **campfire** ringed by people in **mismatched clothing**. It is **very dark**; only a few of the party have **darkvision**.
- [[Characters/PC/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] and a few others creep closer. **Big yurts** stand around the camp.
- [[Characters/PC/Brambleberry|Bramble]] overhears **gnolls, hobgoblins and goblins** making small talk: *"I wonder who will get picked next for ascension"* and *"Lizardmen near river."*
- [[Characters/PC/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] moves closer and casts **Thaumaturgy** to distract them with **ominous whispers**. It works poorly: **a hobgoblin spots him**, but only him.
- A gnoll **calls for backup** and more enemies arrive. **Combat begins.**

## The Fight
- **Faerie Fire** lights up several enemies.
- Zinjaro is hit by **Heat Metal**, but [[Characters/PC/Villhelm Emberstoke|Villhelm]] **breaks the caster's concentration** with punches.
- Maxim's **Fireball** kills a **large group of gnolls** and **sets a yurt on fire**.
- Aolis uses **Inflict Wounds** on a gnoll; Bramble jumps in with **psychic dagger** attacks.
- A hobgoblin says something in **Goblin**; nobody in the party understands it.
- An **ashari firetamer** starts chanting and **transforms into a fire elemental**. Bramble **takes it out** soon after.
- [[Characters/PC/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] **kills two hobgoblins** with their **frost iron sword**.

## The Drow Mage
- **Ominous chanting** starts up in the distance. Meanwhile, new enemies arrive: **duergar** and a **goliath** called **Rivermaw Stormborn**.
- Maxim shoots a **Fireball toward the chanting**, lighting the area a little. The party spots a **drow mage**.
- Aolis rushes toward them and **recognizes them**: the **retainer to his sister**. Aolis is the oldest, so this is the **next-oldest sister** ([[Characters/Characters|Madeleine]] on the roster). He summons his **Hound of Ill Omen**.
- At a **nod from the drow**, which works as unspoken orders to **capture Aolis**, Rivermaw rushes in and **grapples him**.
- Zinjaro casts **Shield of Faith** on Aolis (it feels like **warm sunlight**) and uses **The Solar Gift** to cast **Daylight**, lighting the area.
- Villhelm and Bramble attack Rivermaw, Maxim casts **Hold Person**, and the **Hound knocks Rivermaw prone**. Ogra's **vines pull Aolis to safety**, and Villhelm **finishes off Rivermaw**.

## The Ritual
- Several party members see a **ritual completed in which several goblins are killed**.
- It **summons an aeorian nullifier and two adult kruthik**.
- Ogra, Bramble and Villhelm gain the **Horrified** condition; Zinjaro **resists**.
- *Out of character:* the GM says the party will learn what Horrified means in-game next session.

## Open Threads
- **Aolis's sister's retainer** is in the cult camp. Why a drow from his family is working with a cult, and whether the sister herself is involved, is unknown.
- The **drow mage** wanted **Aolis captured**: a nod acted as unspoken orders to Rivermaw. Rivermaw is dead, but the drow's fate and who sent her are not stated.
- The **ritual summons**: an **aeorian nullifier** and two **adult kruthik**, and the **Horrified** condition on Ogra, Bramble and Villhelm.
- The cult's talk of **"ascension"** (who gets picked next) and **lizardmen near the river**, tying into the camp's earlier reports of reptilian creatures.
- What the **hobgoblin** said in **Goblin**, which nobody in the party understood.
