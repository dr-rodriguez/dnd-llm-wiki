# Session: 2026-10-08

Picking up from [[Sessions/2026/2026-10-01|2026-10-01]], the party sets out on foot from a depleted [[Locations/Tesselia|Tesselia]] toward **[[Lore/Locations of Interest#The Mountain|The Mountain]]**, heading for **Wheat Burrow** and the troubled **Lumber Camp**. On the road they brush off a **gnoll ambush**, are trailed by a goat in a floppy hat that [[Characters/PC/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] unmasks as an **[[Lore/Bestiary#Alp|Alp]]**, and spend an uneasy night: a **riderless, bloodied horse** wanders into camp, and a "rabbit" drains [[Characters/PC/Brambleberry|Bramble]] into **exhaustion** before [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]] kills it.

## Setting Out
- *Out of character:* **The Mountain is a large primordial creature.**
- **Lumber Camp** has been having **some problems**, and it lies on the way.
- Supplies in Tesselia are low: the city is **no longer a Tier 1 capital**. Basic supplies are still available, but **potions are limited** and there are **no magic items**.
- The party discusses buying a **cart for Brambleberry to pull**, then decides to **walk**.
- It is **mid spring**. The party heads toward **Wheat Burrow**, on the way to Lumber Camp. (See [[Quests/Quests#Tasks|Scout The Mountain]].)

## Gnoll Ambush
- In a **forest pass**, a band of **gnolls** ambushes the party. The fight is **no challenge**.
- The gnolls were **well fed** and **free of disease**, so they were **unlikely to have been aggressive**. Their **red eyes dulled to amber** after death; the party does not know whether that is normal. (See [[Lore/Bestiary#Gnolls (Red-Eyed)|Gnolls (Red-Eyed)]].)

## The Goat in the Hat
- Back on the road, Bramble notices a **pale white pygmy goat** following them, wearing a **floppy white hat**.
- Bramble tries to speak with it; it only **bleats**. Aolis tries to pet it and it moves away. [[Characters/PC/Villhelm Emberstoke|Villhelm]] tries too and **scares it**, but it keeps following.
- At camp, Zinjaro casts **Goodberry** as **magical bananas** for anyone who wants one, goat included. [[Characters/PC/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] prepares a **tent**.
- Bramble and Aolis offer the goat Goodberries; the notes say Bramble **"throws her into the campfire"** (presumably her goodberry). Aolis **manages to pet it**. The hat looks a bit like a **wizard hat**.

### True Seeing
- Zinjaro casts **True Seeing**: it is just a goat... but not. He sees its true form, an **Alp**, a **fey creature**.
- An Alp is not innately dangerous, but **inflicts nightmares on travelers**, and sometimes travels with **other creatures that prey on the exhausted adventurers**.

![[Alp.png|303]]
> Image of an alp

- Zinjaro says they should **kill it**. Aolis is reluctant until Zinjaro explains what an Alp is, then **kills it with Chill Touch** and wanders off to **bury it**.

## First Watch: The Riderless Horse
- [[Characters/PC/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] and Aolis take first watch. A **fully saddled horse** walks into camp from the **northeast**.
- Ogra takes its **saddlebag**: **30 gold**, rations and assorted supplies. The bag is **bloody**.
- A **second saddlebag is slashed open**, seemingly by a **poorly maintained weapon**.

## Second Watch: The Rabbit
- Aolis and Bramble take second watch. A **small rabbit with an injured leg** rustles near the fire and eagerly eats some rations and some of Aolis's **Sentinel's Flask**.
- It chews a hole in Bramble's pants (technically **Maxim's old pants**), then **bites her and drains blood**. She **passes out with 2 levels of exhaustion**.
- Aolis notices too late. His **Chill Touch** hurts it and it starts **lunging around**; Maxim wakes as a **second spell attack** from Aolis **kills it**.
- Something **keeps Bramble's wound from closing**, but Aolis wipes it and helps **bind it**.
- Bramble **finishes a long rest**, recovering **one level of exhaustion** (down to 1).
- *Out of character:* it was a **treacle**, which the players did not recognize. (See [[Lore/Bestiary#Treacle|Treacle]].)

- An Alp sometimes travels with **predators that hunt exhausted travelers**; was the treacle one, and are there more?
