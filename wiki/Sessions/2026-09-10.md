# Session: 2026-09-10

The fight with **[[Lore#Interrogator Units|Madasin]]** at the airship elevator goes badly. She gasses the area, **mind-controls [[Characters/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]]**, and drops three of the party. The **[[Lore#The Mechanical Slate|mechanical slate]]** pays off: [[B-Team]] reaches the ground, takes [[B-Team|Maggie]] off [[Characters/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]]'s hands, hands him a **bomb**, and teleports him back to the party with **healing needles**. Madasin sheds her armor when bloodied and becomes far deadlier. **The fight is unresolved.**

## Opening Exchange
- Madasin launches **riot control gas** to obscure the area. The party is **blinded inside the cloud**; [[Characters/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]], already **raged**, just rushes ahead.
- [[Characters/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] tries to **Banish** her — **it fails**.
- She retaliates by **attaching a mind-control device to Ogra**, turning him against Zinjaro.
- She walks into her own gas cloud and **blade dances**: severe damage to [[Characters/Villhelm Emberstoke|Villhelm]], [[Characters/Aolis|Aolis]], and [[Characters/Brambleberry|Brambleberry]], and **kills Aolis's Hound of Ill Omen**.
- [[Characters/Aolis|Aolis]] moves clear of the smoke and **shoots one of the gas canisters**, clearing part of the cloud.
- Zinjaro's pocket beeps — the **[[Lore#The Mechanical Slate|slate Melody gave him]]** displays **"place on ground."** He does.

## Maxim on the Ground
- [[Characters/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]], invisible with [[B-Team|Maggie]] since the takeoff, sees **[[B-Team]] rushing toward the ship**, panicking that it is leaving.
- He **goes visible and hands Maggie over**. In return they **give him a bomb to take to the ship**.
- The **slate then summons Maxim back to the rest of the party** aboard the airship.
- He arrives alongside **seeking needles** that dart into the party and **heal everyone to full**.

## The Tide Turns
- Ogra, mind-controlled, tries to hit and topple Zinjaro and **fails**; he then **shakes off the control and apologizes**.
- Maxim determines Madasin is **resistant to lightning** and has **no vulnerabilities**.
- Madasin uses a **combat stimulant granting Haste**, attacks Villhelm and Brambleberry, then rushes out to reach Zinjaro.
- Zinjaro takes several more hits, drinks a **superior healing potion**, and then **goes down unconscious** anyway.
- Villhelm, running on a **Haste potion**, lands a flurry of attacks.

### Madasin Bloodied
When bloodied, Madasin **sheds her armor**, revealing **bare metallic parts** underneath:
- **AC reduced by 4**
- **STR and DEX each increased by 2**
- **Unlimited uses of blade dance**
- May use **pistol and blades in a single multiattack**
- **+2 additional attacks per multiattack**

She is far deadlier in this form, but **easier to hit**.

## Endgame
- Madasin **strikes down Villhelm and tosses him aside** — two of the party are down.
- Maxim rushes in, grabs Zinjaro, and **Thunder Steps away (Quickened via Metamagic)**, then **feeds him a greater healing potion**, bringing him back.
- She turns her pistol on Maxim — most shots hit his **Mirror Images**, one lands on him.
- **Brambleberry goes down unconscious.**
- **Madasin is badly hurt.** The fight continues next session.
