# Session: 2026-09-24

Picking up from [[wiki/Sessions/2026-09-10|2026-09-10]], **[[Characters/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] cuts [[Lore#Interrogator Units|Madasin]] in half** and the fight ends. The party reaches the airship's cockpit and finds the **[[Lore#The Overseer (VI)|Overseer]]** close to death. It hands [[Characters/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] its core housing and goes unresponsive. The ship is locked on course for **[[wiki/Locations#Tower Isle|Tower Isle]]** under Odin's authority, so [[Characters/Aolis|Aolis]] teleports everyone to the **Tesselia Guild Hall**. The night ends at the bar.

*Correction to last session: B-Team did not give Maxim a bomb. The device only looked like one, and it was what fired the healing needles. The [[wiki/Sessions/2026-09-10|2026-09-10]] page has been updated.*

## The End of Madasin
- [[Characters/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] casts **Mass Cure Wounds**, bringing [[Characters/Brambleberry|Brambleberry]] and [[Characters/Villhelm Emberstoke|Villhelm]] back up. He takes a pistol shot and uses his **Hadozee Dodge** to reduce the damage.
- Madasin **blade dances** again and **drops Bramble and Villhelm a second time**.
- **Ogra slashes her in half**, ending the battle. Her **head is still moving**, but her **lower body is incapacitated**.
- Zinjaro uses **Healing Word** on Bramble and Villhelm.
- [[Characters/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] **drags her body to the elevator door to unlock it**. A **milky white fluid** drips from her. She is **still alive** and warns them off, but **lets the party leave** through the elevator.

## The Upper Level
- **Busted-up robot bodies** lie scattered around.
- There are **nice estate rooms and bathrooms**. Zinjaro asks Ogra to carry off **one of the nice rugs** for his **future beach house**. Bramble argues against stealing it, and Zinjaro takes it anyway.
- A door is **held shut from the other side**. Zinjaro asks to come in, and the **[[Lore#The Overseer (VI)|Overseer]] lets them in**.

## The Overseer's Core
- The Overseer is **badly damaged** and **can no longer move**. It asks whether the hostiles have been dealt with.
- It asks whether it **can trust Maxim**. Maxim says yes.
- It opens its **AI core housing** for Maxim to take, saying the **tools are inaccessible**. The housing is an **auxiliary Tier 4 core housing** with a **small core** inside.
- It is **clutching a ~15 lb object**: the **new core** it is meant to use once the party finds **tools to install it in a new body**. The party **takes the new core with them** but **leaves the Overseer's body** aboard the ship. (See **[[Lore#The Ancient Core Fragment|The Ancient Core Fragment]]**.)
- The Overseer then goes **unresponsive**.

## The Cockpit
- The Overseer's room turns out to be the **ship's cockpit**.
- Meanwhile Aolis searches one of the estate rooms and takes a **tablet**.
- Maxim tries the controls, realizes he is doing something wrong, and **nearly gets locked out**. He learns the ship is **returning to base**. There is **no joystick or manual control**.
- Guidance is locked behind **"permission level Odin."** The ship will reach **[[wiki/Locations#Tower Isle|Tower Isle]]** in about **1 day, 15 hours**.
- *Out of character:* the GM clarified that **this world's drow, Aolis's people, are a blend of the original drow and the Drukhari**, so players know what to expect from them. They **live beneath Tower Isle**, along with **hostile, racist insectoids** and **more robots**. It is **a bad place for the party to go**.

## Teleporting Out
Aolis casts **Teleportation Circle**. The party has these destinations:
- **[[wiki/Locations#Helines|Helines]] Guild Hall**: permanent teleportation circle
- **Tesselia Guild Hall**: permanent teleportation circle
- **Maxim's House**: permanent teleportation circle
- **"Home"**: from [[B-Team|Lavelor]] ("Lavi") to Aolis
- **"His House"**: from Lavelor to Aolis

They choose Tesselia and arrive in the **training area of the [[wiki/Locations#Adventurers Guild Hall|guild hall]]**. The airship continues toward Tower Isle without them.

## The Guild Hall
- **Maggie** ([[wiki/Characters#Maggie North|Maggie North]]; the notes call her "the innkeep") welcomes them and asks whether the rug is magical. It is **just a normal rug**. She tells them to rest while she **sends a message out**.
- Maxim **sends a message to [[B-Team|Wendy]]**. Maxim and Aolis **study the tablet**.
- Zinjaro, Villhelm, Ogra, and Bramble go drinking:
  - **Bramble** passes out after **5** drinks.
  - **Ogra** passes out at **16**.
  - **Villhelm** passes out at **21**.
  - **Zinjaro** is **cut off at 26** once the bartender notices his **Sol'Ar symbol**.
- Villhelm covers the tab. More precisely, the barkeep takes **100 gold** from him.
- The party takes a **long rest** and decides to return to the **underground facility** to speak with [[wiki/Characters#Sofia AI|Sofia AI]] and the others.
