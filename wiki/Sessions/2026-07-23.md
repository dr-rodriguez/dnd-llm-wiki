# Session: 2026-07-23

The party finishes searching the [[wiki/Quests#Current Objective: The Siege of Tesselia (April 2026)|Anti-Air gun]] church site, continuing directly from [[wiki/Sessions/2026-07-16|2026-07-16]], then evacuates ahead of an orbital bombardment.

## Parley with the Overseer
- [[Characters/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] presses the chief [[Lore#Slave Drives|Slave Drive]] on whether they are enemies of [[wiki/Characters#Odin AI|Odin]]. They won't confirm it, but state they are **not allies** — and keep insisting they are **independent of all prior factions**.
- **Odin's units are technologically superior**, so the Overseer avoids direct conflict with them. Its own units arrived through a hole in the roof.
- The party warns them that others will come once they leave.
- Maxim is shown CCTV footage of the party's insectoid friends (the [[Lore#Technocrats|Technocrats]]), but can't determine where they are.
- Asked for a designation, the machines say they don't need names, but may be referred to as **[[Lore#The Overseer (VI)|Overseer]]**.
- **[[Lore#VI vs. AI|VI vs. AI]]:** a VI (virtual intelligence) is better than a modern LLM — it can generate thoughts, but can't really operate without direction. The Overseer sits somewhere *between* VI and AI. Maxim recalls lore about work toward exactly that, but not much: it was around the time he was cryofrozen.

## The Ledger
- [[Characters/Villhelm Emberstoke|Villhelm]] finally completes his investigation of the office: records of church donations and similar filings, notices of **strange people in the congregation**, and a note by the head priest about a **large amount of funding that went missing** — he believed it funded a project he never approved and wanted to know who authorized it. One drawer is locked, and someone has tried to break it.
- [[Characters/Brambleberry|Brambleberry]] examines the lock: someone gummed it with a paper cartridge full of powder. She carefully extracts it; Maxim thinks it may be an **explosive**. She then picks the lock.
- Inside: an **envelope** of old paper currency, and a **ledger** documenting years of money siphoned from the church in ever-increasing amounts.
- Annotations in the ledger:
  - *A month ago:* second-to-final transaction — adventurers poking around the church, may have to pull out.
  - *A few days ago:* organization crumbling, returning to base, all unnecessary assets disposed of.
- Every annotation, and the ledger's end, is signed in an ancient text: **"[[Lore#The Past and Future, Running Free|The Past and Future, Running Free]]"**.

## The Obsidian Altar
- The room next over holds a **pile of dead bodies**, at least one dressed as a priest. A stairway leads to a room of broken furniture.
- Downstairs sits an **altar on a plinth of polished obsidian**. [[Characters/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] boosts [[Characters/Aolis|Aolis]] on top of it.
- Text rings the plinth in a language they can't read; **Comprehend Languages** reveals it as **Draconic** — holy scripture, but naming no specific god.
- Regrouping downstairs, Aolis asks Maxim to catch him and jumps; Maxim instead casts **Telekinesis** to guide him down gently.
- [[Characters/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]]'s religion check muddies things further: the plinth implies a **draconic cult**, but the statues are of **angels**. Possibly a minor place of worship, or one meant to serve multiple religions — common in slums and poor areas where people gather to worship whichever god they choose.

## Bombardment
- Upstairs, Brambleberry ("Raspberry") sees the Overseer and its drones head out through the roof. They recommend vacating the premises **unless she is explosion-proof**.
- Maxim runs up to ask one more question, but the Overseer is gone — only the console remains, and reading it would take time. Aolis runs up to warn him. They **Dimension Door** out as the shadow of one of Odin's ships crosses overhead.
- The party flees and watches the site bombarded. The AA emplacement was in place, but **the guns weren't built yet**, so nothing could stop it. The ship holds station about an hour. The main building's center still partly stands, but the staircases are gone.

## Return to the Facility
- It's dusk — relatively safer, with only minor patrols. The party makes it back to the underground facility to meet [[wiki/Characters#Queen Tesselia|Tesselia]], [[wiki/Characters#Angela Nevermore|Nevermore]], and [[wiki/Characters#Sofia AI|Sofia AI]].
- A meeting is already underway, but they're shown in: a **3D projection of [[wiki/Characters#Alisha Tower|Alisha Tower]]** and **a lady in a glass fish tank**. Alisha says it will take time, but they will start the journey now. The lady in the tank — **[[wiki/Characters#Lady Ellen|Lady Ellen]]** — says the **sea lanes will be protected**.
- Maxim is asked to wheel Ellen out to the main hall. She recognizes his last name and gives him a **psychic twinge**: his family has been polluting the waters, and she opposes that.
- The party debriefs Tesselia, Nevermore, and Sofia AI, describing the other group of robots and how they seemed to be a VI yet acted independently. **Sofia believes the number of Slave Drives is too high for a single VI.** Maxim gets a document on his tablet to fill out with everything he remembers about the Overseer and its drives. The party also asks for help locating their insectoid friends.

## Pastries and a Cliffhanger
- Told to rest, the party heads back to their rooms; [[Characters/Amber Lyre|Amber Lyre]] waves at Maxim on the way.
- Amber makes the rounds with pastries. Zinjaro gets mango ones, then casts **Goodberry** (bananas) to supplement her supply.
- Reaching Maxim and Aolis's room, Amber recognizes the banana Goodberries as Zinjaro's. They commiserate about being shot at and about the high tech. Maxim mentions the phrase **"Past and Future, Running Free"** — Amber finds it odd, because she found a **book** bearing it (which she used to block a laser shot). She also mentions spotting a **heavy** wielding a railgun comparable to a **Disintegrate** spell.
- As she tries to leave, both Amber and Maxim must roll **Wisdom saves**. Maxim feels intense pain, takes heavy damage, and **falls unconscious**. Amber feels the pain too, but resists it.
