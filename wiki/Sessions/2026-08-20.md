# Session: 2026-08-20

The airship boarding fight continues. The robots reveal they want prisoners, not corpses — and the fight ends with a Colossal reinforcement inbound and the party in full retreat.

## The Fight Aboard
- Combat continues from [[Sessions/2026-08-13|2026-08-13]]. [[Characters/Villhelm Emberstoke|Villhelm]] takes several hits, kept standing by [[Characters/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]]'s **Vitality of the Tree**.
- An enemy throws a **flashbang**, **stunning and blinding [[Characters/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] and [[Characters/Aolis|Aolis]]** for a turn. It nearly catches [[Characters/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] as well.
- **The party notices some robots switching from guns to manacles** — the enemy wants to **take them alive**. This is consistent with [[B-Team|Maggie]]'s capture rather than her death.
- Zinjaro casts **Conjure Celestial**, summoning the light of [[wiki/Characters#Sol'Ar|Sol'Ar]]. The light **heals allies and harms enemies**, and persists as a **moving cylinder of light**.
- The **[[Lore#The Overseer (VI)|Overseer-VI]] and its [[Lore#Slave Drives|Slave Drives]] arrive to help the party** — the first time they fight openly alongside the party rather than merely creating a distraction.
- Zinjaro is **shot by a critical hit but maintains concentration**; a **second flashbang then stuns him**, ending the Conjure Celestial cylinder.
- Aolis is hit hard and **retreats to hide under the ramp with Maxim**.

## The Consoles and the Super Heavy
- **A tank robot moves to interface with a console**, and a **scout robot does the same** at another.
- Maxim throws a **lightning-transmuted Fireball** at them, doing heavy damage.
- One of the Slave Drives **hums**, which **allows the Overseer to act** — a hint at how the Overseer's command structure works. The Overseer runs to the party and tells them **they need to run: a Super Heavy is coming**. It points out a **door leading inside** the ship.
- The tank robot **goes down — but its console interface succeeds**, summoning the **[[Lore#Super Heavy Chassis|Super Heavy Chassis]]**, a **Colossal**-sized creature.
- The party breaks for the door.

*Session ends mid-flight, to be continued.*

## Open Threads
- The party must get through the interior door ahead of the Super Heavy Chassis.
- [[B-Team|Maggie]] is still held on an upper layer of the ship, and the [[Lore#The Ancient Core Fragment|ancient core fragment]] is still unrecovered — with the Overseer's core failing within days.
