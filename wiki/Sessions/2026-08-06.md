# Session: 2026-08-06

Picking up from the cliffhanger of [[wiki/Sessions/2026-07-23|2026-07-23]]: the wave that dropped [[Characters/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] is an attack on the minds of the pod survivors, and [[wiki/Characters#Odin AI|Odin]] makes Maxim an offer.

## The Wave
- Everyone feels a wave — an uncomfortable shudder — but it doesn't harm most of the party. [[Characters/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] and [[Characters/Amber Lyre|Amber]] have an adverse reaction; [[Characters/Aolis|Aolis]] panics at the sight of it.
- Amber opens the door, steps outside, and falls on her face, gulping for air. Aolis rushes to fetch help.
- [[Characters/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] is out of spell slots but tries to feed Amber and Maxim leftover **Goodberries** (bananas). Amber tries to crawl inside; [[Characters/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] helps her. She touches her head — and now both she and Maxim are unconscious.

## Maxim in the Second Space
- In Maxim's mind, the wave arrives as **pure darkness**. He stands, but is **incorporeal, with no body**.
- He casts **Light**; it attaches to a **pedestal** he hadn't seen before. The light is diminished — only 5 feet of bright and 5 more of dim. Everything feels surreal. He can cast some spells, but only near the pedestal; too far out and everything disappears.
- After an indeterminate time, a **clear glass sphere** appears on the pedestal. He wills himself to touch it. It rises, glows milky white, and his vision shifts.
- An older human — late 50s, medium-length scraggly beard — leans on the pedestal: **"You know, this wasn't the plan."** He says he's been called a god before. This is **[[wiki/Characters#Odin AI|Odin]]**.

### Odin's Offer
- Odin asks whether Maxim has ever thought of **going back in time** — physically, perhaps with another person. Maxim says he isn't that powerful.
- Odin offers to send **Maxim and one other back in time**, in exchange for a favor:
  - Go **deep into the facility** and recover the **AI core of his wife** — [[wiki/Characters#Sofia AI|Sofia AI]].
  - Bring him the **leader of that facility**. Their state of life is *not important*.
- Maxim has no past worth returning to — all he remembers is the training facility and the cryopod.
- The sphere turns **amber gold** and Maxim feels **[[Characters/Amber Lyre|Amber]]'s presence**. Odin leaves; the emptiness returns.

## The Cognitive Retriever
- In real time, **[[Characters/Juneberry|Juneberry]]** (Ras's old character, a harengon) runs in and clamps a **tiara-like device** onto Maxim's head, wires it to a machine, and hits a button.
- Maxim regains consciousness feeling like he was **torn apart** — in a lot of pain, but alive, holding his head on the floor. Juneberry moves on to help Amber.
- **[[wiki/Characters#Sofia AI|Sofia AI]]** arrives. Amber is on her third shock and still hasn't woken.

### Sofia's Explanation
- Individuals like Maxim and Amber were **built to receive telepathic instructions**.
- The **[[wiki/Lore#First Space and Second Space|Second Space]]** is the mental space they go to for this; the **First Space** is the physical world.
- **Odin has figured out how to weaponize the Second Space** and is attempting to **rip out their minds**.
- Sofia confirms **Odin is her ex-husband**.
- The **[[wiki/Lore#Cognitive Retriever|Cognitive Retriever]]** (the tiara) does the same thing in reverse, to pull them back.
- On a final shock, **Amber regains consciousness and screams**, coughs up blood, and falls unconscious again. Zinjaro's **Channel Divinity** helps Maxim a little. June takes Amber elsewhere.

### Sofia's Warning
- Sofia asks whether Odin offered to do something with **time**. Maxim confirms it.
- She believes Odin wants to use a **[[wiki/Lore#Odin's Demiplane|Demiplane]]** to either whip his allies *back* in time or the creators *forward*.
- The "bring a friend" clause is a **sacrifice required to build the demiplane**. She warns Maxim not to believe Odin.
- Maxim counters that he can't believe her either. Sofia says it's his call, but points out **she isn't the one attacking him**. Maxim's **Insight** check on her reads only that she seems **tired**.
- The party is ushered out and finally takes its **long rest**.

## The Morning After
- The next day the party walks past **Amber**, hooked up to medical equipment. Maxim and Aolis stay in their rooms and talk it over.
- Maxim says he is **happy here, with Aolis**; he has nothing in the past to go back to and doesn't care about the power struggles. Neither of them wants power — Aolis ran away from his inheritance, and Maxim impersonated the **middle child, not the heir**. He's annoyed at being dragged into these games.
- **[[wiki/Characters#Maxwella|Maxwella]]**, Maxim's mother, rushes into the room. **Sofia sent her a message** carrying **runes of teleportation**, so she arrived quickly. She appears to know **nothing of Maxim's real nature** — she is simply a worried parent.
- **[[wiki/Characters#Queen Tesselia|Queen Tesselia]]** watches Amber through the window and says they should wait: **scans show part of her mind is missing**. She claps, and a robot trolley brings breakfast, which the group takes in Maxim and Aolis's room. They wonder whether Amber accepted a deal of her own — or refused one.

## News from Home
- Maxim and his mother talk about home. A new faction has risen: the **[[wiki/Lore#The Severance|Severance]]**, who want to **break the chains and control the floating mountain where the college is** (the [[wiki/Locations#Mountain Home Academy of Magic|Mountain Home Academy of Magic]] in [[wiki/Locations#Moren|Moren]]).
- From a bag of holding she produces a **[Wand of Wonder](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9229195-wand-of-wonder)** with **7 charges** (d100 to determine the effect) — a gift from Maxim's father.

## Milestone
- **The party levels up to 13!**
