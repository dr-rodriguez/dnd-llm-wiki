# Session: 2026-08-27

The party escapes the Super Heavy Chassis by way of a hidden elevator, pushes through a trapped hallway into the airship's older security levels, and fights through turrets and dormant robots — only to learn a *second* Super Heavy waits ahead.

## Escape and the Hidden Elevator
- Fleeing the fight from [[Sessions/2026-08-20|2026-08-20]], the **[[Lore#The Overseer (VI)|Overseer-VI]] rushes ahead faster than anyone can follow**. [[Characters/Aolis|Aolis]] keeps up with **Misty Step**, [[Characters/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] pins the **[[Lore#Super Heavy Chassis|Super Heavy Chassis]]** in place for a turn with a **Light Snare** while he runs, and [[Characters/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] **goes invisible** to avoid being targeted.
- Inside the room, the Overseer fiddles with what looks like a **medical bed** — it is actually a **secret elevator**, which it activates. The party rides up to another level of the ship.

## The Trapped Hallway
- A long hallway lined with **protrusions and trap doors**.
- Aolis steps on a trap door; a **gun appears and shoots him** — a small, **apparently non-lethal** shot.
- Zinjaro **ducks under side-mounted flame jets** by being short enough; Maxim walks past untouched while still invisible.
- [[Characters/Brambleberry|Brambleberry]] investigates the flame protrusion and, using her **psionic knack**, succeeds in **disarming it**.

## The Two Doors
- Two locked doors at the end. Zinjaro approaches one — **lights flash, then dim**; it does not open. Brambleberry cannot work out the mechanism either. The Overseer inspects it and believes **it can open the door**.
- One door leads to **machinery** by the noise — possibly a **robot fabrication room**. The other is **quiet**.
- The Overseer recommends [[Characters/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] simply **force the quiet door** with brute strength.

## The Turret Room
- Inside: **swiveling turrets** and **deactivated [[Lore#Slave Drives|Slave Drives]]**.
- Maxim explores the room while invisible.
- The Overseer **remotely disables a turret** — it **takes damage doing so**, and says it can only manage this **one more time**.
- The party moves carefully through the room, staying out of line of sight.

## Combat with the Old Guard
- The next room holds several robots. Maxim slips past them, but **[[Characters/Villhelm Emberstoke|Villhelm]] walks straight in, triggering combat**. The turrets and Slave Drives go fully active and hostile.
- Aolis is hit and answers with a **Sorcerous Burst tuned to lightning** — but it is **less effective than against the robots the party has faced before**, probably because **these are older models**.
- Ogra enters **Rage**, **picks up Aolis and throws him**, then **grabs a turret to stop it turning**.
- The Overseer **takes control of one of the Slave Drives**.
- A **modern** robot **clamps manacles on Villhelm**, restraining him: he can still move and attack, but **auto-fails anything requiring hands** (such as grabbing something).
- Zinjaro casts **Mass Healing Word**. It heals the party a little, but **does not work on the Overseer** — magical healing has no effect on the machine.
- Aolis casts **Tidal Wave**, knocking over the room's tank robot and damaging it, then summons his **Hound of Ill Omen** — which now bears **dark spots** from when he was shot in an earlier fight.
- Maxim, invisible the whole fight, reports the surviving robots **fled into another room where a second [[Lore#Super Heavy Chassis|Super Heavy Chassis]] waits**.
- Combat ends, but more is coming.

## Open Threads
- A **second Super Heavy Chassis** stands between the party and the upper levels.
- The **machinery door** (possible robot fabrication room) is still unopened — the Overseer thinks it can open it.
- The Overseer can only remotely disable **one more turret**, and it is damaged each time.
- [[B-Team|Maggie]] and the [[Lore#The Ancient Core Fragment|ancient core fragment]] are both still above, with the Overseer's core failing within days.
