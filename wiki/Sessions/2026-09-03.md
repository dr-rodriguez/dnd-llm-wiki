# Session: 2026-09-03

The party pushes through the freight airship's fabrication decks, finds **[[B-Team|Maggie]] being tortured by two interrogator androids**, and buys her freedom with **[[Characters/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]]'s memories**. Maxim escapes with Maggie by Dimension Door as the ship takes off — leaving everyone else aboard, facing an interrogator at the elevator.

## The Lower Decks
- [[Characters/Villhelm Emberstoke|Villhelm]] is still in the **manacles** from [[Sessions/2026-08-27|2026-08-27]] and cannot break them. They are **smooth like portafab, with no visible keyholes**.
- One room holds **massive arms supporting several [[Lore#Super Heavy Chassis|Super Heavy Chassis]]**, suspended and apparently deactivated.
- Another **keyless locked door**, built for robots to operate — like the others, it likely has to be forced.
- **[[Lore#Regenerator Tanks|Regenerator tanks]]** fill the next room, humanoid figures inside: some only **fragments**, others nearly complete. Maxim recognizes them. **Three** hold nearly fully healed people. He tries to speak to an **elvish** one — the response is distant and unclear, **as though brain-dead**. He fails to break the tank open.
- The **[[Lore#The Overseer (VI)|Overseer-VI]] frees Villhelm from the cuffs**; he and [[Characters/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] had been working at them for a while.
- Further rooms: a **staircase**, and a very hot chamber of **tesla-coil-type devices**. The party goes up.

## The Upper Level
- **Super Heavy Chassis in active assembly**, and a **lounge** — [[Characters/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] recognizes the lounge **from his Scrying**: Maggie is close.
- A **target-practice room** with active chassis. [[Characters/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] and others step in without being targeted; **Ogra walks into a line of sight and is hit by flames**.
- A terminal with a **palm scanner**. **[[Characters/Brambleberry|Brambleberry]] unlocks it instantly on a natural 20**, before Zinjaro can finish casting **Resilient Friendship** to help.

## Maggie and the Interrogators
- Inside, a woman is **screaming** — Maggie. Ogra charges in and **rages**.
- She is being **heavily tortured** by two female cybernetic robots, blood everywhere. One immediately **puts a gun to Maggie's head**.
- Ogra's tree roots try to hold one robot; **she breaks free**. Brambleberry **psionically teleports** closer and is simply **pushed back**.
- The two **[[Lore#Interrogator Units|interrogator units]]**: **Riley** to the south, **Madasin** to the north with the gun. They are extracting information about the **[[Tesselia Mission|Tesselia]] bunker** from Maggie.

### Maxim's Bargain
- Maxim offers to **take Maggie's place and tell them everything, "for [[wiki/Characters#Odin AI|Odin]]"**. They consider it — they are **not allowed to use the "brain drain"**.
- They offer a **circlet that scans memories**. Maxim agrees on condition that **Zinjaro may take Maggie out and heal her**.
- Zinjaro moves to Maggie and casts **Healing Word**; [[Characters/Aolis|Aolis]] uses his **Sentinel's Flask**. Madasin **releases Maggie's bonds** and the party leaves the room while the circlet works.
- Maxim gets **vertigo** from the scan, but his **Guarded Mind** feat plus his **psionic powers** let him **shield memories of [[wiki/Characters#Sofia AI|Sofia AI]]** and similar secrets.
- The interrogators **type something into a terminal** and let Maxim go.

## The Ship Takes Off
- The party regroups outside, Ogra carrying Maggie, trying to plan how to get her off the ship while still hunting the **[[Lore#The Ancient Core Fragment|sentinel core]]**.
- The **ground shakes** — the ship is lifting off. **Maxim casts Dimension Door and leaves with Maggie**, then **Invisibility** on both of them. From the ground he watches **the airship take off and leave with the rest of the party aboard**.
- Moving out, the remaining party hits **combat**: one of the chassis is active. Aolis and Brambleberry, out ahead, **Dash back** to shelter.
- Villhelm runs upstairs for the **elevator** seen earlier — destination unknown. The party needs the **cockpit** for the sentinel core and hopes the elevator leads there.
- Zinjaro uses **Divine Intervention** to place a **Death Ward** on Aolis — if he drops, he recovers at 1 HP.
- **Madasin is standing right outside the elevator.** The **Overseer-VI is gone** — nobody knows where.
- The party argues whether to keep going, and **decides not to betray their allies**. Madasin taunts them: they could have run. **Combat begins.**

## Open Threads
- **Maggie is rescued** — but she and Maxim are on the ground, invisible, while the airship flies away with everyone else.
- The party is **airborne and split**, hunting the **sentinel core** in the cockpit.
- **Madasin** blocks the elevator; **Riley** is unaccounted for.
- The **Overseer vanished** mid-escape, with its own core still failing.
- The interrogators wanted the **Tesselia bunker**, and **typed something at a terminal** after reading Maxim — his memories may already have been transmitted.
- **What the regenerator tanks are growing**, and who the three nearly-complete, seemingly brain-dead occupants are.
