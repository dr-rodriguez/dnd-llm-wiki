# Session: 2026-07-09

The party pushes into the central area at the [[wiki/Quests#Current Objective: The Siege of Tesselia (April 2026)|Anti-Air gun]] church site, continuing directly from [[wiki/Sessions/2026-06-25|2026-06-25]] after [[Characters/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] melted the door.

## Combat
- The melee fighters rush in and take cover behind fallen pillars, while the spellcasters stay outside and lob spells into the room.
  - **Half Cover:** +2 AC and DEX saving throws.
  - **Three-Quarters Cover:** +5 AC and DEX saving throws.
- [[Characters/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] summons a **Spiritual Weapon** to fight for him while he stays behind cover.
- Maxim summons the **Hunger of Hadar** to control the area.
- Enemy reinforcements begin arriving from side rooms.
- [[Characters/Villhelm Emberstoke|Villhelm]] goes down, but Zinjaro brings him back up. [[Characters/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] then takes heavy damage — the party weighs breaking out a **Mass Cure Wounds** as the session ends.
