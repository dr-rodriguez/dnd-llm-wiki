# Session: 2026-07-16

The central-area fight at the [[wiki/Quests#Current Objective: The Siege of Tesselia (April 2026)|Anti-Air gun]] church site concludes, continuing directly from [[wiki/Sessions/2026-07-09|2026-07-09]].

## Combat Conclusion
- The fight is dicey: [[Characters/Villhelm Emberstoke|Villhelm]] and [[Characters/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] are very low on health, and [[Characters/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] is out of most spell slots.
- Zinjaro's well-placed **Banishment** removes two robots from the battlefield, tipping the scales.
- The tide turns with high-level **Witch Bolts** from [[Characters/Aolis|Aolis]], a **Fireball** from [[Characters/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]], and more attacks from [[Characters/Brambleberry|Brambleberry]], Villhelm, and Ogra.
- The banished robots are expected to return, but strangely never do — an Arcana check doesn't reveal much.
- The party takes a short rest (one hour) to recover.

## The Slave Drives
- Exploring upstairs, the party finds abandoned defenses and a locked door; Bramble hears a mechanical whirr from the other side.
- Inside are strange, outdated **clockwork creatures** — the [[Lore#Slave Drives|Slave Drives]] — who pay the party no attention. The **AA gun emplacement** sits at the center of the room.

  ![[raw/images/slave-drive.png|328]]
- One works rapidly at a terminal. Zinjaro casts **Comprehend Languages** and reads fragments of a program: "function", "signify when", "use library" (he lacks Maxim's technical background to fully understand it).
- The **chief robot** notices Zinjaro and asks who the party is; when he glances at another robot, it gets shocked by lightning and groans in pain.
- The chief robot says it is building a **failsafe** to keep itself alive. It recognizes Maxim as being from the past and asks, *"Query: allegiance with entity Odin?"* Maxim denies any allegiance to [[wiki/Characters#Odin AI|Odin AI]] — *"Statement: this is good to hear."*
- The party explains they are here to defeat the bad guys and protect the city. The chief calls the others "Slave Drives" and says it will leave with them once its task is done.
- The Slave Drives do not know [[wiki/Characters#Sofia AI|Sofia AI]] — could they belong to another faction?

## The Nanotrasen Virus
- Villhelm and Brambleberry explore a side room. A trap hits Bramble with a poison gas from a canister, and she contracts a disease.
- Zinjaro can't diagnose it but casts **Lesser Restoration** to help. Her coughing stops, but she begins vomiting a tar-like substance with glittering specks — like a starry sky. Everyone backs away.
- The chief robot asks Brambleberry to submit to a medical exam, which she agrees to. Verdict: the **[[Lore#Nanotrasen Virus|Nanotrasen virus]]** ("Novid") — less effective on organic matter, but **lethal to inorganic matter** (e.g., robots).
- Villhelm spends time investigating the room; another room contains some bodies.
