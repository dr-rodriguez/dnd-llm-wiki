# Light

| Attribute | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Type | City |
| Located In | [[Locations/Drakenweld\|Drakenweld]] |
| Other Names | "The First and Last City", Austere, the Bastion |

Light is the great city of [[Locations/Drakenweld|Drakenweld]], and the big city on the party's map. It is called **"the First and Last City"** and is known by many other names, including **Austere** and **the Bastion**. [[Sessions/2025/2025-07-10|Session: 2025-07-10]]

- It was built during the **dragon wars**. [[Sessions/2025/2025-06-19|Session: 2025-06-19]]
- To reach it, take the **east fork** of the road north of the mountain pass. The west fork likely leads to the demon portal. Maggie of [[Characters/NPC/B-Team|B-Team]] told the party this. [[Sessions/2025/2025-06-19|Session: 2025-06-19]]
- B-Team went north of the fork after crossing the border, to meet someone who "may not be pleased to see them". [[Sessions/2025/2025-07-17|Session: 2025-07-17]]
- **Miss Meow**, a cat-folk baker, runs a bakery here. [[raw/DnD5e Sofia Campaign 2023 Characters_Locations - Characters.csv|Characters CSV]]
