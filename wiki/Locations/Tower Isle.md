# Tower Isle

| Attribute | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Island |
| Located In | The central (inland) sea |
| Inhabitants | [[Lore/Factions and Organizations#Technocrats\|Technocrats]] (insectoids), this world's drow (below), robots |
| Notable Residents | Nock, Screeck, Tick; [[Characters/NPC/Odin AI\|Odin AI]] (orbital station above) |

Tower Isle is a large island in the central sea, surrounded by several continents. It is home to the insectoid **[[Lore/Factions and Organizations#Technocrats|Technocrats]] of the Ministrate**. [[Sessions/2024/2024-07-11|Session: 2024-07-11]], [[Sessions/2024/2024-08-15|Session: 2024-08-15]]

## Defenses and Government
- **[[Lore/Major Projects#Project Horizon|Project Horizon]]** is located here. [[Sessions/2024/2024-08-01|Session: 2024-08-01]]
- **300mm guns** with a range of over 20 miles keep non-Technocrats away. [[Sessions/2024/2024-08-01|Session: 2024-08-01]]
- The island is split into regions, each run by an oligarchic dictator. The Technocrats act as a trading kingdom and are known as hoarders. [[Sessions/2024/2024-08-01|Session: 2024-08-01]]
- It is **shielded from astral teleportation**. The wizard Alor was steering his wandering tower toward it. [[Sessions/2024/2024-08-15|Session: 2024-08-15]]

## The Tower and the Sky Father
- Most insectoids worship the **Sky Father** atop the tower. A platform comes down from the tower and delivers gifts to the rulers. Tick belongs to a minority who believe a god gave them technology, but that the one in the tower is evil. The players think the Sky Father is [[Characters/NPC/Odin AI|Odin AI]]. [[Sessions/2026/2026-02-19|Session: 2026-02-19]]

## Threat to the Mainland
- Odin sent the Tower Isle robots to take [[Characters/NPC/Sofia AI|Sofia AI]]'s core, which led to the siege of [[Locations/Tesselia|Tesselia]]. [[Sessions/2026/2026-06-04|Session: 2026-06-04]]
- Tower Isle is the **home base of the freight airship**. On 2026-09-24 the ship was returning there under "permission level Odin", about 1 day and 15 hours out, when the party teleported off. [[Characters/NPC/Madasin|Madasin]] and the [[Characters/NPC/Overseer (VI)|Overseer]]'s body were left aboard. [[Sessions/2026/2026-09-24|Session: 2026-09-24]]
- *Out of character*, the GM said that **this world's drow** ([[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]]'s people, a blend of the original drow and the Drukhari) **live beneath Tower Isle**, along with hostile, racist insectoids and more robots. It is "a bad place for the party to go". [[Sessions/2026/2026-09-24|Session: 2026-09-24]]
