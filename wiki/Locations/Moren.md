# Moren

| Attribute | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Type | City |
| Located In | Testro Plains, in a mountain pass |
| Notable Residents | [[Characters/NPC/Maxwella\|Maxwella]] and the Arkelius house, Mr. Manager ("the Overseer"), Lindsey, Harry Teller |

Moren is a city near the center of the Testro Plains, built in a pass through the mountains. It is [[Characters/PC/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]]'s home. [[Sessions/2025/2025-02-27|Session: 2025-02-27]]

## The City
- **Mountain Home Academy of Magic:** a single mountain once stood in the middle of the pass. The wizards lifted it into the sky, and it is **chained to the ground** so it does not keep rising. Magitek gondolas run up and down, and the city is built in the crater the mountain left behind. [[Sessions/2025/2025-02-27|Session: 2025-02-27]]
- The city has **heavy smog**. It has a steampunk feel, and about 90% of the people outside the wizard school are human. [[Sessions/2025/2025-02-27|Session: 2025-02-27]], [[Sessions/2025/2025-03-27|Session: 2025-03-27]]
- **Districts:** the North and South are business and commercial (the South is lower class, the North upper class), the West is heavy industry, the East is residential, and the Center holds the wizard school and the government. [[Sessions/2025/2025-03-27|Session: 2025-03-27]]
- It is the only settlement left near [[Locations/Locations#The Wastes|The Wastes]], thanks to its wizards and the Gates. [[Sessions/2025/2025-06-12|Session: 2025-06-12]]
- From [[Locations/Helines|Helines]] it is about a week away. [[Sessions/2025/2025-02-13|Session: 2025-02-13]]

## Places
- **Arkelius estate:** home of Maxim's mother, [[Characters/NPC/Maxwella|Maxwella]], who put the party up in 2025. [[Sessions/2025/2025-04-17|Session: 2025-04-17]]
- **Singing Mandrake:** a tavern where the party went for local news and to find Maxim's informant, Harry Teller. In April 2025 [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]] was rescued from it after a run-in with the demon Byren Brimstone. [[Sessions/2025/2025-04-10|Session: 2025-04-10]], [[Sessions/2025/2025-04-17|Session: 2025-04-17]]
- **Lindsey's Laundries:** in the North district. Lindsey is a link to the Overseer. [[Sessions/2025/2025-04-24|Session: 2025-04-24]]

## Politics
- **Mr. Manager**, known as the **Overseer**, works through intermediaries. In May 2025 he held a **gala** that the party attended with Maxwella. He is not the same being as the [[Characters/NPC/Overseer (VI)|Overseer (VI)]] in [[Locations/Tesselia|Tesselia]]. [[Sessions/2025/2025-05-08|Session: 2025-05-08]], [[Sessions/2025/2025-05-15|Session: 2025-05-15]]
- In August 2026 Maxwella reported a new faction, **[[Lore/Factions and Organizations#The Severance|The Severance]]**, who want to "break the chains" and take the floating mountain where the college is. [[Sessions/2026/2026-08-06|Session: 2026-08-06]]
