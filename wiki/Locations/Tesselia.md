# Tesselia

Tesselia is the capital of the kingdom of Andore, in Northern Andore, reached by sea across [[Locations/The Divide|The Divide]]. It is a thriving trade hub, sometimes treated as a city-state and sometimes as the capital. Andore is almost always at civil war, but **Tesselia has never fallen**. [[Sessions/2023/2023-11-30|Session: 2023-11-30]]

It was the party's home base in the 2023–2024 campaign and the target of the [[Quests/Tesselia Mission|Tesselia Mission]] in 2025–2026.

## Government
- The city is ruled by the child **[[Characters/NPC/Queen Tesselia|Queen Tesselia]]**, who was 7 when the party met her. The party feared her regent, [[Characters/NPC/Ashton Whitemore|Ashton Whitemore]], would kill her before her coronation at 16. [[Sessions/2023/2023-12-07|Session: 2023-12-07]]
- The first royal family of Tesselia died out about 200 years ago, during the 3rd Alduran civil war. [[Characters/PC/Dumo|Dumo]] carries their house sigil. [[Sessions/2023/2023-12-14|Session: 2023-12-14]]
- In April 2024 the party found the Queen held in a mechanical cage. [[Characters/NPC/Angela Nevermore|Nevermore]] freed her and said Ashton must die. Afterwards the Queen was "sort of in charge", but still under age, with Nevermore as her advisor. [[Sessions/2024/2024-04-04|Session: 2024-04-04]], [[Sessions/2024/2024-04-11|Session: 2024-04-11]]
- Tesselia controls **Little Tess**, a small treaty port across The Divide in Southern Andore. [[Sessions/2024/2024-09-12|Session: 2024-09-12]]

## Under the Castle
- The control center of **[[Characters/NPC/Sofia AI|Sofia AI]]** lies beneath the castle. The Queen opened its door with the access code "TESSELIA". [[Sessions/2024/2024-04-18|Session: 2024-04-18]]
- The castle holds a **teleportation circle** that works as an anchor for the *Teleportation Circle* spell. [[Sessions/2024/2024-08-08|Session: 2024-08-08]]
- In 2026 the party sheltered in Tesselia's underground facility, about 200 feet down, during the bombardment. Merfolk had set up emergency exits. [[Sessions/2026/2026-06-11|Session: 2026-06-11]]

## Places in the City
The city has a Lower City, an Inner City, and an Upper City. See the [[Locations/Locations|Locations]] table for details.
- **[[Locations/Locations#Adventurers Guild Hall|Adventurers Guild Hall]]**: Run by [[Characters/NPC/Maggie North|Maggie North]]. The party gets quests and rests here. [[Sessions/2023/2023-11-30|Session: 2023-11-30]] In 2026 it became one of [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]]'s teleportation destinations. [[Sessions/2026/2026-09-24|Session: 2026-09-24]]
- **Magisters Magical Managerie** ("The M"): magic shop that takes item commissions.
- **Guard Station, Lower City**: contact point for the banditry quest.
- **The First Frost**: a tavern in the Inner City where bandits gather.
- **The Raven's Den**: Nevermore's gambling parlor.
- **North Shire Inn** and **Lordling's Bordello**: neighbors in the Upper City. The bordello was the scene of a murder. [[Sessions/2024/2024-04-11|Session: 2024-04-11]]
- **Mysterious Shop**: a pristine shop run by the Yellow Man.
- **Southeast docks**: where the freight airship was docked in 2026. [[Sessions/2026/2026-09-03|Session: 2026-09-03]]

## Getting There
From [[Locations/Port Altarall|Port Altarall]] it is a two-day sail across The Divide. The party made that trip with Captain Fish and arrived in August 2024. [[Sessions/2024/2024-07-11|Session: 2024-07-11]], [[Sessions/2024/2024-08-01|Session: 2024-08-01]]

## The Siege (2026)
- In January 2026 [[Characters/NPC/Lady Poomf|Lady Poomf]] in [[Locations/Helines|Helines]] sent the party to check on the Tesselia guild and protect the Queen. [[Sessions/2026/2026-01-15|Session: 2026-01-15]]
- The party reached the rocky islands outside Tesselia in April 2026 and found the city **under siege** by automatons. [[Sessions/2026/2026-04-09|Session: 2026-04-09]]
- One district was untouched by the fighting and had asphalt streets, unlike anything else in Tesselia. [[Sessions/2026/2026-04-30|Session: 2026-04-30]]
- The attack was [[Characters/NPC/Odin AI|Odin AI]]'s attempt to take Sofia's core. His airships kept up a **Saturation Bombardment**. [[Sessions/2026/2026-06-04|Session: 2026-06-04]], [[Sessions/2026/2026-06-11|Session: 2026-06-11]]
- The party cleared the Anti-Air gun sites (including a church site) from June to July 2026, and boarded the freight airship at the docks from August to September 2026. See [[Quests/Quests|Quests]]. [[Sessions/2026/2026-06-18|Session: 2026-06-18]], [[Sessions/2026/2026-07-23|Session: 2026-07-23]]
- The siege **ended on 2026-10-01** when all the enemy ships withdrew. [[Sessions/2026/2026-10-01|Session: 2026-10-01]]
