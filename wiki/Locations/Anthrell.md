# Anthrell

| Attribute | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Type | City (western capital) |
| Located In | [[Locations/Drakenweld\|Drakenweld]], in the demon-held west |
| Ruler | [[Characters/NPC/Mariquin\|Mariquin]] (AI supervisor) |

Anthrell is the **western capital of the demon lands** in [[Locations/Drakenweld|Drakenweld]]. [[Characters/NPC/Alisha Tower|Alisha Tower]] advised the party to go there to find Mayor Maxwell of [[Locations/Helines|Helines]]. [[Sessions/2025/2025-09-18|Session: 2025-09-18]] The session notes call it simply "the city"; the [[Characters|Characters]] roster places its AI, Mariquin, in Anthrell.

## Approach
- [[Characters/PC/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]]'s *Scrying* found the Mayor in a bare room of a 5–6 story building, with a **broken cityscape** outside and deep red snow clouds above. [[Sessions/2025/2025-09-18|Session: 2025-09-18]]
- Danyel told the party that an AI, unrelated to the demons, lives in the city to the west, and that it may have made a deal with them. [[Sessions/2025/2025-10-02|Session: 2025-10-02]]
- On the road the party saw demons hauling wagons of food and metal toward the city. *Sending* was intercepted. [[Sessions/2025/2025-11-13|Session: 2025-11-13]]

## The City
- From outside it looks like **ruins** with a big hole in the wall. Crossing in, the party felt a wave of tech-magic and found a **modern-day city**: roads, warm weather (about 73°F), and demons living ordinary lives, such as a pit fiend in skinny jeans trimming trees. A **magical dome** blocks *Scrying*. [[Sessions/2025/2025-11-13|Session: 2025-11-13]]
- The illusion is a giant holoprojection powered by **thermonuclear reactors**. Mariquin put the glamour up after adventurers raided the city. [[Sessions/2025/2025-12-04|Session: 2025-12-04]]
- Places the party visited: **Wendy's Coffee** (old and new currency both accepted), a hotel at **3rd and Auvery** (100g a night), microbreweries, a card-only bakery, a game shop, **Pit and Fiend, LLC**, the **Underdark Relations Bureau**, and a jeweler (nothing magical, by the governor's rule). Rooms have TVs showing news and cartoons. [[Sessions/2025/2025-11-13|Session: 2025-11-13]], [[Sessions/2025/2025-11-20|Session: 2025-11-20]]
- Drow envoys of [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]]'s sister Errera were let in by "the caretaker of this place" on condition of no violence. [[Sessions/2025/2025-11-20|Session: 2025-11-20]]

## Mariquin and the Metro Media Building
- The city's AI, **Mariquin**, manages the city, its surveillance, and a media empire from the **Metro Media Building**, the tallest building in the area. She wears a flesh body. [[Sessions/2025/2025-12-04|Session: 2025-12-04]]
- She calls [[Characters/NPC/Odin AI|Odin]] "Father" and [[Characters/NPC/Sofia AI|Sofia]] "Mother". She woke when the demons restored her power and plans to carve out land of her own. [[Sessions/2025/2025-12-04|Session: 2025-12-04]]
- Mayor Maxwell was held here under **indoctrination** and **reeducation**. Mariquin drugged the party, and they fought her and a room of demons. [[Sessions/2025/2025-12-04|Session: 2025-12-04]], [[Sessions/2025/2025-12-18|Session: 2025-12-18]]
- The party left with the Mayor while a Mariquin clone went rampant. They left the **central AI** in place, to deal with later. [[Sessions/2026/2026-01-08|Session: 2026-01-08]]
