Port Altarall is a port city in Northern Andore on the western bank of [[Locations/The Divide|The Divide]]. In April 2024 [[Characters/PC/Shar|Shar]] traced the maker's mark on a murder weapon to a smithy there. [[Sessions/2024/2024-04-11|Session: 2024-04-11]]

## The Visit (July 2024)
The party passed through on the way back to [[Locations/Tesselia|Tesselia]]. [[Sessions/2024/2024-07-11|Session: 2024-07-11]]
- **Market square:** the party watched guards shoot down and then patch up a fleeing thief, Ronny. [[Characters/PC/Soren|Soren]] bought Tinker's Tools here.
- **The Bear Den:** a food shop run by a werebear chef, "the Bear Man".
- **The Sea's Edge:** a tavern, where a gnomish woman served hot cider.
- **Salvaged-tech shop:** run by an arachnid-like person. The shopkeeper showed [[Characters/PC/Nova|Nova]] a map of several continents around a central sea, with a large island in it, home of the **[[Lore/Factions and Organizations#Technocrats|Technocrats]] of the Ministrate** (see [[Locations/Tower Isle|Tower Isle]]).
- **Captain Fish**, a merfolk captain, gave the party passage across The Divide to Tesselia, a two-day sail.

## Later Mentions
- In August 2024 the guild had a job from Port Altarall about a **wizard tower that seemed to move around**. [[Sessions/2024/2024-08-08|Session: 2024-08-08]]
