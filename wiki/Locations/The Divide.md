# The Divide

| Attribute | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Sea / waterway |
| Located In | Andore, between Northern and Southern Andore |
| Notable Places | The Drowned Nexus, [[Locations/Port Altarall\|Port Altarall]] (western bank), [[Locations/Tesselia\|Tesselia]] |

The Divide is a waterway that runs in a straight line in from the western sea and splits Andore into Northern and Southern Andore. [[Sessions/2023/2023-11-30|Session: 2023-11-30]]

## Origin
No one agrees on what made The Divide. [[Sessions/2023/2023-11-30|Session: 2023-11-30]], [[Sessions/2024/2024-01-04|Session: 2024-01-04]]
- **Religious view:** the gods made it as a punishment, and the sinners live on the other side.
- **Arcane view:** two spellcasters cast too many 9th-level spells.

It is not ancient. The very old map of Andore that [[Characters/NPC/Sofia AI|Sofia AI]] showed the party has **no Divide**, and extra land where it now runs. The Themus Geysers and half of a mountain range are also missing from that map. [[Sessions/2024/2024-04-18|Session: 2024-04-18]], [[Sessions/2024/2024-05-02|Session: 2024-05-02]]

## Crossing
- **[[Locations/Port Altarall|Port Altarall]]** sits on the western bank. [[Sessions/2024/2024-04-11|Session: 2024-04-11]]
- From Port Altarall to [[Locations/Tesselia|Tesselia]] is a **two-day sail**. At the center of The Divide is **The Drowned Nexus**, a massive whirlpool, and the islands around it hold bold pirates. The party sailed on *The Voyage* with Captain Fish. [[Sessions/2024/2024-07-11|Session: 2024-07-11]]
- From Tesselia south to **Little Tess** takes most of a day. The party made that crossing in September 2024, with [[Characters/NPC/Maggie North|Maggie North]] along. [[Sessions/2024/2024-09-12|Session: 2024-09-12]]
- Southern Andore, across The Divide from Tesselia, is known for cults. [[Sessions/2024/2024-01-04|Session: 2024-01-04]]
