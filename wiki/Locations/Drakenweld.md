# Drakenweld

| Attribute | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Region / kingdom |
| Located In | North of the Testro Plains, beyond [[Locations/Locations#The Wastes\|The Wastes]] and [[Locations/Locations#The Gates\|The Gates]] |
| Ruler | [[Characters/NPC/Alisha Tower\|Alisha Tower]] (Queen, as of October 2026) |
| Major Cities | [[Locations/Light\|Light]], [[Locations/Anthrell\|Anthrell]] |

Drakenweld is the land of **dragons and demons**, north of the Testro Plains. It lies past The Wastes and the remnants of the Great Gate. [[Sessions/2025/2025-02-27|Session: 2025-02-27]] The party travelled here on the [[Quests/Drakenweld Quest|Drakenweld Quest]].

## Land and Climate
- A pass through the Drakenweld mountains leads in from The Wastes. [[Sessions/2025/2025-06-12|Session: 2025-06-12]]
- It is very cold and snowy, with rocky soil. [[Sessions/2025/2025-06-19|Session: 2025-06-19]] The cold starts abruptly at the border, "almost like we crossed some line". [[Sessions/2025/2025-07-17|Session: 2025-07-17]]
- The growing season is only about two months long. [[Sessions/2025/2025-07-10|Session: 2025-07-10]]
- North of the pass the road forks. The **east fork** leads to [[Locations/Light|Light]], and the **west fork** likely leads to the demon portal. [[Sessions/2025/2025-06-19|Session: 2025-06-19]]
- Drakenweld was once said to have rivers of magic. The **Godsway**, waterways beneath the tundra, is where the veil between the realms of mortals and gods is thinnest. [[Sessions/2025/2025-08-28|Session: 2025-08-28]]
- Other places: the Spider Mountains (where dragons were last seen), an abandoned village in the corrupted lands, and The Fork, a small waystation with an inn. See [[Locations/Locations|Locations]].

## People and Rule
- The **Tower** family are the high-lords, in effect the emperors, of Drakenweld. See [[Characters/NPC/Alisha Tower|Alisha Tower]].
- The border is a keep with security lines. Guards check for anything demonic and look at guild credentials. Some soldiers bear the sigil of **Luna**. [[Characters/NPC/Lady Poomf|Lady Poomf]] had sent word ahead, so the party was expected. [[Sessions/2025/2025-07-17|Session: 2025-07-17]]
- There is an underground trade that smuggles people **out** of Drakenweld. [[Sessions/2025/2025-07-17|Session: 2025-07-17]]
- Its first currency was the **dracoshilling**. The diamond dracoshilling is the largest coin. [[Sessions/2025/2025-07-03|Session: 2025-07-03]]
- [[Characters/PC/Wobbles Wibbbles|Wobbles]] is from Drakenweld, and Lady Novara Kavash, the Arkelius family's ore contact, is a Drakenweld noble. [[Sessions/2025/2025-03-06|Session: 2025-03-06]], [[Sessions/2025/2025-05-15|Session: 2025-05-15]]

## The Demon War
- Drakenweld is at war with demons, so it has little infrastructure and everything is built to be defended. [[Sessions/2025/2025-05-08|Session: 2025-05-08]]
- The western lands are demon-held. Their capital is [[Locations/Anthrell|Anthrell]]. [[Sessions/2025/2025-09-18|Session: 2025-09-18]]
- Queen Elizabeth Tower **summoned a demon** of her own accord and tried to hand the kingdom to it. She died at her daughter's hand. [[Sessions/2025/2025-09-18|Session: 2025-09-18]], [[Sessions/2025/2025-12-04|Session: 2025-12-04]]
- By October 2026 Alisha was **Queen of Drakenweld**, with a **new court of nobles**. [[Sessions/2026/2026-10-01|Session: 2026-10-01]]
