# Helines

| Attribute | Detail |
| --- | --- |

| Located In | Trader's Peninsula, Testro Plains |

Helines is the "Gateway to the Testro Plains", a city on the Trader's Peninsula. [[Sessions/2025/2025-01-23|Session: 2025-01-23]], [[Sessions/2025/2025-02-27|Session: 2025-02-27]]

It was the party's starting point in 2025. Four of them ([[Characters/PC/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]], [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]], [[Characters/PC/Brambleberry|Brambleberry]], and [[Characters/PC/Wobbles Wibbbles|Wobbles]]) arrived by boat. [[Sessions/2025/2025-01-30|Session: 2025-01-30]]

## Adventurers Guild
- The guild is led by **[[Characters/NPC/Lady Poomf|Lady Poomf]]**. Most of the party are members; Maxim and Thadius are affiliates. [[Sessions/2025/2025-01-30|Session: 2025-01-30]]
- Lady Poomf gave out both the [[Quests/Drakenweld Quest|Drakenweld Quest]] and the [[Quests/Tesselia Mission|Tesselia Mission]] here. [[Sessions/2026/2026-01-15|Session: 2026-01-15]]
- The guild hall has a **permanent teleportation circle**. It is one of Aolis's known destinations. [[Sessions/2026/2026-09-24|Session: 2026-09-24]]

## The Missing Mayor
- **Mayor Maxwell** went north to [[Locations/Drakenweld|Drakenweld]] and did not come back. His brother **Lucious** is acting mayor. [[Sessions/2025/2025-01-23|Session: 2025-01-23]], [[Sessions/2025/2025-02-13|Session: 2025-02-13]]
- The party found Maxwell in [[Locations/Anthrell|Anthrell]], and Aolis teleported them back to Helines with the Mayor in tow. His body was saved, but his mind may be broken or replaced. [[Sessions/2026/2026-01-08|Session: 2026-01-08]]

## Distances
From Helines, Drakenweld is about two weeks away and [[Locations/Moren|Moren]] about one week. [[Sessions/2025/2025-02-13|Session: 2025-02-13]]
