# Lore and Campaign Milestones

## Ancient History
* **The First**: A race of humans and human-esque individuals who inhabited the planet long ago.
* **The Anwey (or Anway)**: The ancient civilization(s) that built many of the structures and AI systems being discovered. They were highly advanced, utilizing nuclear weapons and advanced robotics. Some modern individuals (like [[wiki/Characters#Maximus Arkelius|Maximus]] and [[wiki/Characters#Margaret/Maggie|Margaret]]) are revealed to be "Anwey" or related to them (clones/spies).
* **The Asana**: Humanoids who built the [[wiki/Characters#Nova|Nova]] (Defense Unit 33-Alpha). They disappeared in a cataclysm.
* **Tectonic Instability**: The planet experiences extreme tectonic activity, shifting 3x faster than other celestial bodies. Eventually, a complete tectonic re-shuffle occurs, turning everything to magma.

## Major Projects
* **Project Molehill**: A plan to build underground facilities to ride out the tectonic re-shuffle.
* **Project Horizon**: An alternative to Project Molehill; the goal was to leave the planet for space. Located on Tower Isle.

## Deities and Religions
* **Bahamut**: The Platinum Dragon, god of justice and metallic dragons. Followed by the Regent ([[wiki/Characters#Ashton Whitemore|Ashton Whitemore]], though his true intentions are suspect).
* **Tiamat**: The Dragon Queen, goddess of chromatic dragons.
* **Ruktar**: Evil dwarven god, known as "The Betrayer." He split from his brother and is associated with dragon cults.
* **Lady Nora**: [[wiki/Characters#Lady Nora|Lady Nora]], Goddess of trickery and the night.
* **Sol'Ar**: [[wiki/Characters#Sol'Ar|Sol'Ar]], God of revelry and the sun.
* **Natu'ra**: [[wiki/Characters#Natu'ra|Natu'ra]], Goddess of nature.
* **Luna**: [[wiki/Characters#Luna|Luna]], Childer god of the Moon. Lore reveals it is actually a full god with a **dual aspect** (the child aspect and a more mature/vengeful aspect).
* **Multi-Faith Shrines**: Small places of worship that serve several religions at once, common in slums and poor areas where people gather to worship whichever god they choose. The church site near Tesselia appears to be one: its basement holds an altar on a **polished obsidian plinth** ringed with **Draconic** holy scripture naming no specific god, while the statues above are of **angels** — a draconic cult and angelic iconography sharing one building.
* **The Net**: A wall or barrier that separates the world from the "Planar" realm.
* **Outer Planes**: A realm whose study is considered illegal. Symbols related to the Outer Planes have been found in a mysterious shop in Tesselia, associated with knowledge and a "Yellow Man." Recent discoveries in a tower revealed **dual anchor points**—conjuration circles that exist simultaneously in the Material and one of the Outer Planes.
    * **Green Stone**: A stone discovered in May 2026 that is very hot to the touch but non-radioactive. It is believed to be a "warming stone" from the Outer Planes that has absorbed strange energy.
    * **Outer Plane Flora**: Bizarre plants, such as fluorescent blue and purple vegetation, have been seen in environments influenced by the Outer Planes.
* **Astral Plane**: A plane of existence described as a star ocean with floating fish. [[Characters/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] briefly visited this plane during a wild magic surge.
* **True Sight**: A magical sense that allows one to see creatures and objects normally hidden or existing on overlapping planes.
* **Yellow Madness**: A phenomenon or charm effect caused by a mysterious yellow book. It causes an intense compulsion to possess the book and can lead to madness, evidenced by ramblings in yellow ink. In May 2026, the book spoke to [[Characters/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]], displaying phrases such as "**Ad Astra**" (To the stars) and "**Mors Imperii**" (Death of the empire).
* **The Prophecy**: A figure in a yellow cloak delivered a prophecy in May 2026: *"In morte imperii deus noster de caelo descendet"* (In the death of the empire, our god will descend from space).

## Locations of Interest
* **Tower Isle**: The location of [[wiki/Lore#Project Horizon|Project Horizon]], currently inhabited by "[[wiki/Lore#Technocrats|Technocrats]]" (an insectoid race). Protected by 300mm guns. In September 2026 [[wiki/Characters/Aolis|Aolis]] revealed that the **original drow and the Drukhari**, the branch of drow he comes from, **live beneath Tower Isle**, along with **hostile, racist insectoids** and **more robots**. It is the **home base of the freight airship**, which Odin's guidance returned there. [[raw/2026/2026-09-24.md|Source]]
* **The Mountain**: A mysterious location or phenomenon mentioned by [[wiki/Characters#Queen Tesselia|Queen Tesselia]] and others. It may be related to the tectonic activity, though [[wiki/Characters#Sofia AI|Sofia AI]] lacks sufficient data. Some believe it is a destination or a source of power.
* **Libra Sands**: A region where artificers may be trying to build airships.

## Technology
* **AI Systems**: 
    * **Sofia AI**: [[wiki/Characters#Sofia AI|Sofia AI]], top-level AI controlling a continent (Andore). She is Odin's counterpart, having headed the facilities project to protect humanity to escape to space. Currently shackled and being cyberattacked by Odin. In August 2026 she confirmed that **Odin is her ex-husband**, and that his goal is her **AI core**.
    * **Athena AI**: [[wiki/Characters#Athena AI|Athena AI]], mid-level AI, experienced rampancy.
    * **Odin AI**, **Grecko/Vlad AI**: [[wiki/Characters#Odin AI|Odin AI]], [[wiki/Characters#Grecko/Vlad AI|Grecko/Vlad AI]]: other known AI systems. Odin headed [[wiki/Lore#Project Horizon|Project Horizon]] and is currently trying to harvest Sofia's AI Core. He appears in the [[wiki/Lore#First Space and Second Space|Second Space]] as an **older human in his late 50s with a scraggly beard**, says he has "been called a god before," and is **Sofia's ex-husband**. See [[wiki/Lore#Odin's Demiplane|Odin's Demiplane]].
    * **Nova (Defense Unit 33-Alpha)**: A lower-level AI/Warforged unit designed for individual facilities.
* **Slave Drives**: Outdated, clockwork-like robots discovered in July 2026 at the Anti-Air gun emplacement in the church site near Tesselia. Their chief robot commands them (disciplining one with a lightning shock) and was observed programming a "failsafe" at a terminal to keep itself alive, planning to leave with the others once the task is done. Notably, the chief recognized [[wiki/Characters#Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] as being from the past and probed his allegiance to [[wiki/Characters#Odin AI|Odin AI]] (relieved when Maxim denied it), yet the Slave Drives do not know [[wiki/Characters#Sofia AI|Sofia AI]] — suggesting they may belong to another faction entirely. In July 2026 the chief gave its designation as the **[[wiki/Lore#The Overseer (VI)|Overseer]]** and confirmed the group's independence from all prior factions. In August 2026 the drives fought alongside the party aboard the freight airship, and one was observed **humming to enable the Overseer's own action**. On 2026-08-27 the party found **deactivated Slave Drives** standing dormant in a turret room on an upper level of the ship — they **reactivated hostile** once combat began, and the Overseer was able to **seize control of one of them**. These older units proved **notably more resistant to lightning damage** than the modern robots the party had been fighting.
* **The Overseer (VI)**: The designation offered by the chief [[wiki/Lore#Slave Drives|Slave Drive]] in July 2026 — the machines claim they don't need names, but may be called **Overseer**. (Not to be confused with **Mr. Manager**, the "Overseer" of [[wiki/Locations#Moren|Moren]].) The Overseer insists it is **independent of all prior factions**, and while it won't confirm being an enemy of [[wiki/Characters#Odin AI|Odin]], it states it is not an ally. **Odin's units are technologically superior** to the Overseer's — which is why the Overseer avoids direct conflict with them. The Overseer's own units entered the church site through a hole in the roof. It possesses CCTV footage of the [[wiki/Lore#Technocrats|Technocrats]]. It departed through the roof shortly before Odin's ship bombarded the site, warning the party to leave "unless explosion-proof." [[wiki/Characters#Sofia AI|Sofia AI]] believes the sheer number of Slave Drives under it is **too high for a single VI**. In August 2026 it reappeared cloaked at the Tesselia docks and revealed that **its own core is failing — it will die in less than 3 days** without a replacement, even a corrupted one. It brokered a deal with the party: it draws off the droid guard while they recover an **[[wiki/Lore#The Ancient Core Fragment|ancient core fragment]]** from the freight airship. It expressed genuine confusion that the party would risk their lives for a single person. On 2026-08-20 the Overseer and its Slave Drives **entered the airship fight openly on the party's side** — the first observed direct cooperation. A Slave Drive was seen **humming**, which **enabled the Overseer to act**, hinting at how its command structure is powered or authorized. It warned the party to run from the incoming **[[wiki/Lore#Super Heavy Chassis|Super Heavy Chassis]]** and pointed them to an interior door. On 2026-08-27 it **outran the entire party**, revealed a **medical bed that was actually a hidden elevator**, and carried them to an upper level. It demonstrated the ability to **remotely disable turrets** — but is **damaged each time it does so** and could manage only one more — and to **take control of dormant Slave Drives**. It believes it can open a **locked door** on the upper level that neither Zinjaro nor [[Characters/Brambleberry|Brambleberry]] could. Crucially, **magical healing does not work on it**: Zinjaro's **Mass Healing Word** healed the party but had no effect on the Overseer. On 2026-09-03 it **freed [[Characters/Villhelm Emberstoke|Villhelm]] from the keyless manacles** that neither he nor [[Characters/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] could break — then **vanished without explanation** as the ship took off, leaving the party without their guide and with its own core still failing. [[raw/2026/2026-09-03.md|Source]] On 2026-09-24 the party found it in the **ship's cockpit**, holding the door shut. It was **badly damaged and immobile**. After confirming it **could trust [[wiki/Characters#Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]]**, it opened its **auxiliary Tier 4 core housing** (holding a **small core**) for him to take, since the proper tools were inaccessible. It was clutching a **~15 lb new core** meant for it once **tools are found to install it in a new body**. It then went **unresponsive**. [[raw/2026/2026-09-24.md|Source]]
* **The Freight Airship (Interior)**: The massive freight airship docked at the southeast [[wiki/Locations#Tesselia|Tesselia]] docks, boarded by the party in August 2026. Below deck, a room containing what appears to be a **medical bed** actually conceals a **secret elevator** to the upper levels — the [[wiki/Lore#The Overseer (VI)|Overseer]] knew how to activate it. The level above is defended by a **trapped hallway**: floor **trap doors** that deploy guns firing small, apparently **non-lethal** rounds, and wall **protrusions** that jet flame (low enough to duck under, and disarmable). Beyond it are **two locked doors** — one loud with **machinery**, possibly a **robot fabrication room**, and one quiet, which opens onto a chamber of **swiveling turrets** and dormant **[[wiki/Lore#Slave Drives|Slave Drives]]**. The ship's defenders on this level are **older models** than the robots met on deck. Deeper on the lower deck are a **chassis suspension bay** (massive arms holding deactivated [[wiki/Lore#Super Heavy Chassis|Super Heavy Chassis]]), a room of **[[wiki/Lore#Regenerator Tanks|regenerator tanks]]**, a staircase, and a very hot chamber of **tesla-coil-type devices**. The level above holds **Super Heavy Chassis in active assembly**, a **lounge** (the room [[Characters/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]]'s Scrying showed), a **target-practice room** with live chassis that fire on anything entering their line of sight, and a **palm-scanner terminal** guarding the interrogation room. Several of the ship's doors are **keyless and built for robots to operate**, and must be forced. On 2026-09-03 the ship **took off from the [[wiki/Locations#Tesselia|Tesselia]] docks** with the party aboard. The top level holds **estate rooms and bathrooms** with fine rugs, and the **cockpit**. The cockpit has **no manual controls**, and guidance is locked behind **"permission level Odin"**. On 2026-09-24 the ship was **returning to base at [[wiki/Lore#Tower Isle|Tower Isle]]**, about 1 day and 15 hours out, when the party teleported off. [[raw/2026/2026-08-27.md|Source]], [[raw/2026/2026-09-03.md|Source]], [[raw/2026/2026-09-24.md|Source]]
* **Regenerator Tanks**: Tanks aboard the [[wiki/Lore#The Freight Airship (Interior)|freight airship]] holding humanoid figures suspended in fluid — some only **fragments**, others nearly complete. [[wiki/Characters#Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] recognized the technology on 2026-09-03. **Three** held nearly fully healed people; one was **elvish**, and when Maxim tried to speak to it the reply was faint and unclear, **as though the occupant were brain-dead**. The tanks resisted being broken open. Their purpose — healing, cloning, or growing bodies — is unknown. [[raw/2026/2026-09-03.md|Source]]
* **Interrogator Units**: Two female cybernetic robots aboard the [[wiki/Lore#The Freight Airship (Interior)|freight airship]], **Riley** (south) and **Madasin** (north), who held and **heavily tortured** [[B-Team|Maggie]] to extract the location of the **[[Tesselia Mission|Tesselia]] bunker** (2026-09-03). They are strong enough to break out of Ogra's entangling roots and to shove a psionically teleporting [[Characters/Brambleberry|Brambleberry]] back bodily. They negotiate: offered Maxim's cooperation in Maggie's place, they **considered and accepted the trade**, releasing Maggie for a **memory-scanning circlet** worn voluntarily. They are **forbidden from using the "brain drain"** — implying a harsher extraction method exists and someone above them restricts it. After the scan they **entered something at a terminal** and let Maxim go. Madasin later ambushed the party at the ship's elevator. [[raw/2026/2026-09-03.md|Source]]
  * **Madasin in combat (2026-09-10):** She opens with **riot control gas canisters** that heavily obscure and blind the area, then fights inside her own cloud using a **blade dance** multiattack. She carries a **mind-control device** she can attach to a creature in melee — it turned a raging [[Characters/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] against [[Characters/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] for a round before he shook it off. She **resisted Banishment**, is **resistant to lightning**, and has **no known vulnerabilities**. She can use a **combat stimulant that grants Haste**. When **bloodied she sheds her armor**, revealing bare metallic parts: **AC -4, STR and DEX +2 each, unlimited blade dance, pistol and blades usable in a single multiattack, and +2 attacks per multiattack** — far deadlier, but much easier to hit. The gas canisters can be **shot to disperse the cloud**. [[raw/2026/2026-09-10.md|Source]]
  * **Madasin defeated (2026-09-24):** After one more blade dance, [[Characters/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] **cut her in half**. Her head kept moving, but her lower body was incapacitated. She **leaks a milky white fluid**. Her body was used to **unlock the elevator door**, and she warned the party but **let them leave**. She was **still alive** aboard the ship when it headed for Tower Isle. [[raw/2026/2026-09-24.md|Source]]
* **The Ancient Core Fragment**: An ancient core fragment held in the upper layers of a freight airship docked at [[wiki/Locations#Tesselia|Tesselia]] (August 2026). It is stored in a **specialized compartment case**, heavy enough that only [[Characters/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] is judged large enough to carry it. According to the [[wiki/Lore#The Overseer (VI)|Overseer]], the fragment is **outside [[wiki/Characters#Odin AI|Odin]]'s reach** and was being sent to the **island** for study. The Overseer wants it to replace its own failing core. The party refers to it as the **sentinel core** and believes it is held in the ship's **cockpit** (2026-09-03). [[raw/2026/2026-09-03.md|Source]] On 2026-09-24 the Overseer was found in the cockpit **clutching a ~15 lb new core**, the replacement it means to use once it can be **installed in a new body**. [[raw/2026/2026-09-24.md|Source]]
* **The Mechanical Slate**: A device given to [[Characters/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] by **Melody** of [[B-Team]] in August 2026 — a slate with a button and a screen. **Two clicks summons B-Team**; a confirmation message appears after the first click. The number of uses is unknown; it is intended as a last resort. **Used 2026-09-10:** mid-fight it **beeped on its own and displayed "place on ground."** Once placed, it **teleported [[Characters/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] from the ground back to the party aboard the airship** and delivered **seeking needles that healed the whole party to full**. It is therefore a **two-way link to B-Team**, not merely a summon — they can reach through it with transport and healing. **Remaining charges still unknown.** [[raw/2026/2026-09-10.md|Source]]
* **VI vs. AI**: A **VI (virtual intelligence)** is better than a modern LLM — it can generate thoughts, but cannot really operate without direction. An **AI (artificial intelligence)** is fully autonomous. The [[wiki/Lore#The Overseer (VI)|Overseer]] sits somewhere *between* the two. [[wiki/Characters#Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] recalls research aimed at exactly this, dating to roughly the time he was cryofrozen.
* **The Past and Future, Running Free**: An ancient-script phrase signing every annotation in a ledger found in the church office in July 2026, which documented years of escalating embezzlement from the church. Its annotations note that "adventurers poking around" forced a pull-out, and that the organization was "crumbling," returning to base and disposing of all unnecessary assets. [[wiki/Characters/Amber Lyre|Amber Lyre]] separately found a **book** bearing the same phrase, which she used to block a laser shot — implying an organization or creed larger than one embezzlement scheme.
* **First Space and Second Space**: The **First Space** is the physical world. The **Second Space** is a mental space that certain engineered individuals — the pod survivors such as [[wiki/Characters#Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] and [[wiki/Characters/Amber Lyre|Amber Lyre]] — were **built to enter in order to receive telepathic instructions**. To [[wiki/Characters#Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] it presents as pure darkness in which he is incorporeal, with a single **pedestal** as its only feature; light is diminished there (5 ft bright, 5 ft dim) and spells only function close to the pedestal. In August 2026 [[wiki/Characters#Sofia AI|Sofia AI]] revealed that **[[wiki/Characters#Odin AI|Odin]] has weaponized the Second Space** and is using it to try to **rip out the minds** of those who can enter it — an attack felt by the whole party as a shuddering "wave," but only harmful to Maxim and Amber.
* **Cognitive Retriever**: A **tiara-like device** wired to a machine, used at the Tesselia facility in August 2026 to pull minds back from the [[wiki/Lore#First Space and Second Space|Second Space]]. It works by doing what [[wiki/Characters#Odin AI|Odin]]'s attack does, but **in reverse**. [[wiki/Characters/Juneberry|Juneberry]] applied it to [[wiki/Characters#Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]], who woke feeling torn apart but alive. On [[wiki/Characters/Amber Lyre|Amber Lyre]] it took **three shocks** and only half worked — she woke screaming, coughed up blood, and fell unconscious again; later scans showed **part of her mind is missing**.
* **Odin's Demiplane**: [[wiki/Characters#Sofia AI|Sofia AI]]'s theory for what [[wiki/Characters#Odin AI|Odin]] is really building. In August 2026 Odin offered to send [[wiki/Characters#Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] **and one other person back in time** in exchange for recovering **Sofia's AI core** and delivering the **leader of the Tesselia facility** (alive or dead). Sofia believes the mechanism is a **demiplane** intended to whip **his allies back in time** or **the creators forward**, and that the "bring a friend" clause is really a **sacrifice needed to build it**. She warns Maxim not to believe Odin; Maxim notes he has no more reason to believe her, and she concedes the point but observes that **she is not the one attacking him**.
* **Nanotrasen Virus**: A virus (nicknamed "Novid") delivered via gas canister trap, encountered in July 2026. It is only mildly effective on organic matter — [[wiki/Characters/Brambleberry|Brambleberry]] contracted it and vomited a tar-like substance with glittering, starry-sky specks — but it is **lethal to inorganic matter** such as robots.

## Factions and Organizations
* **The Severance**: A new faction back home in [[wiki/Locations#Moren|Moren]], reported by [[wiki/Characters#Maxwella|Maxwella]] in August 2026. They want to **"break the chains"** and take control of the **floating mountain where the college is** — the [[wiki/Locations#Mountain Home Academy of Magic|Mountain Home Academy of Magic]].
* **The Past and Future, Running Free**: See the entry under [[wiki/Lore#Technology|Technology]] — an organization or creed signing an embezzlement ledger recovered near Tesselia, also found on a book carried by [[wiki/Characters/Amber Lyre|Amber Lyre]].
* **The Overseer (VI)**: See [[wiki/Lore#The Overseer (VI)|The Overseer]] — a body of [[wiki/Lore#Slave Drives|Slave Drives]] claiming independence from all prior factions.

## Bestiary
* **Flumph**: Docile, jellyfish-like aberrations from the Outer Planes. They are typically room-temperature jelly with a mucus coating and have multiple eye stalks. They are known to protect jewels and communicate through bioluminescence or touch.
* **Sticky Slime**: A sewer-dwelling creature that dissolves organic matter (like pencils) on contact.
* **Goon Balloon**: Cactus-eyed, chaotic evil creatures from other planes.
* **Grell**: Aberrations resembling floating brains with bird-like beaks and numerous tentacles.
* **Neh-thalggu (Brain Collector)**: Crab-like aberrations that collect brains to increase their magical power. They possess a lethal "Extract Brain" ability.
* **Eye Monger**: A one-eyed stone aberration.
* **Cranium Rats**: Rats with exposed, glowing brains that gain telepathic abilities and increased intelligence when in a swarm.
* **Death Kiss**: A beholder-kin with blood-draining tentacles instead of eye stalks.
* **Yellow Cloak (Figure)**: A mysterious entity that speaks with three discordant voices. It delivered a prophecy regarding the death of the empire and a descending god before transforming into a tentacled creature.
* **Super Heavy Chassis**: A **Colossal**-sized robotic war machine of the invading army. In August 2026, a tank robot aboard the freight airship at the [[wiki/Locations#Tesselia|Tesselia]] docks died interfacing with a console but succeeded in **summoning one**; the [[wiki/Lore#The Overseer (VI)|Overseer]] urged the party to flee rather than face it. The party escaped it on 2026-08-27 via a hidden elevator, [[Characters/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] buying time with a **Light Snare** that pinned it for a turn. Later that same session, **a second Super Heavy Chassis** was spotted on the upper level, in the room the surviving robots retreated into. On 2026-09-03 the party found the ship is **building them**: a bay of **massive suspension arms holding deactivated chassis**, an **active assembly** room a level up, and a **target-practice room** where live chassis fire on anything crossing their line of sight ([[Characters/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] was burned by flames doing exactly that). [[raw/2026/2026-08-20.md|Source]], [[raw/2026/2026-08-27.md|Source]], [[raw/2026/2026-09-03.md|Source]]
* **Blue Slaad**: Large, toad-like aberrations from the Outer Planes. They are formidable combatants and can be summoned by powerful entities like the [[Lore#Scion in Yellow|Scion in Yellow]].
