# The Invention of Christmas

In session [[Sessions/2024/2024-08-22|2024-08-22]], the party introduced the traditions of Christmas to the town of **Wheat Burrows** during the local holiday of **Cherish Day**.

## The Transformation of Cherish Day
While Cherish Day was already an established holiday, the party decided to enhance the festivities using stories from [[Characters/PC/Nova|Nova]]'s background regarding "Santa Claus."

### Roles in the First "Christmas"
*   **Storyteller:** [[Characters/PC/Nova|Nova]], with assistance from [[Characters/PC/Shar|Shar]], explained the legends of Santa to the townspeople.
*   **Helper Elves:** [[Characters/PC/Soten|Soten]] and [[Characters/PC/Soren|Soren]] were dressed as elves by [[Characters/PC/Tririn Bronzepride|Tririn]].
*   **The Reindeer:** [[Characters/PC/Juneberry|Juneberry]] used polymorph to transform into a reindeer.
*   **The Sleigh:** **Soren** provided a *Floating Disk* to serve as a magical sleigh for the gift-giving procession.

The party paraded through Wheat Burrows on the floating disk, tossing presents to the citizens, an event so successful that the town will speak about it for years.
