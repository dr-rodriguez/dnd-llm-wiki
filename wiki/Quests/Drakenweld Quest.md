# The Drakenweld Quest

The journey north from **[[Locations/Helines|Helines]]** to **[[Locations/Drakenweld|Drakenweld]]** is a major campaign arc that began in mid-2025.

## Origins and Quest Giver
The quest was officially assigned by **[[Characters/NPC/Lady Poomf|Lady Poomf]]**, the bird-like Guild Leader of the Adventurer's Guild in Helines. In February 2025 she told the party that **[[Characters#Mayor Maxwell|Mayor Maxwell]]** had not returned from his journey north. In January 2026, after they brought him back, she briefed them on the ancient technology carried by the "Island Dwellers" **Nock** and **Screeck**.

## Primary Objectives
1. **Locate Mayor Maxwell:** The Mayor of Helines traveled north to Drakenweld and failed to return. His brother, **Lucious**, has been serving as acting mayor in his absence.
2. **Investigate Ancient Technology:** The party was tasked with investigating the origins and status of ancient devices and the **Net** (a planar wall) in the Drakenweld region.
3. **The Demon Threat:** Following the opening of a portal in the Drakenweld mountains, demons have begun to infest the area, complicating an existing treaty with the local dragons.

## Timeline
*   **January–February 2025:** The party joins the Helines guild under *Lady Poomf*, who reports that Mayor Maxwell has not returned from the north ([[Sessions/2025/2025-01-30|Source]], [[Sessions/2025/2025-02-13|Source]]).
*   **June 2025:** The party officially sets off north, passing through the **[[Locations/Locations#The Gates|The Gates]]** into the **[[Locations/Locations#The Wastes|The Wastes]]**.
*   **October 2025:** The "Drakenweld Excursion" phase begins in earnest.
*   **January 2026:** The party rescues Mayor Maxwell from [[Locations/Anthrell|Anthrell]] and returns to Helines; Lady Poomf then sends them on to Tesselia ([[Sessions/2026/2026-01-08|Source]], [[Sessions/2026/2026-01-15|Source]]).
*   **February 2026:** The party learns of the "rot" afflicting the region ([[Sessions/2026/2026-02-12|Source]], [[Sessions/2026/2026-02-26|Source]]).
*   **April 2026:** The party defeats a radioactive dragon in a volcanic caldera, then continues on to Tesselia ([[Sessions/2026/2026-04-03|Source]]).

## Key Locations
*   **[[Locations/Helines|Helines]]:** The starting city and gateway to the north.
*   **[[Locations/Locations#The Wastes|The Wastes]]:** A depopulated region between Helines and the mountains, filled with ruins.
*   **[[Locations/Drakenweld|Drakenweld]]:** The northern destination, characterized by extreme cold and volcanic activity.
