## Backstory / Events
Maximus Arkelius, also known as **Maxim**, is an **Ancient Anwey** from a distant, highly advanced civilization. He was originally created by the Anwey as a spy, but after assuming his current form—which resembled a specific "baseform"—he grew accustomed to life with a family and eventually abandoned his original mission. He spent a significant amount of time unconscious in another plane while ages passed in the material world. He has admitted that "Maximus Arkelius" is not his real name, and his family was historically involved in manufacturing and heavy machinery.

Maxim is a **Changeling**, a fact he was initially surprised to learn meant he was considered fey. As an **Aberrant Sorcerer**, he possesses knowledge of advanced and forgotten technologies, including radiation and nuclear weaponry, and can read ancient Anwey script. He often travels with a homunculus servant and a kobold companion named [[Characters/PC/Wobbles Wibbbles|Wobbles]].

During his adventures, Maxim formed a close bond with [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]], with whom he shared damage through a pair of bonding rings. This bond was eventually broken in April 2026 during a near-death encounter with a dragon to prevent Aolis from taking further shared damage. He has been instrumental in navigating ancient Anwey structures, using his magic to scout invisibly, summon otherworldly help, and identify hazardous radioactive materials. In April 2026, during a fight against automatons, Maxim was reunited with another changeling survivor from the "pods" named **[[Characters/NPC/Amber Lyre|Amber Lyre]]**.
- **May 14, 2026:** Identified several strange creatures including [[Lore/Bestiary#Flumph|Flumphs]] and [[Lore/Bestiary#Goon Balloon|Goon Balloons]]. During a battle in a tower, his magic triggered a **Wild Magic Surge**, granting him the potential for reincarnation. He also briefly vanished to the **[[Lore/Deities and Religions#Astral Plane|Astral Plane]]**, where he witnessed floating fish in a star ocean.
- **May 21, 2026:** Cast **Wall of Fire** during a battle in a tower, later using **Control Flames** to extinguish the spreading fire. He heard whispers from his yellow book, which revealed the phrases "**Ad Astra**" (To the stars) and "**Mors Imperii**" (Death of the empire) before finally displaying "**UP**" to direct the party to a teleportation point. When encountering the [[Lore/Bestiary#Yellow Cloak (Figure)|figure in the yellow cloak]], the entity's discordant voices sounded like generic voices to him.
- **May 28, 2026:** Delivered the killing blow to the [[Lore/Bestiary#Scion in Yellow|Scion in Yellow]] using a **Psychic Lance**. During the battle, a **Wild Magic Surge** caused him to grow a third eye for an hour. He was the one who opened the Scion's trembling yellow book, which triggered the party's teleportation back and revealed a final message: "*He has seen you. You have been noted.*"
- **June 11, 2026:** Caught up with [[Characters/NPC/Amber Lyre|Amber Lyre]] about their shared past; he remembers waking to an unrecognizable world after his time in cryo, and admits he no longer trusts the AIs. He stated he has no allegiance to Tesselia, Drakenweld, or any AI - he likes his old modern society but won't give up his mind to advance it, and just wants a comfortable life with his friends, having fully adopted the "Maxim" identity. Received a **Potion of Heroism** from [[Characters/NPC/Angela Nevermore|Nevermore]] as advance payment for the next mission: clearing out Anti-Air gun sites near the party's original landing spot.
- **June 25, 2026:** During the robot fight, countered a robot that targeted [[Characters/PC/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] (to grant attackers advantage) by dropping darkness over the area with a **Hunger of Hadar** spell. After the fight, he melted the door to the central area, opening a new room of enemies.
- **July 9, 2026:** Fought the central-area room from outside cover with the spellcasters, summoning **Hunger of Hadar** again to control the area as enemy reinforcements poured in from side rooms.
- **July 16, 2026:** Helped turn the tide of the central-area fight with a **Fireball**. Upstairs, the chief [[Lore/Technology#Slave Drives|Slave Drive]] robot recognized him as being from the past and asked, "Query: allegiance with entity Odin?" — Maxim denied any allegiance to [[Characters/NPC/Odin AI|Odin AI]], which the robot said was "good to hear."
- **July 23, 2026:** Led the parley with the [[Characters/NPC/Overseer (VI)|Overseer]], learning it is independent of all prior factions and not an ally of [[Characters/NPC/Odin AI|Odin]]; he explained the [[Lore/Technology#VI vs. AI|VI/AI distinction]] and recalled research on the middle ground from around the time he was cryofrozen. Identified the powder cartridge in the office lock as a likely explosive, and caught [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]] with **Telekinesis** instead of his arms. Dimension Doored out of the church site as Odin's ship arrived to bombard it. Back at the facility he wheeled [[Characters#Lady Ellen|Lady Ellen]] to the main hall; she recognized his family name and gave him a psychic twinge over his family polluting the waters. Filed a report on the Overseer for [[Characters/NPC/Sofia AI|Sofia AI]]. During the rest he mentioned "**[[Lore/Technology#The Past and Future, Running Free|Past and Future, Running Free]]**" to [[Characters/NPC/Amber Lyre|Amber Lyre]], who had seen the phrase on a book. On her way out, a **Wisdom save** hit him with intense pain — he took heavy damage and fell unconscious.

- **August 6, 2026:** The wave knocked him into the **[[Lore/Technology#First Space and Second Space|Second Space]]** — pure darkness, incorporeal, with a single pedestal lit by a diminished **Light** spell. A glass sphere on the pedestal brought **[[Characters/NPC/Odin AI|Odin]]** before him, who offered to send **Maxim and one other back in time** in exchange for **[[Characters/NPC/Sofia AI|Sofia]]'s AI core** and the **leader of the Tesselia facility**, dead or alive. Maxim admitted he has **no past worth returning to** — only the training facility and the cryopod. The sphere then turned amber gold and he felt **[[Characters/NPC/Amber Lyre|Amber]]'s presence**. [[Characters/PC/Juneberry|Juneberry]] pulled him back with the **[[Lore/Technology#Cognitive Retriever|Cognitive Retriever]]**; he woke feeling torn apart, in heavy pain, and [[Characters/PC/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]]'s **Channel Divinity** eased it slightly. Sofia warned him that Odin's offer is really a **[[Lore/Technology#Odin's Demiplane|demiplane scheme]]** and that the "bring a friend" clause is a sacrifice; Maxim argued he can't trust her either, and his **Insight** check on her read only "tired." The next morning he told [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]] he is **happy where he is** and wants no part of the power struggles — noting he impersonated the **middle child, not the heir**. His mother **[[Characters/NPC/Maxwella|Maxwella]]** arrived via Sofia's teleportation-runed message, apparently still unaware of his true nature, bringing news of the **[[Lore/Factions and Organizations#The Severance|Severance]]** faction and a **Wand of Wonder** (7 charges, d100 effect) as a gift from his father. **Leveled up to 13.**
- **August 13, 2026:** **Sent a message** whose contents he did not share with the party. At the docks he threw dirt at the ripples [[Characters/PC/Brambleberry|Brambleberry]] had spotted and felt a **hard mechanical grip clamp around his neck** — a cloaked [[Lore/Technology#Slave Drives|Slave Drive]], called off by the **[[Characters/NPC/Overseer (VI)|Overseer]]**. He negotiated the resulting bargain and pressed the Overseer for the **[[Lore/Technology#The Ancient Core Fragment|core fragment]]**'s appearance and location, learning it sits in a heavy specialized compartment case in the ship's upper layers.
- **August 20, 2026:** **Stunned and blinded for a turn** by an enemy flashbang. Once free, threw a **lightning-transmuted Fireball** at the tank and scout robots interfacing with the ship's consoles, hurting both badly. Sheltered under the ramp with [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]] as the retreat began.
- **August 27, 2026:** **Went invisible** to avoid being targeted during the escape and stayed that way for the entire session — walking untouched past the trapped hallway, scouting the turret room, and slipping past the robots in the next room without triggering them (it was [[Characters/PC/Villhelm Emberstoke|Villhelm]] who started that fight). Reported that the surviving robots **fled to a room holding a second Super Heavy Chassis**.
- **September 3, 2026:** Recognized the **[[Lore/Technology#Regenerator Tanks|regenerator tanks]]** on the lower deck and tried to speak to the **elvish** occupant of one — the reply came back distant, as if **brain-dead** — then failed to break the tank open. In the interrogation room he **offered himself in [[B-Team|Maggie]]'s place**, "for [[Characters/NPC/Odin AI|Odin]]", and bargained the **[[Lore/Technology#Interrogator Units|interrogators]]** into releasing her to [[Characters/PC/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] for healing in exchange for wearing a **memory-scanning circlet**. The scan left him with **vertigo**, but his **Guarded Mind** feat and psionic training let him **shield his memories of [[Characters/NPC/Sofia AI|Sofia AI]]**. When the ship began to lift off he cast **Dimension Door** and left with Maggie, then **Invisibility** on them both — watching from the ground as the airship departed **with the rest of the party still aboard**.
- **September 10, 2026:** On the ground he saw **[[B-Team]] racing for the departing ship**, **dropped his invisibility**, and **handed Maggie over to them**. The **[[Lore/Technology#The Mechanical Slate|mechanical slate]] then teleported him back to the party** mid-fight, arriving alongside a bomb-like device that fired **seeking needles that healed everyone to full**. He identified [[Characters/NPC/Madasin|Madasin]]'s **lightning resistance and lack of vulnerabilities**, then **Quickened a Thunder Step** to snatch the downed [[Characters/PC/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] out of her reach and **revived him with a greater healing potion**. Her return fire mostly struck his **Mirror Images**, landing once.
- **September 24, 2026:** Dragged the halved [[Characters/NPC/Madasin|Madasin]] to the elevator to **unlock it with her body**. In the cockpit the dying [[Characters/NPC/Overseer (VI)|Overseer]] asked if it could trust him. He said yes, and it gave him its **auxiliary Tier 4 core housing** (holding a small core) before going unresponsive. At the controls he **nearly got locked out**, learning that guidance is locked to **"permission level Odin"** and that the ship is returning to **[[Locations/Tower Isle|Tower Isle]]**. After teleporting to Tesselia he **messaged [[B-Team|Wendy]]** and studied [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]]'s tablet with him. [[Sessions/2026/2026-09-24|Session]]

- [[Sessions/2026/2026-08-20|Session: 2026-08-20]]
- **October 1, 2026:** Was caught in his underwear by [[Characters/NPC/Maxwella|Maxwella]], who refused to dress him. Recognized [[Characters/NPC/Angela Nevermore|Nevermore]]'s device as an **anti-espionage device**. Learned from [[Characters/NPC/Sofia AI|Sofia AI]] that the [[Characters/NPC/Overseer (VI)|Overseer]] is a **Gen 3**, ancient even in his time, and that the new core is a **Gen 4**. Doubted that scouting [[Lore/Locations of Interest#The Mountain|The Mountain]] is as pressing as the Odin situation, and doubts the dragon can be talked to. [[Sessions/2026/2026-10-01|Session]]
