## Backstory / Events
Amber Lyre is a changeling bard who was discovered by the party in April 2026 during a fight against automatons. She survived the "pods" just like [[Characters/PC/Maximus Arkelius|Maximus Arkelius]] and was relieved to find another survivor. She aided the party in combat and helped repair their scavenged technology. She is familiar with ancient tech, recognizing the Fabricator/PortaFab when [[Characters/PC/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] presented it.
- **April 2026:** Amber revealed she crash-landed from the orbital station. She successfully repaired a communicator and contacted the Odin AI, receiving instructions from him.
- **June 11, 2026:** Caught up with [[Characters/PC/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] about their shared past, though she can't remember most of the last week before going into cryo. She thinks [[Characters/NPC/Odin AI|Odin]] is just following its programming to its ultimate end and wouldn't mind going back to the old modern world. She volunteered to run reconnaissance solo for the party's next mission (clearing Anti-Air gun sites), as she works best alone.
- **July 23, 2026:** Made the rounds of the party's rooms with pastries during the rest, recognizing the banana **Goodberries** as [[Characters/PC/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]]'s work. When [[Characters/PC/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] mentioned the phrase "**[[Lore/Technology#The Past and Future, Running Free|Past and Future, Running Free]]**," she found it odd — she had found a book bearing it, which she used to block a laser shot. She also reported spotting a **heavy** armed with a railgun comparable to a **Disintegrate** spell. Leaving the room, she and Maxim both had to make **Wisdom saves**; she felt the pain but resisted it, while Maxim fell unconscious.

- **August 6, 2026:** Hit hard by the same wave as [[Characters/PC/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] — she staggered outside, collapsed gulping for air, then went unconscious after touching her head while [[Characters/PC/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] helped her crawl back inside. [[Characters/NPC/Sofia AI|Sofia AI]] explained that Amber, like Maxim, was **built to receive telepathic instructions** through the **[[Lore/Technology#First Space and Second Space|Second Space]]**, which [[Characters/NPC/Odin AI|Odin]] has weaponized to rip out their minds. Maxim sensed **her presence** in his own vision of the Second Space, when the sphere turned amber gold. The **[[Lore/Technology#Cognitive Retriever|Cognitive Retriever]]** took **three shocks** to reach her: she woke screaming, coughed up blood, and fell unconscious again. The next day she was on medical equipment, and [[Characters/NPC/Queen Tesselia|Queen Tesselia]] reported that scans show **part of her mind is missing**. The party wonders whether she was offered a deal of her own, and what she answered.
- **October 1, 2026:** Reported **stable** by Nevermore and Maggie. [[Sessions/2026/2026-10-01|Session]]
