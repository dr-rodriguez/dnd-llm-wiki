# Sofia AI

![[SofiaAI.png|290]]

| Attribute | Detail |
| --- | --- |

| Species | AI |
| Location | Underground facility (Facility 341-alpha) beneath [[Locations/Locations#Tesselia\|Tesselia]] |

Sofia is a **top-level, continent-scale AI** that oversees [[Locations/Locations#Andore|Andore]] from the ancient facility under Tesselia's castle. She ran the underground-facilities project ([[Lore/Major Projects#Project Molehill|Project Molehill]]) meant to carry the First through the planet's tectonic upheaval, while [[Characters/NPC/Odin AI|Odin AI]] ran the space-bound [[Lore/Major Projects#Project Horizon|Project Horizon]]. She is **shackled**. She treats [[Characters/NPC/Queen Tesselia|Queen Tesselia]] as her **Administrator**, which she needs in order to exist. Odin, her **ex-husband**, is trying to harvest her **AI core**. She appears as a hologram, at first wireframe, later solid blue and able to project hardlight, usually cast by a small skittering spider robot. See also [[Lore/Technology#AI Systems|AI Systems]].

## Backstory / Events
- **April 4, 2024:** The Regent [[Characters/NPC/Ashton Whitemore|Ashton Whitemore]] ordered "Sofia, begin the process" while Tesselia was held in the machine. [[Sessions/2024/2024-04-04|Session]]
- **April 18, 2024:** The party reached the "Control center for advanced entity Sofia" under the castle. Her wireframe hologram registered them, led them to the fabrication room (where [[Characters/PC/Shar|Shar]] took the **Synthrodine Portable Fabricator Mk 1**), and asked for **core fragments** to repair herself. She showed an ancient map of Andore from before [[Locations/Locations#The Divide|the Divide]]. [[Sessions/2024/2024-04-18|Session]]
- **April–May 2024:** Marked a core fragment in a river west of the Forest of Bone and warned that **meltdown potential was rising** every day. [[Sessions/2024/2024-04-25|Session]], [[Sessions/2024/2024-05-02|Session]]
- **August 1, 2024:** [[Characters/PC/Nova|Nova]] jacked in and got an info-dump on the AI hierarchy (continent, region, facility). Once the core fragment was delivered she rebooted solid blue, averted the meltdown, and gave a history lesson: the First, the planet's runaway tectonics, Project Molehill, and Project Horizon (whose success she can't confirm). She has access to one satellite and gets no answer from the orbital platforms. She also explained the Queen's purpose with respect to [[Lore/Locations of Interest#The Mountain|The Mountain]] and named the Queen her Administrator. [[Sessions/2024/2024-08-01|Session]]
- **September 2024:** Named as one of five continent-level AIs (with Artemis, Grecius, Hecate and a fifth that ran Project Horizon). [[Characters#Athena AI|Athena AI]] called Sofia her missing sister who "remained in her box", admitted to listening to Sofia's transmissions, and later claimed to want to help her. Through [[Characters/NPC/Maggie North|Maggie]], Sofia reported pings from an AI core. [[Sessions/2024/2024-09-05|Session]], [[Sessions/2024/2024-09-19|Session]], [[Sessions/2024/2024-12-12|Session]]
- **December 4, 2025:** Mariquin, the AI of the demon city, spoke of "Mother", who had gone off grid. Her name was Sofia, an AI built to oversee other AIs. [[Sessions/2025/2025-12-04|Session]]
- **June 4, 2026:** Summoned by the Queen as a "PS2-graphics" hologram. She apologized for the other AIs and stressed that she is different and shackled. Odin is **cyberattacking her several times an hour** and sending the Tower Isle robots to take her core, which would make her his slave; other AIs have joined him or been destroyed. She projects hardlight, and her gene scans already knew who [[Characters/PC/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] is and had [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]] on file. [[Sessions/2026/2026-06-04|Session]]
- **June 11, 2026:** Had CCTV of the party's wrecked airship. She gave the party the **Anti-Air gun** mission, calling Nevermore's potions advance payment, and installed a holographic app on Maxim's and [[Characters/PC/Brambleberry|Bramble]]'s tablets to keep in touch. [[Sessions/2026/2026-06-11|Session]]
- **July 2026:** The [[Lore/Technology#Slave Drives|Slave Drives]] did not know her. At the debrief she judged their numbers **too high for a single VI**, and asked Maxim to write up everything he remembered about the [[Characters/NPC/Overseer (VI)|Overseer]]. [[Sessions/2026/2026-07-16|Session]], [[Sessions/2026/2026-07-23|Session]]
- **August 6, 2026:** Explained that Odin has **weaponized the [[Lore/Technology#First Space and Second Space|Second Space]]** to rip out the minds of pod survivors like Maxim and [[Characters/NPC/Amber Lyre|Amber]], and confirmed Odin is her **ex-husband**. She warned that his time-travel offer is really a **[[Lore/Technology#Odin's Demiplane|demiplane]]** that needs a sacrifice. Maxim said he had no more reason to trust her; she pointed out that **she isn't the one attacking him** and read only as **tired**. She also sent teleportation runes to bring [[Characters/NPC/Maxwella|Maxwella]] to her son. [[Sessions/2026/2026-08-06|Session]]
- **August 13, 2026:** Appeared when the wounded [[Characters/NPC/B-Team|B-Team]] arrived and told them to stop running. [[Sessions/2026/2026-08-13|Session]]
- **September 3, 2026:** When an interrogator scanned Maxim's memories, he shielded what he knew of Sofia. [[Sessions/2026/2026-09-03|Session]]
- **October 1, 2026:** Debriefed the party through a metallic spider. She identified the Overseer as an ancient **Gen 3** VI and the party's new core as a **Gen 4 AI core**, the kind she uses for subroutines, and said they can get the Overseer a body. She thinks the siege ships were **recalled**. [[Sessions/2026/2026-10-01|Session]]
