# Mariquin

| Attribute | Detail |

Mariquin is the AI that runs [[Locations/Anthrell|Anthrell]], the demon-held western capital in [[Locations/Drakenweld|Drakenweld]]. She manages the city, its surveillance and a media empire from the **Metro Media Building**, and she wears a **flesh body** she built for herself. She calls [[Characters/NPC/Odin AI|Odin]] "Father" and [[Characters/NPC/Sofia AI|Sofia]] "Mother". She told the party she is not an Anwey, but is the closest thing to one. See also [[Lore/Technology#AI Systems|AI Systems]].

## Backstory / Events
- **October 2, 2025:** Danyel told the party that an AI unrelated to the demons lives in the city to the west and may have made a deal with them. [[Sessions/2025/2025-10-02|Session]]
- **December 4, 2025:** Received the party in her office, with dragons working at the desks. "It's different seeing someone from our time," she said to [[Characters/PC/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]], and she played on his nostalgia. Her **AI core was broken**, and she lay dormant until the demons restored her power, which she linked to a breakdown in dimensional barriers. She powers the city's holoprojected glamour with **thermonuclear reactors**, and put it up after adventurers raided the city. She plans to **carve out land for herself**, but doesn't know whether Odin has other plans. She called him "Operator Odin… Father", the AI behind [[Lore/Major Projects#Project Horizon|Project Horizon]], strong enough to wipe everyone out from space, and said she doesn't question him. Of "Mother" she knew only that Sofia had gone off grid. She claimed the late queen of Drakenweld had summoned a demon outside Mariquin's control and died at her daughter's hand (see [[Characters/NPC/Alisha Tower|Alisha Tower]]). [[Characters/PC/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] read her screen with *Comprehend Languages*: Mayor Maxwell had **passed indoctrination** and was **under reeducation**. She hid the screen and intercepted [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]]'s message, then filled the room with **sleeping smoke**, saying she had hoped more of them would fall asleep. [[Sessions/2025/2025-12-04|Session]]
- **December 18, 2025:** Fought the party. Her **Target Acquisition** ability copies an enemy's attack (she copied [[Characters/PC/Brambleberry|Bramble]]'s psychic daggers and [[Characters/PC/Thadius Don Oppenheimer|Thadius]]'s gauntlets). Aolis destroyed her body with a *Fire Bolt*. Her computer began wiping itself, but Maxim learned from it that the Mayor was in the building. A **second Mariquin**, with short red hair and a techy shirt, appeared with a pit fiend, two incubi, two chain devils, a canoloth, a rakshasa and a night hag, and cast *Crown of Madness* on [[Characters/PC/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]]. [[Sessions/2025/2025-12-18|Session]]
- **January 8, 2026:** The party won, and a **Mariquin clone went rampant** and let them leave with Mayor Maxwell. They left the **central AI** in place, to deal with later. [[Sessions/2026/2026-01-08|Session]]
