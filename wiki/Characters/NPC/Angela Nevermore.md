# Angela Nevermore

Lady Angela Nevermore (first heard as "Analia") is a Tesselian noblewoman who plotted against the Regent [[Characters/NPC/Ashton Whitemore|Ashton Whitemore]], helped kill him, and has since served as **Regent Advisor** to [[Characters/NPC/Queen Tesselia|Queen Tesselia]]. She is a long-lived **changeling** of the fey, magical kind, not a genetic changeling like the [[Lore/Ancient History#The Anwey (or Anway)|Anwey]]. She knew the Queen's mother and seems to have made her a promise. She knows the "secret codes" of [[Characters/NPC/Sofia AI|Sofia AI]]'s facility under the castle. Her base is **The Raven's Den**, an old gambling den turned parlor.

## Backstory / Events
- **December 7, 2023:** The Regent hired the party to tail her. They watched her lobby a guard station to act against the Regent and pay **Old Tommy's Smithy** a third of the price to arm a militia in city-guard style, with dwarf steel from [[Locations/Locations#Hollow Mountain|Hollow Mountain]]. [[Characters/PC/Juneberry|Juneberry]] stole the smith's ledger. Nevermore caught them, cast *Disguise Self*, and admitted she was planning a **coup**: she would rule until the Regent could be made to abdicate, without harming the young queen. She asked them to report that she had done nothing illegal. [[Sessions/2023/2023-12-07|Session]]
- **December 21, 2023:** The Regent questioned the party about her; they committed to nothing. [[Sessions/2023/2023-12-21|Session]]
- **January 4, 2024:** Invited the party to The Raven's Den, where [[Characters#Zarali|Zarali]] now works as her bodyguard. She warned that the Regent would kill the girl by her next birthday (31 January), showed a sketch of him at a draconic altar, and reported that he was buying crystallized amethyst. She sent the party to Hollow Mountain to restart her metal supply, with a sending stone and a scroll. [[Sessions/2024/2024-01-04|Session]]
- **March 2024:** Called the party to guard the Queen's carriage on its way to her safehouse, then led them into the castle by a side entrance while a dragon perched on it. [[Sessions/2024/2024-03-21|Session]], [[Sessions/2024/2024-03-28|Session]]
- **April 4, 2024:** In the throne room she told the party that Ashton must die and that they must not pull Tesselia out of the device. Cloaked in translucent energy, she vanished in a bolt and drove the dragon off, then came back, worked the controls and freed the Queen. [[Sessions/2024/2024-04-04|Session]]
- **April 2024:** Became "Regent Advisor". She revealed her changeling nature (her skin went dark grey in a blink), said she knew the being in the device, and said she had known the Queen's mother. She brought the disguised Queen to the party and led them to the tombs beneath the castle. [[Sessions/2024/2024-04-11|Session]], [[Sessions/2024/2024-04-18|Session]]
- **May–August 2024:** The party reported the core meltdown risk to her and the Queen, and later found them both with Sofia AI in the underground bunker. [[Sessions/2024/2024-05-02|Session]], [[Sessions/2024/2024-08-01|Session]]
- **June 2026:** Met the returning party in the besieged city with [[Characters/NPC/Maggie North|Maggie]] and the old party, and led them through a secret tunnel to the underground facility. She later gave every party member a **Superior Healing Potion** plus one more potion each, which Sofia called payment in advance. [[Sessions/2026/2026-06-04|Session]], [[Sessions/2026/2026-06-11|Session]]
- **July 23, 2026:** Took part in the debrief with the Queen and Sofia after the Anti-Air gun mission. [[Sessions/2026/2026-07-23|Session]]
- **October 1, 2026:** Visited the guild hall with [[Characters/NPC/Alisha Tower|Alisha Tower]] and [[Characters/NPC/Maxwella|Maxwella]] and spoke privately with Maggie. She set an **anti-espionage device** before Sofia's debrief and raised [[Lore/Locations of Interest#The Mountain|The Mountain]], which "the Queen" wants the party to scout. [[Sessions/2026/2026-10-01|Session]]
