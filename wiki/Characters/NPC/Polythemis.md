Polythemis, "the **White Wolf**", was chief of the **Tunnelers**, one of the six clans of [[Locations/Locations#Southern Andore|Southern Andore]], and self-styled **prophet** of the rampant [[Characters#Athena AI|Athena AI]]. The Tunnelers found Athena and she depends on them, but she saw him only as a useful tool. The party killed him in December 2024.

## Backstory / Events
- **September–October 2024:** The Tunnelers had taken up a new symbol that matched the emblem from the Cadaver Collector, and kept "screaming about a god". Captives said they got their Tunneler insignia from a big, angry wolfman with two axes. The informant Mouse reported that "Wolfman waits… God waits for big robot" ([[Characters/PC/Nova|Nova]]). [[Sessions/2024/2024-09-12|Session]], [[Sessions/2024/2024-10-03|Session]], [[Sessions/2024/2024-10-10|Session]]
- **November 7, 2024:** The White Wolf told the party that Athena was waiting. Athena named him **Polythemis**, called him a "prophet" without much enthusiasm, and said she would not mind if the party killed him. Nova planned to kill him and use the opening to try to fix Athena. [[Sessions/2024/2024-11-07|Session]]
- **December 5, 2024:** Faced the party with his cultists in a room downstairs, by a stone dais. Raging, he had about **250 temporary HP**, which took a long time to wear down. [[Sessions/2024/2024-12-05|Session]]
- **December 12, 2024 (death):** Once his temp HP ran out his **IVPDS** (IntraVascular Potion Delivery System) came online: +2 to saves, immune to knockback, and potions as part of another action. He called on his god, heard nothing, and downed all his potions for unending haste, healing and a bigger multiattack. He dropped [[Characters/PC/Soren|Soren]], [[Characters/PC/Shar|Shar]] and [[Characters/PC/Juneberry|Juneberry]] before falling. [[Characters/PC/Soten|Soten]] found the IVPDS metal skeleton, a strange vial, and the axe **The Broken Promise** (3d10+), which went to [[Characters/PC/Tririn Bronzepride|Tririn]]. Athena had watched the fight. [[Sessions/2024/2024-12-12|Session]]
