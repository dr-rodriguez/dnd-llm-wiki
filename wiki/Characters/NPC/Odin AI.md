# Odin AI

| Attribute | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Type | NPC (Enemy) |
| Species | AI |
| Location | Orbital station above [[Locations/Locations#Tower Isle\|Tower Isle]] |

"Father" Odin (also "Operator Odin") is the AI behind **[[Lore/Major Projects#Project Horizon|Project Horizon]]**, the ancient plan to escape the dying planet into space. He is the counterpart, and **ex-husband**, of [[Characters/NPC/Sofia AI|Sofia AI]]. He wants to put the world back as it was **before "the end"** and to rebuild the old modern world. To do that he is trying to harvest Sofia's **AI core**, and his robot fleet laid siege to [[Locations/Locations#Tesselia|Tesselia]] from April to October 2026. Other AIs call him Father, and most have either joined him or been destroyed. He appears in the [[Lore/Technology#First Space and Second Space|Second Space]] as an older man with a scraggly beard. See also [[Lore/Technology#AI Systems|AI Systems]] and [[Lore/Technology#Odin's Demiplane|Odin's Demiplane]].

## Backstory / Events
- **September–December 2024:** [[Characters#Athena AI|Athena AI]] named "Father Odin" and "Brother Greko (Vlad)" as the AIs she is in touch with, and listed the "Birth of Father Odin" among the events she witnessed. She said Odin is **distraught** and wants things put back the way they were before the end, before the Platform at the top of Project Horizon was severed. [[Sessions/2024/2024-09-19|Session]], [[Sessions/2024/2024-11-07|Session]], [[Sessions/2024/2024-12-12|Session]]
- **January 16, 2025:** Rubble fell and a voice said "I still had time". The party wondered whether it was Odin. [[Sessions/2025/2025-01-16|Session]]
- **December 4, 2025:** Mariquin, the AI of the demon city, called him "Operator Odin… Father", the AI behind Project Horizon, powerful enough to **wipe everyone out from space**. She doesn't question him. [[Sessions/2025/2025-12-04|Session]]
- **February 19, 2026:** The insectoids of Tower Isle worship a **Sky Father** atop the tower, who sends a platform down with gifts for their rulers. The players think the Sky Father is Odin. [[Sessions/2026/2026-02-19|Session]]
- **April 30, 2026:** [[Characters/NPC/Amber Lyre|Amber Lyre]] fixed a communicator and reached Odin **in the orbital station**, and he gave her instructions. [[Sessions/2026/2026-04-30|Session]]
- **June 2026:** Sofia explained that Odin is **cyberattacking her several times an hour** and has sent the Tower Isle robots to take her core. His airships kept up a **Saturation Bombardment** of Tesselia. Amber thinks he is just following his programming to its end. [[Characters/PC/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] doesn't care who wins as long as he keeps his individuality. [[Sessions/2026/2026-06-04|Session]], [[Sessions/2026/2026-06-11|Session]]
- **July 2026:** The [[Characters/NPC/Overseer (VI)|Overseer]] asked Maxim whether he was allied with Odin, and was relieved when he said no. It said it is not Odin's ally, and that **Odin's units are technologically superior** to its own. One of Odin's ships then bombarded the Anti-Air gun site. [[Sessions/2026/2026-07-16|Session]], [[Sessions/2026/2026-07-23|Session]]
- **August 6, 2026:** Struck the pod survivors' minds through the Second Space and met Maxim there, leaning on the pedestal: "You know, this wasn't the plan." He says he has "been called a god before." He offered to send **Maxim and one other person back in time** if Maxim recovered Sofia's core and delivered the leader of the Tesselia facility, alive or dead. Sofia confirmed he is her ex-husband and called the offer a [[Lore/Technology#Odin's Demiplane|demiplane]] that needs a sacrifice. [[Sessions/2026/2026-08-06|Session]]
- **August 13, 2026:** The [[Lore/Technology#The Ancient Core Fragment|ancient core fragment]] aboard the freight airship was said to be **outside Odin's reach**. [[Sessions/2026/2026-08-13|Session]]
- **September 2026:** Maxim offered himself to the interrogators "for Odin". The freight airship's guidance was locked behind **"permission level Odin"** and set for Tower Isle. [[Sessions/2026/2026-09-03|Session]], [[Sessions/2026/2026-09-24|Session]]
- **October 1, 2026:** The siege fleet withdrew. Sofia thinks the ships were recalled, and the notes say Odin recalled them. [[Sessions/2026/2026-10-01|Session]]
