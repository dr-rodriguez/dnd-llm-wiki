# Sol'Ar

| Attribute | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Type | NPC (Deity) |
| Species | God |
| Domain | Revelry and the sun |

Sol'Ar (also written "Solar") is the god of **revelry and the sun**, and patron of [[Characters/PC/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]]. Zinjaro worships him hedonistically: good wine, rich food and festivals count as devotion, since "the sun shines on all". He appears as a **fire lizard** or salamander. He has said it is hard for him to reach the material world. Luna, the dual-aspect moon god, is his more somber counterpart. See [[Lore/Deities and Religions|Deities and Religions]].

## Backstory / Events
- **May 2025:** Zinjaro began wearing Sol'Ar's religious dress in public, a white suit with yellow and orange highlights and a yellow bowtie, so everyone knows he is a cleric of Solar. [[Sessions/2025/2025-05-08|Session]]
- **August 14, 2025:** Professor Namthyis guessed that Zinjaro is a cleric of Sol'Ar. [[Sessions/2025/2025-08-14|Session]]
- **September 4, 2025:** Appeared on the ship as a **fire lizard** while Zinjaro poured out wine from the Alchemy Jug, and chatted with him like an old friend. He spoke of how hard it can be to reach the material world, and noticed that [[Characters/NPC/Alisha Tower|Alisha]] was not really praying to Luna ("it's all cold"). He warmed Zinjaro's holy symbol, drank half the jug and vanished, leaving Zinjaro **"The Solar Gift"**: immunity to the Frightened condition, and *Daylight* once a day. [[Sessions/2025/2025-09-04|Session]], [[Sessions/Extra Notes|Extra Notes]]
- **September 11, 2025:** One of the Hydra's heads took the form of a fiery lizard like Sol'Ar and looked at Zinjaro with disappointment. [[Sessions/2025/2025-09-11|Session]]
- **September 18, 2025:** Zinjaro's **Shard of Solar** summoned daylight that broke the Hydra's terror. Afterwards **Sol'Ar's avatar appeared, visible to everyone**. To prove himself he made Maxim incredibly drunk. Luna appeared beside him, and the boat split into Solar's partying side and Luna's somber side. He left calling the three-foot Zinjaro "Big Guy". [[Sessions/2025/2025-09-18|Session]]
- **October 2025:** Danyel described **the Net**, a wall separating the world from the "Planar". Zinjaro wondered if it was the wall Sol'Ar had mentioned. [[Sessions/2025/2025-10-02|Session]], [[Sessions/2025/2025-10-09|Session]]
- **2026:** Zinjaro credited Sol'Ar for some unripe green bananas, and took a fire salamander he saw to be Sol'Ar. In August he cast *Conjure Celestial*, summoning Sol'Ar's light as a moving cylinder that healed allies and harmed enemies. In September a bartender cut him off at 26 drinks on spotting his Sol'Ar symbol. [[Sessions/2026/2026-02-12|Session]], [[Sessions/2026/2026-03-12|Session]], [[Sessions/2026/2026-08-20|Session]], [[Sessions/2026/2026-09-24|Session]]
