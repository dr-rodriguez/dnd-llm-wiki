# Maxwella

Lady Maxwella is [[Characters/PC/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]]'s mother. She is a very tall (7–8 ft) wood elf and the lady of the wealthy Arkelius house in [[Locations/Locations#Moren|Moren]]. Maxim was nearly named after her, "but turns out he wasn't a girl". She has had four children, including a son sent north to look into the old forts. She is a powerful spellcaster who can cast *Banishment* at 9th level, and she seems to know **nothing of Maxim's real nature**. She is not related to Mayor Maxwell of [[Locations/Locations#Helines|Helines]].

## Backstory / Events
- **April 17, 2025:** Welcomed the party at the estate, where the maid Olga said the master was away. She sensed something wrong with [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]]. The demon encounter may have taken Aolis's **capacity for affection toward Maxim**, not his soul. She has heard of Moren's Overseer (Mr. Manager), who works through intermediaries, but never met them. [[Sessions/2025/2025-04-17|Session]]
- **April–May 2025:** Set up a meeting with **Lindsey** of Lindsey's Laundries. She was **scrying** on the party when Maxim was nearly kidnapped. She was invited to the Overseer's **gala** along with the "violent adventurers" in her employ, put the party up until it, and **smuggled their armor in**. There she chatted with Lady Novara Kavash, the family's ore contact from [[Locations/Locations#Drakenweld|Drakenweld]]. [[Sessions/2025/2025-04-24|Session]], [[Sessions/2025/2025-05-08|Session]], [[Sessions/2025/2025-05-15|Session]]
- **May 15, 2025:** Long ago she had made a deal with a dragon to look after its eggs. She asked [[Characters/PC/Wobbles Wibbbles|Wobbles]] to take home the one viable **crystalline dragon egg** and teleported him away. [[Sessions/2025/2025-05-15|Session]]
- **June 5, 2025:** Danced with Maxim at the gala while he told her telepathically that Byren and the Overseer were dead. She wants to take over some of the Overseer's role. That night she burst in on drow agents of Aolis's matron mother, **banished most of them with a 9th-level *Banishment***, freed the paralyzed Maxim, and ordered the matron mother out. [[Sessions/2025/2025-06-05|Session]]
- **August 6, 2026:** [[Characters/NPC/Sofia AI|Sofia AI]] sent her teleportation runes, and she rushed into the Tesselia facility to her stricken son, plainly just a worried parent. She brought news of the **[[Lore/Factions and Organizations#The Severance|Severance]]**, a faction that wants to seize the floating mountain of the [[Locations/Locations#Mountain Home Academy of Magic|Mountain Home Academy of Magic]], and a **Wand of Wonder** (7 charges) from Maxim's father. [[Sessions/2026/2026-08-06|Session]]
- **August 13, 2026:** Was at breakfast with the party when the wounded [[Characters/NPC/B-Team|B-Team]] burst in. [[Sessions/2026/2026-08-13|Session]]
- **October 1, 2026:** Visited the Tesselia guild hall with [[Characters/NPC/Alisha Tower|Alisha]] and [[Characters/NPC/Angela Nevermore|Nevermore]]. She found Maxim in his underwear, refused to dress him, and suggested Aolis whip him so he gets up earlier. [[Sessions/2026/2026-10-01|Session]]
