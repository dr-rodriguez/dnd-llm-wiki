The Overseer is the chief of the clockwork **[[Lore/Technology#Slave Drives|Slave Drives]]** found around [[Locations/Tesselia|Tesselia]] in 2026. It sits somewhere between a VI and a full AI (see [[Lore/Technology#VI vs. AI|VI vs. AI]]), and [[Characters/NPC/Sofia AI|Sofia AI]] identified it as an ancient **Gen 3**. It claims to be **independent of all prior factions**: not [[Characters/NPC/Odin AI|Odin]]'s ally, though Odin's units outclass its own. It does not know Sofia. It is not the same being as **Mr. Manager**, the "Overseer" of [[Locations/Moren|Moren]].

## Backstory / Events
- **July 16, 2026:** At the Anti-Air gun site in a church, the chief Slave Drive was building a **failsafe** to keep itself alive and disciplined a drive with a lightning shock. It recognized [[Characters/PC/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] as being from the past and asked: *"Query: allegiance with entity Odin?"* When Maxim denied it, it replied: *"Statement: this is good to hear."* [[Sessions/2026/2026-07-16|Session]]
- **July 23, 2026:** Said the machines need no names but may be called **Overseer**. It would not say whether it is Odin's enemy, only that it is not his ally, and it avoids his superior units. It had **CCTV footage of the [[Lore/Factions and Organizations#Technocrats|Technocrats]]** but would not say where they were. It left through the roof, warning the party to go "unless explosion-proof", just before Odin's ship bombarded the site. Sofia thinks it commands **too many drives for a single VI**. [[Sessions/2026/2026-07-23|Session]]
- **August 13, 2026:** Reappeared, cloaked, at the Tesselia docks. Its **core is failing** and it will die in **under 3 days** without a replacement, even a corrupted one. It made a deal: it would draw off the droid guard while the party recovered an **[[Lore/Technology#The Ancient Core Fragment|ancient core fragment]]** from the freight airship. It was genuinely confused that they would risk their lives for one person. [[Sessions/2026/2026-08-13|Session]]
- **August 20, 2026:** It and its Slave Drives **joined the airship fight openly** on the party's side. A drive was seen **humming**, which **let the Overseer act**. It warned the party to flee the [[Lore/Bestiary#Super Heavy Chassis|Super Heavy Chassis]]. [[Sessions/2026/2026-08-20|Session]]
- **August 27, 2026:** Outran the whole party and revealed a **medical bed that was really a hidden elevator**. It can **remotely disable turrets**, but each time damages itself, and it could manage only one more. It can also **take over dormant Slave Drives**. **Magical healing does not work on it.** [[Sessions/2026/2026-08-27|Session]]
- **September 3, 2026:** Freed [[Characters/PC/Villhelm Emberstoke|Villhelm]] from keyless manacles, then **vanished** as the ship took off. [[Sessions/2026/2026-09-03|Session]]
- **September 24, 2026:** Found in the cockpit holding the door shut, **badly damaged and immobile**. Once it was sure it could **trust Maxim**, it opened its **auxiliary Tier 4 core housing** (holding a small core) for him to take. It was clutching a **~15 lb new core** meant to go into a **new body** once tools are found. Then it went unresponsive. The party took the new core and left the body aboard. [[Sessions/2026/2026-09-24|Session]]
- **October 1, 2026:** Sofia said nobody expected a Gen 3 to still exist, since they were **ancient even in Maxim's time**. The new core is a **Gen 4 AI core**, the kind Sofia uses for subroutines, and she said **they can get it a body**. See [[Quests/Quests#Tasks|Restore the Overseer]]. [[Sessions/2026/2026-10-01|Session]]
