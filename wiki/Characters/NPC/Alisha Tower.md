# Alisha Tower

| Attribute | Detail |

Lady Alisha Tower is the daughter of Queen Elizabeth Tower, granddaughter of Margaret Tower, and daughter of **Arvin the Dragonslayer**. The Towers are the high-lords, effectively the emperors, of [[Locations/Drakenweld|Drakenweld]]. Alisha is a front-line demon hunter known as "the **Slayer**". She would rather fight the war than govern, which caused long friction with her mother. In 2025 she revealed she is an **Aasimar** and the **Sky Dancer**, as her father was before her. By October 2026 she was **Queen of Drakenweld**. See the [[Quests/Drakenweld Quest|Drakenweld Quest]].

## Backstory / Events
- **July 17, 2025:** Expected the party, since [[Characters/NPC/Lady Poomf|Lady Poomf]] had sent word ahead. She told [[Characters/PC/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]] she wanted to be on the front lines, which would be "improper" for a lady of her station. She didn't know what bananas were, gave [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]] a scrollcase, and stood watch with the party. [[Sessions/2025/2025-07-17|Session]]
- **July 2025:** Soldiers called her "the Slayer". She warned Maxim off his boasting about summoning a Mind Flayer, and used *Detect Poison and Disease* on an infected wound. [[Sessions/2025/2025-07-24|Session]], [[Sessions/2025/2025-07-31|Session]]
- **August 7, 2025:** Follows a sect that sees **Luna** as a silent observer of one's eventual death. As a girl her mother had her mix with common folk, and she apprenticed with **Miss Meow**, the baker of Light. [[Sessions/2025/2025-08-07|Session]]
- **August 14, 2025:** In the throne room Queen Elizabeth glared at her and later scolded her: Alisha wants the front line, while the Queen wants her to take her place ("Why are you so much like him?" "Because he's where we should be"). Aolis noticed flecks of blood in her hair. She was sent off to study astronomy with her tutor, the elven professor Namthyis Au'ren. [[Sessions/2025/2025-08-14|Session]]
- **August 2025:** Joined the party with the Professor, led them through a tundra shortcut, and smote a **Marilith** that challenged her. She said she had seen that demon twice in the past year, and that demons were being re-summoned faster. Asked about governing, she said she cared more about the war. [[Sessions/2025/2025-08-21|Session]], [[Sessions/2025/2025-08-28|Session]]
- **September 4, 2025:** On the ship she prayed at the mast, but someone noticed she wasn't praying to Luna: "it's all cold". In a vision, after seeing her father swallowed whole, she swore an oath: "Give me the power and I'll rip every demon until the princes of Hell are before me." Her hair turned from brunette to blonde. [[Sessions/2025/2025-09-04|Session]]
- **September 2025:** The Hydra took the form of her father among its heads. She dove into the water, **sprouted wings**, saved Aolis, and summoned a halberd. She said she understands duality and is the **Sky Dancer**, as her father was. She gave [[Characters/PC/Ogra Oaksworn|Ogra]] the **Frost Iron Blade** and left the party once the ship docked. [[Sessions/2025/2025-09-11|Session]], [[Sessions/2025/2025-09-18|Session]]
- **December 4, 2025:** [[Characters/NPC/Mariquin|Mariquin]] claimed that "the once queen" had summoned a demon of her own accord, and that the queen was now dead at her daughter's hand. [[Sessions/2025/2025-12-04|Session]]
- **July 23, 2026:** Appeared as a 3D projection at [[Characters/NPC/Queen Tesselia|Queen Tesselia]]'s meeting and promised to set out for Tesselia. [[Sessions/2026/2026-07-23|Session]]
- **October 1, 2026:** Arrived at the Tesselia guild hall as **Queen of Drakenweld**, just after the siege ended. She had been "caring for her mother", who was trying to **hand the kingdom to a demon**. A new court of nobles now sits in Drakenweld, and she got a lot of use out of her spear, **Sky Dancer**. [[Sessions/2026/2026-10-01|Session]]
