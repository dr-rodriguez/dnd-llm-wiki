Queen Tesselia ("Tessa") is the child monarch of [[Locations/Locations#Andore|Andore]], ruling from the capital that shares her name. Her father, the Regent [[Characters/NPC/Ashton Whitemore|Ashton Whitemore]], ruled for her until the party killed him. Since then [[Characters/NPC/Angela Nevermore|Angela Nevermore]] has advised her. [[Characters/NPC/Sofia AI|Sofia AI]] treats her as its **Administrator**, which it needs in order to exist. Her coronation is due at 16.

## Backstory / Events
- **November–December 2023:** The guild described her as a 5-year-old queen whose father, the Regent, was abusing his position. Nevermore later gave her age as 7 and feared the Regent would kill her before she was crowned at 16. [[Sessions/2023/2023-11-30|Session]], [[Sessions/2023/2023-12-07|Session]]
- **January 2024:** Nevermore warned that the Regent would kill "that girl" by her next birthday on **31 January**, and suspected he meant to turn her into a dragon-creature with crystallized amethyst. [[Sessions/2024/2024-01-04|Session]]
- **March 2024:** The party guarded her carriage on its way to Nevermore's safehouse. [[Sessions/2024/2024-03-21|Session]]
- **April 4, 2024:** Found in the throne room inside a crackling **mechanical** cage. Ashton told "Sofia" to "begin the process" and said "she's becoming mortal". She was not in pain but wanted out. After Ashton died, Nevermore freed her. She said: "Both of my parents are dead." [[Sessions/2024/2024-04-04|Session]]
- **April 18, 2024:** Visited the party in disguise with Nevermore and opened the hidden door under the castle with the access code **TESSELIA**, leading to the tombs and Sofia's facility. [[Sessions/2024/2024-04-18|Session]]
- **May–August 2024:** Heard the party's report on the core meltdown, and was later with Sofia in the underground bunker, where Sofia explained the Queen's purpose in relation to [[Lore/Locations of Interest#The Mountain|The Mountain]]. [[Sessions/2024/2024-05-02|Session]], [[Sessions/2024/2024-08-01|Session]]
- **September 2024:** Met Queen Elnasha of the merfolk at the docks, after which a guild branch was set up in Southern Andore. [[Sessions/2024/2024-09-12|Session]]
- **June 2026:** Now **13**, sheltering underground during the siege. She summoned Sofia for the returning party and kept trying to build defenses that kept getting knocked down. [[Sessions/2026/2026-06-04|Session]], [[Sessions/2026/2026-06-11|Session]]
- **July 23, 2026:** Hosted a meeting with projections of [[Characters/NPC/Alisha Tower|Alisha Tower]] and Lady Ellen, then took the party's debrief. [[Sessions/2026/2026-07-23|Session]]
- **August 6, 2026:** Reported that scans show part of [[Characters/NPC/Amber Lyre|Amber Lyre]]'s mind is missing, and had a robot trolley bring the party breakfast. [[Sessions/2026/2026-08-06|Session]]
- **October 1, 2026:** After Nevermore raised The Mountain, "the Queen" (presumably Tesselia, though the notes don't name her) wanted the party to scout it. The pay is **500 platinum** plus any hoard the resident dragon holds. [[Sessions/2026/2026-10-01|Session]]
