Lord Ashton Whitemore was the acting **Regent** of Tesselia and father of the child [[Characters/NPC/Queen Tesselia|Queen Tesselia]]. He disliked the Adventurers Guild and was widely thought to be abusing his position. [[Characters/NPC/Angela Nevermore|Angela Nevermore]] plotted against him. He was the main villain of the campaign's first chapter and died in the throne room on 2024-04-04.

## Backstory / Events
- **November 30, 2023:** Through [[Characters/NPC/Maggie North|Maggie North]], the guild described him as "a bastard" who might not want to give up the regency. He hired the party to tail someone across the city. [[Sessions/2023/2023-11-30|Session]]
- **December 7, 2023:** The target was Nevermore, who was plotting a coup against him. [[Sessions/2023/2023-12-07|Session]]
- **December 21, 2023:** Met the party in a small room of a trapped dungeon with his court wizard **Lander** and his spymaster **Theodore**, wearing fancy armor. He shrugged off the cut tongues of the prisoners as necessary, scolded Theodore for attacking [[Characters/PC/Gnash|Gnash]], and asked about Nevermore before vanishing with Lander. The dying Theodore, bound by a rune of silence, confirmed that the Regent meant to **kill the queen**. Ashton left Gnash a note and a reward chest. [[Sessions/2023/2023-12-21|Session]]
- **January 4, 2024:** Nevermore produced a sketch of him praying at an altar to a winged, draconic beast. He claimed to follow **Bahamut**, but the shrine didn't match Bahamut's temple. He had been buying **crystallized amethyst**, which Southern Andore cults use with dragon fire to make dragon-creatures. The party feared he meant to transform the queen. [[Sessions/2024/2024-01-04|Session]]
- **April 4, 2024 (death):** In the throne room he stood beside the crackling machine holding Tesselia, ordering "Sofia, begin the process" and saying "she's becoming mortal". His weapon glowed with the same blue electric energy as Nevermore. In the fight he used **Apostate of Hate** (a stronger sword at the cost of shield AC) and, at half health, **A Father's Fury** (poison cleared, dual blades), and his legendary actions took down [[Characters/PC/Soren|Soren]]. Soren finished him with *Magic Missile*, but Gnash died in the fight. On his body: the **Tyrant's Blade** and its shield, a wand of paralysis, +1 plate, a **keycard**, and healing potions. [[Sessions/2024/2024-04-04|Session]]
- **Aftermath:** Nevermore became Regent Advisor. [[Sessions/2024/2024-04-11|Session]]
