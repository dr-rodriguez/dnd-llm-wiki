Lady Poomf is the bird-like leader of the Adventurers Guild in [[Locations/Locations#Helines|Helines]]. She gave out the jobs that became the [[Quests/Drakenweld Quest|Drakenweld Quest]] and the [[Quests/Tesselia Mission|Tesselia Mission]]. She has psychic power and some knowledge of [[Characters/PC/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]]'s past, and she has used illusions to show up where her body is not.

## Backstory / Events
- **January 2025:** Greeted the party at the Helines guild hall and hoped Maxim would help lead. She hired them to clear the quarantined ruins to the east (the Temple Excavation and Artistic Gallery): 600gp up front, 2,000gp on completion, and 50gp for each surviving adventurer. [[Sessions/2025/2025-01-30|Session]]
- **Briefing with the Island Dwellers:** In a meeting that went oddly quiet, she brought in two insectoid "Island Dwellers", **Nock** and **Screeck**, who carried battle plans for metal sky-ships heading west. Worried about losing contact with the West continent, she sent the party to [[Locations/Locations#Tesselia|Tesselia]] (which had given them their Fabricator Cube) to check on the guild there, report back, and **protect the guild and the young monarch** [[Characters/NPC/Queen Tesselia|Tesselia]] if the enemy resisted. [[Sessions/2025/2025-01-15|Session]]
- **February 2025:** Maxim sent her word of the party's situation. She met them on site and told them **Mayor Maxwell** had not come back from his trip north. She then took Maxim aside, hinted that she had **seen his past**, and hit him with a **psychic blast** as a warning ("Do you find destiny immutable?"). [[Characters/PC/Aolis|Aolis]] saw that she was an **illusion**, and when he touched her she said "friend" in a drow dialect, the words of a drow children's best-friend ritual. She later spoke down to [[Characters/PC/Zinjaro|Zinjaro]] when his *Scrying* and *Sending* kept failing. [[Sessions/2025/2025-02-06|Session]], [[Sessions/2025/2025-02-13|Session]], [[Sessions/2025/2025-02-27|Session]]
- **March 2025:** Aolis had overheard her saying Maxim was from another time and might be loyal to long-dead people, which fed his suspicion of Maxim. [[Sessions/2025/2025-03-06|Session]]
- **July 17, 2025:** Had sent word ahead to Drakenweld, so [[Characters/NPC/Alisha Tower|Alisha Tower]] was expecting the party. [[Sessions/2025/2025-07-17|Session]]
