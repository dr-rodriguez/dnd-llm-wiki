## Backstory
Shar is a Vulpin warlock who joined the party following the death of [[wiki/Characters/Gnash|Gnash]]. He is known for summoning a demon named **"Barbara,"** though he has occasionally lost control of the entity during combat. 

In addition to his magical abilities, Shar demonstrated a unique linguistic skill by understanding Portuguese during an encounter with a group of gunners. He also helped organize a successful spectacle with [[wiki/Characters/Nova|Nova]] and [[wiki/Characters/Juneberry|Juneberry]] to raise funds for the party.
