## Backstory
Juneberry is a Harengon thief with a notorious reputation for kleptomania. During her time with the party, she participated in several high-stakes missions, including the investigation of a locked and trapped door in **North Shire Inn** and the final confrontation with the [[Characters#Athena AI|Athena AI]]. 

Her penchant for stealing occasionally caused friction within the group, notably when she stole donuts from [[Characters/Soten|Soten]], leading to a physical altercation. She was also the victim of theft herself when her **Wand of Magic Detection** was stolen by Jerbeen and Mapach bandits. One of her more lucrative finds was a bag of gems worth 350g discovered in a stump. 

### Key Events
- **August 6, 2026:** Reappeared at the underground facility in [[Locations/Locations#Tesselia|Tesselia]] during [[Characters#Odin AI|Odin]]'s attack on the pod survivors. She rushed in, clamped the **[[Lore/Technology#Cognitive Retriever|Cognitive Retriever]]** onto [[Characters/Maximus Arkelius|Maxim]]'s head, wired it to a machine, and hit the button — pulling him back out of the **[[Lore/Technology#First Space and Second Space|Second Space]]**. She then worked on [[Characters/Amber Lyre|Amber Lyre]], and took her away for treatment after the third shock.
